Structural relaxation with run_dmft.py
=====================================

In an initialized, charge-self-consistent eDMFT case, add the following to
params.dat and run run_dmft.py in the case directory.

    relax = True

The combined force/stress response currently supports nonmagnetic eDMFT,
including supported SOC inputs. Spin-dependent DMFT branches require an
extension of the screened atomic-force backend.

The run updates case.struct and case.clmsum. The charge and impurity controls
in params.dat retain their usual meanings. For example, runIMP=False keeps the
impurity self-energy fixed. Use a charge tolerance appropriate to the desired
force and stress resolution. The following optional controls show the defaults.

    relax_pressure = 0.0          # imposed hydrostatic pressure in GPa
    relax_force_tol = 0.5         # maximum screened per-atom norm, mRy/Bohr
    relax_stress_tol = 0.02       # maximum principal stress residual, GPa
    relax_max_strain = 0.01       # maximum principal log strain of one cell step
    relax_bulk_modulus = 120.0    # initial bulk stiffness in GPa
    relax_shear_modulus = 60.0    # initial shear stiffness in GPa
    relax_refresh_bohr = 0.08     # maximum internal motion before a new measurement
    relax_max_steps = 20          # maximum screened evaluations per invocation

Chemical potential and pressure
-------------------------------

For a Mott insulator, recomputeEF=0 keeps the supplied EF.dat during charge
convergence and preparation of the screened stress. The analytic pressure
and stress conserve the particle number of that central solution to first
order in strain. The chemical-potential derivative is solved internally.
It does not update EF.dat or correct a pre-existing error in the integrated
charge. The normalization choice in case.inm is independent.

For a fixed-chemical-potential derivative, use the following in case.stress_response.json.

    {"solver": {"stress_ensemble": "fixed-mu"}}

The corresponding explicit fixed-number setting is "fixed-n". A legacy
solver.physical_policy.fixed_number override still applies to both stages
unless stress_ensemble specifies the derivative. The response log records
the central chemical-potential choice and the derivative constraint.

Following a run
---------------

    tail -f case.relax/history
    tail -f case.outputdmf2_response

Replace "case" with the WIEN case name. The first log spans the entire
relaxation. A typical inner iteration looks like this.

    [2026-11-22T16:40:00] 0018.020 MSR1a (int.-relax) FOR+scrn   0.03860 mRy/Bohr, rho 1.28e-5   (<=2e-5,yes)
    [2026-11-22T16:40:03] 0018.021 MSR1  (posi.-held) FOR+scrn   0.01240 mRy/Bohr, rho 1.48e-5   (<=2e-5,yes)

MSR1a (int.-relax) allows internal motion. MSR1 (posi.-held) holds the atoms
fixed. The force is the largest per-atom norm used by the mixer. FOR+scrn
includes the most recent screened-minus-ordinary correction. The rho field
gives the mixer charge distance (:DIS) in scientific notation with three
significant digits, its target (cc), and whether the unrounded value meets
that target. Energy convergence (ec) is still checked.
A small force while :DIS is large calls for more electronic iterations.
During STRFT, the second log shows preparation and
the individual GMRES residuals. It is rewritten for each dmft2 call.
During FOR, case.scfm can list a very large pressure marked partial.
The ordinary pass lacks the full valence stress. Only the completed STRFT
tensor in case.relax/history is used to update the lattice. The log reports
tensile-positive stress. The next line, labeled "pressure (GPa)", gives minus
one third of its trace. Positive pressure means compression.

case.broyd* records the native mixer history. Successive MSR1a atomic moves
share it. The history is cleared on a change of cell, on a change of mixer
mode, and when starting a block with a new screened force correction. The log
gives the reason and the number of files removed. The input charge is retained.

At each screened measurement the history gives the representative fractional
positions, Cartesian forces, screened-minus-ordinary corrections, the tensile
stress tensor, and the decision on the cell. case.relax/status.json records
the current step and convergence status. Numbered subdirectories hold the
initial and measured structures and measurement.json. Follow the report
path in that file for the complete response calculation. When the cell
moves, next.struct and next.clmsum record the structure and charge seed.

How one geometry block works
---------------------------

The internal-coordinate update is native WIEN2k MSR1a. Its standard
run_lapw -min workflow also finishes with fixed-position charge iterations
after checking the mixer force flag, charge and energy. Here the inner
stopping rule uses the force norm explicitly because the next, screened
measurement has its own force tolerance. The screened force correction
and the variable-cell optimizer are eDMFT extensions.

1. At fixed cell the driver requests FOR. WIEN's MSR1a mixer updates the
   charge and symmetry-allowed internal coordinates. The driver archives an
   incoming SOlapm.dat in the numbered directory. FOR and STRFT both
   normalize the projector on their current LAPW states. A successful
   measurement supplies a fresh SOlapm.dat when all branches share it.
   When consecutive ordinary force estimates are small and the latest charge
   distance meets cc, the driver switches to MSR1 so charge can settle at
   fixed positions. It resumes internal moves if the equilibrated force
   estimate requires them.
2. After electronic convergence the driver measures ordinary FOR and screened
   STRFT with the same final LAPW states and geometry. A single adjoint
   supplies all symmetry-allowed stress and force components. The geometry
   stays fixed during publication and the final charge-mixing step.
3. The next block adds the most recent screened-minus-ordinary force to
   newly computed ordinary forces. This is a local approximation. A large
   change in internal positions triggers an early remeasurement. The previous
   adjoint can also initialize GMRES on a compatible density basis. The new
   solve must satisfy the ordinary residual check.
4. Once charge and screened forces meet their targets, residual stress can
   drive a cell move. The response uses tensile-positive sigma, so positive
   external pressure P requires sigma + P I = 0. The driver keeps only the
   symmetry-allowed tensor, makes a bounded elastic step, and uses measured
   stress changes to update an inverse-BFGS compliance when the curvature
   is reliable. Fractional positions are fixed during the cell move. The
   driver keeps the muffin-tin radii, shortens a trial step if spheres
   overlap and adjusts the interstitial constant of the charge seed to
   conserve its native electron count.
5. Success requires a fresh screened measurement at the final cell and
   positions with charge, force and stress all within tolerance.

The interface temporarily manages case.in0, active case.in2 inputs and
case.inm and restores their original first lines when the run exits. Leave
these managed fields to the driver during a run. Keep params.dat unchanged
during a screened measurement because it belongs to the response snapshot.
The body of each electronic block uses the ordinary finish, cc, ec and
iteration limits. An exhausted limit or failed response is reported. Restart
run_dmft.py in the same directory to continue from the saved geometry and
charge. A cell-step journal supports recovery after an interrupted update.
Keep each case.relax directory with its own calculation.
