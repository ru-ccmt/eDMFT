RADIAL-BASIS STRESS TESTS — 8 NOVEMBER 2026

All stresses here are mean tensile stress Tr(sigma)/3 in GPa. Pressure has
the opposite sign. There is no further division by three.

RESULT
The DFT radial-basis contribution is significant, especially when the
linearization-energy reference follows the actual DFT input convention.
The eDMFT radial contribution is smaller and does not explain its remaining
stress mismatch. The preexisting default-off radial H/O diagnostic fails
independent closure tests and is not ready for production use.

No production source, executable, default switch, or original material
calculation was changed. All computations used new /private/tmp copies.
The 14 files in the prior response-installation manifest are unchanged.

1. MATHEMATICAL TEST
At the central cell, hold the physical KS Hamiltonian H0 fixed and vary only
the radial trial functions, their matching coefficients, and local orbitals.
Use the measured spherical potential response at fixed physical radius.
The muffin-tin radii, fractional atom positions, reciprocal-vector labels,
band counts, and k-point weights are fixed during this artificial test.

Let chi_a(t) be this radial-dependent basis. The basis-only matrices are
  dH_ab = <d chi_a|H0|chi_b> + <chi_a|H0|d chi_b>
  dO_ab = <d chi_a|chi_b> + <chi_a|d chi_b>.
The desired stationary contraction is
  D_B = Re Tr[rho dH - rho_E dO] + D_projector,B.
The normalized projector must include both its raw-overlap derivative and
the derivative of X=(sum_k w_k P_k^dagger P_k)^(-1/2), with the selected
KS window and KS metric treated consistently.

An independent DFT reference is sum_kn f_kn d epsilon_kn with CENTRAL
tetrahedron occupations f_kn. This avoids numerical changes in occupation
weights while testing exactly the first derivative.

For frozen Sigma, linearizing Phi_imp-Phi_DC cancels the first-order change
of Tr[(Sigma-Vdc)Gloc]. At fixed physical H0 the remaining lattice derivative
is that of L_B(t) = -T sum_kn w_k log det[-G_k(t)^(-1)] + mu(t) N,
evaluated with the same regularization as dmft2. The saved :VSUM is this
quantity. In the orthonormal endpoint KS basis,
  G_k^(-1)=(i omega+mu) I-diag(epsilon_k)-U_k(Sigma-Vdc)U_k^dagger.
Only the chosen window is correlated. Each endpoint recomputes normalized
U and adjusts mu to keep N fixed; Sigma is unchanged. The basis-only test
therefore retains cancellations among H, O, window motion, and projector
normalization without trying to subtract their large pieces numerically.

These are diagnostic directional derivatives, not a newly installed fully
analytic stress implementation. Adding them to a saved baseline is shown
below as an estimate of their importance; the actual production correction
still needs a validated analytic contraction.

2. INPUTS AND CONVENTIONS
SrVO3 is NREL, RKMAX=8, 75 k points, 43 stored bands, W=12..31.
FeSe is RELA, RKMAX=8, 140 k points, 66 stored bands, W=21..44.
For eDMFT the physical radial-potential endpoints are the saved screened
response potentials from frozen_sigma_response_20261108, h=0.00025.
Half-step tests interpolate the perturbation by 1/2, h=0.000125.
For DFT the potential direction comes from the converged +/-0.001 triplets;
both h=0.001 and the half-sized perturbation were tested.

The DFT IN1 EF reference changes with strain; the eDMFT IN1 references are
identical across each triplet. The first DFT control kept that reference
fixed, to isolate the potential-induced response. A second control also
varied it according to the actual endpoint inputs, while retaining central
geometry, RKMAX, G vectors, and band counts. All other meaningful IN1
differences were checked: endpoint RKMAX changes compensate geometric
strain and must not be copied into this fixed-cell radial test.

4. VALIDATION AND FAILED CONTROLS
PASS: all sampled cases retain identical k/G sets and stored band counts.
PASS: fixed-index DMFT windows remain isolated at their boundaries.
PASS: zero basis perturbation reproduces DFT eigenvalues exactly and eDMFT
      :VSUM to its printed precision (zero discrepancy in both materials).
PASS: halving the perturbation changes the relevant radial stresses by
      at most about 3e-5 GPa.
PASS: an independent DFT band-sum check agrees with the fixed-occupation
      derivative to about 2e-5 GPa after subtracting mu0*(Nplus-Nminus).
      This is relevant in FeSe: tetrahedron integration leaves N errors
      of order 1e-7, enough to contaminate a small uncorrected derivative.

FAIL: the old optional radial H/O contraction does not reproduce the DFT
      eigenvalue test. This persists at 100x smaller radial probe step:
          SrVO3: old +0.107648 GPa vs eigenvalue about +0.00818 GPa;
          FeSe:  old -0.007953 GPa vs eigenvalue about -0.07386 GPa.
      This comparison holds the IN1 reference fixed on both sides.
      Consequently its eDMFT result must not be promoted to production.

  SrVO3 old radial H/O plus explicit normalized-projector branch:
      +0.470271557 GPa; independent lattice result -0.052377959 GPa.
  FeSe old radial H/O plus explicit normalized-projector branch:
      +0.042717027 GPa; independent lattice result -0.069117403 GPa.

For this comparison the printed explicit-projector diagnostic uses the
raw WIEN stress sign. Its change must be NEGATED before adding it to the
physical H-O radial derivative. No sign was selected by fitting results.

Complete-eigensystem reconstruction was also tested:
  O=C^(-dagger) C^(-1), H=C^(-dagger) diag(epsilon) C^(-1).
At h=0.001 its separate H/O finite difference is badly affected by
cancellation and nonlinear changes of the coefficient representation.
At h=1e-5 it approaches the eigenvalue control (differences about 0.00065
and 0.00131 GPa for SrVO3 and FeSe). This is not the precision reference.
The robust reference is the combined eigenvalue/free-energy derivative.

5. WHAT THESE TESTS ESTABLISH
The full radial dependence, including the chosen linearization energies,
can account for much of the remaining DFT mismatch. In FeSe the estimated
residual becomes very small; SrVO3 still has a roughly 0.06 GPa residual.
For the fixed-reference eDMFT inputs, the measured radial term moves the
stress away from the saved numerical target. It does not solve the eDMFT
mismatch, and the larger DFT SrVO3 correction must not be transferred to
eDMFT because its IN1 reference follows a different path.

The old radial diagnostic has a reproducible implementation/formulation
inconsistency already in DFT. Its exact source is not identified here.
A candidate must not be inferred from the total-stress mismatch alone.
No new production-force or charge-self-consistency bug is established.

6. NEXT IMPLEMENTATION WORK
First differentiate the actual assembled LAPW radial matrices and matching
coefficients together, including local orbitals, finite-energy construction
of udot, and RELA conventions. Require agreement with the DFT reference
above before introducing the eDMFT moments or projector normalization.
Then require the full rho*dH-rho_E*dO plus normalized-projector derivative
to reproduce the stable lattice-free-energy reference in both materials.
Treat the IN1 linearization-energy convention as an explicit input to this
derivative, not as an accidental property of a particular test directory.
Only after these controls pass should an opt-in production correction be
added. Source changes require asking the user first under their instruction.
