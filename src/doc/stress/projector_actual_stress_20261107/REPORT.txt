ACTUAL STRAIN POTENTIAL-OPERATOR TEST: SrVO3
7 November 2026

Outcome
=======
The complete material-coordinate KS potential derivative, contracted with
both existing projector-window response matrices, gives -2.98675885 GPa.
The present analytic hydrostatic mean stress is +5.67688415 GPa; adding
this diagnostic contribution gives +2.69012530 GPa. The half-step relaxed
frozen-effective-self-energy numerical value is +2.93383641 GPa. Thus the
analytic-minus-numerical discrepancy changes from +2.74304774 to
-0.24371111 GPa. This removes about 91% of its magnitude, but overshoots
and does NOT establish complete stress accuracy.

All values are mean tensile-sign stress, +(1/(3 Omega)) dGamma/deta,
for F=(1+eta) I. Compressive pressure is the negative. These numbers are
already divided by three. Omega=382.70904 bohr^3; conversion factor
14710.507641078 GPa per Ry/bohr^3. The correction is -0.233110842923 Ry
per unit isotropic length strain.

No production source/executable was changed, and no original material
calculation directory was modified. This is a postprocessed diagnostic,
not an installed correction or a fresh complete SCF benchmark. Only SrVO3
was tested here; FeSe, MnO, MnTe, shear, and band-complement convergence
remain untested for this contribution.

Important revision of the preceding interpretation
==================================================
The missing contribution is NOT identified merely as induced Hartree-XC
response. The large term is the explicit coordinate derivative of the
potential inside a muffin-tin. The fixed-geometry interstitial-potential
probe established a nonzero functional projector response; it did not
measure a strain correction or prove density response dominated it.

A first subtraction of saved potential files gave +0.47188337 GPa. That
is NOT the complete potential-operator derivative: the MT functions are
stored at fixed physical radius, and the VNS Fourier coefficients contain
the moving interstitial step function. In the correct material convention:

  MT: Vop_eta(r) = (partial_eta V_eta^a(r))_fixed_r + r partial_r V_0^a(r)

  I:  Vop_eta(s) = partial_eta V_eta(F_eta s) at fixed central coordinate s.

The local atom center follows the lattice, and the MT radius is fixed in
bohr. The derivative of the interstitial characteristic function belongs
to the domain/basis bookkeeping, not to the potential operator itself.

Measured decomposition, including normalization in every row:
  MT potential change at fixed physical radius          +0.618040584 GPa
  MT coordinate derivative r partial_r V_KS              -3.516178766 GPa
  Interstitial material potential derivative             -0.088620668 GPa
  TOTAL                                                   -2.986758850 GPa

Equivalently, the naive saved-file result must be corrected by adding the
MT coordinate term and removing the interstitial boundary contribution.
The latter is approximately -0.057537 GPa in the raw file derivative.
The actual half-step replacement of raw by unwarped interstitial potential
is +0.057536549 GPa. A central analytic-step-function derivative gives
+0.057536892 GPa; their tiny difference includes endpoint discretization
and the last density update used by the R2V reconstruction.

The total decomposes by projector branch as
  fixed-normalization window response                     -2.821768383 GPa
  normalization-window response                            -0.164990467 GPa.
Omitting the normalization derivative would therefore be material here.

Klein functional and why this is a projector term
================================================
Use an auxiliary-field Klein functional schematically as

 Gamma = L[H_KS,O,U,Sigma_tilde] - integral (V_KS-V_ext)n + E_Hxc[n]
         + E_ion + Phi_imp[g]-Phi_DC[nloc] - Tr(Sigma_tilde g) + mu N.

L is the consistently regularized lattice trace log. Sigma_tilde includes
the double-counting subtraction. At the impurity stationary point,
dPhi = Tr(Sigma_tilde dg), and the dg and dSigma_tilde variations cancel.
Along the frozen-Sigma validation path we use the agreed linear impurity
functional tangent, rather than claiming the unknown full impurity Phi is
known at both endpoints.

At fixed central representation define the gradient C by

 delta_U Gamma = 2 Re sum_k Tr[C_k^dagger delta U_k].

One explicit convention is C_k = w_k T sum_omega G_k U_k Sigma_tilde,
with the full positive/negative frequency pairing and the same regulated
high-frequency completion as L. Spin weights are included consistently.
The code dumps already include k/spin/frequency weights; do not multiply
them by another k-point or spin factor.

Let phi be the fixed physical disk orbital column, Pi_W the projector onto
fixed band indices W, and X=S^(-1/2). In a fixed Hilbert-space representation

 U_k = Pi_W,k phi_k X,
 S = Sym sum_k w_k phi_k^dagger Pi_W,k phi_k.

Sym is the actual forward orbital/equivalent-atom averaging. This physical
projector is not independent of V, despite phi being fixed. With geometry
held fixed for a potential perturbation:

 delta U = delta Pi_W phi X + Pi_W phi delta X,
 delta S = Sym sum_k w_k phi^dagger delta Pi_W phi,
 X^(-1) delta X + delta X X^(-1) = -X delta S X.

The last equation is the exact inverse-square-root Frechet derivative,
valid without assuming S and delta S commute. In an eigenbasis of S with
positive eigenvalues s_i:

 (delta X)_ij = -(delta S)_ij /
                 [sqrt(s_i) sqrt(s_j) (sqrt(s_i)+sqrt(s_j))].

The symmetry adjoint must be applied in the correct reverse order when
forming a contraction with delta S; the current installed exact adjoint
was used in these tests.

For n inside W and a outside W,

 delta Pi_W = sum_(n,a) [ |a><a|delta V|n><n|/(epsilon_n-epsilon_a)
                         + Hermitian conjugate ].

No energy-window edge derivative is introduced: the band indices are
fixed. Smoothness still requires an uncut gap between W and its complement.
Here all 75 k points have 43 stored bands, W=[12,31], and the minimum
inside/outside separation is 0.05641984 Ry.

Substitution gives

 delta_U Gamma = Re sum_k Tr[(R_k+R_k^N) delta V_k].

R is the fixed-X window response and R^N the normalization-window response.
They are already constructed in dmft2. To make the fixed-X formula explicit,
let q_a=<psi_a|phi>, F_a=q_a X denote the untruncated normalized raw overlap,
and C_n the gradient defined above. Then, in the indicated trace convention,

 R_an = sum_l F_al C_nl^* / (epsilon_n-epsilon_a),
 R_na = R_an^*,

with zero other blocks. R^N is the same chain rule through S and X. F_a is
not the physically truncated embedding U_a, which is zero outside W.
Confusing those two would incorrectly eliminate the response.

For the actual strain calculation,

 Delta D_eta = Re sum_k Tr[(R_k+R_k^N) Vop_eta,k],
 Delta mean_stress = Delta D_eta/(3 Omega).

This is a contraction with a potential DERIVATIVE. It is not a derivative
of R and not a second derivative of the Klein functional. All R matrices
are evaluated at the central state. No impurity vertex is needed for this
first-derivative term in the stationary/tangent formulation.

Where the current implementation omits it
========================================
The material-coordinate matrix decomposition previously derived is

 H_eta = T_eta + B_eta + Vop_eta,
 O_eta = O_eta,basis,

where T is the kinetic operator derivative and B the basis/Pulay variation.
Blaha's G1/G2 generalizations implement the T/B/metric and corresponding
surface pieces. The usual DFT cancellation of the potential variation uses
the physical charge n_G, not the extra window-response density R+R^N.

src/dmft2/l2main.f90 builds the response matrices near line 1033 and passes
them to cmp_dmft_stress near line 1382. The normalization/fused pass is in
l2mainSubs.f90 near lines 3761-3841. cmp_dmft_stress contracts the kinetic,
G2 basis, APW/surface and relativistic kernels. The ordinary charge calls
cmp_MT_density (l2main near 1218) and cmp_interstitial (near 1441) receive
the physical density, not these response matrices. There is no corresponding
R Vop contraction or R-dependent lapw0 electrostatic/XC density assembly.

The V_KS already present INSIDE G2's matrix elements (basis_derivative|
H_KS-epsilon|psi) does not supply the separate operator derivative Vop.
This distinction is the source-level reason that adding the measured term
is consistent with the previously derived decomposition, rather than an
empirical adjustment based on numerical agreement.

Numerical method and checks
===========================
1. Saved full +/-0.001 and converged half +/-0.00049997741149 SrVO3
   endpoints were used. No new charge or impurity convergence was performed.
2. WIEN_LAPW1_BASIS_VSP holds the radial basis at the central potential while
   the physical Hamiltonian changes. All test geometries are the central
   geometry for matrix-element extraction. Radial-energy headers were
   identical across the physical-potential probes.
3. A complete central WIEN LAPW eigensystem supplies the dual coefficient
   basis, avoiding an incorrect identification of WIEN and dftKS auxiliary
   LO coordinates. Only the original 43 bands enter the response sum. The
   full auxiliary eigensystem here does NOT mean complement convergence was
   improved. Endpoint Hamiltonians projected into the central 43-state space
   have truncation errors at finite perturbation; their centered derivative
   is checked by halving the step.
4. The dftKS/WIEN band gauge is aligned only inside degenerate blocks (and
   by a phase for nondegenerate states). Maximum central eigenvalue mismatch
   is 6.5e-7 Ry. An unconstrained all-band least-squares gauge was rejected.
   An initial direct use of the dftKS overlap metric on WIEN LO coordinates
   failed normalization and was rejected; its preliminary numbers are NOT
   used in the final result.
5. R2V output supplies unwarped potential star coefficients. Their star-sum
   convention differs from VNS's star-average convention: divide VTOTAL
   coefficients by each star's size before expanding. Independently
   reconstructing central VNS via the exact Fourier interstitial step on
   the 60^3 FFT mesh agrees within 4.9e-14 Ry coefficient-wise, including
   the required complex conjugation for case.vector coefficients.
6. Central MT potentials supply r dV/dr using cubic interpolation on the
   logarithmic radial mesh. For VSP storing s=rV the stored derivative is
   d s/d(log r)-s; for VNS radial harmonics it is d V_L/d(log r).
   Fixed-basis Hamiltonian probes at +/-3e-5 and +/-1.5e-5 yield MT advection
   -3.516178693 and -3.516178766 GPa, respectively.
7. Total correction using full-step potentials: -2.986775592 GPa;
   half-step: -2.986758850 GPa. Difference 0.000016742 GPa.
8. Independent nonlinear lattice trace-log endpoints are evaluated with
   fixed physical embedding versus updated Pi_W and X, along the extracted
   material potential direction. Differences in their slopes are
   -2.986735999 GPa (h=1e-5) and -2.986735057 GPa (h=5e-6), versus direct
   R/R^N contraction -2.986758850 GPa. Residual <0.000024 GPa, retained rather
   than described as roundoff; the earlier trace-log tail diagnostic showed
   small differences in production functional/gradient tail completion.
9. A separate installed dmft2 run on central WIEN eigenvectors reconstructs
   R and R^N directly. It gives -2.986759413 GPa, differing by 0.000000563 GPa
   from the primary result. Central subspace metric error 2.3e-13 and maximum
   response-matrix difference 3.7e-9. Thus the large correction is not caused
   by a dftKS/WIEN LO convention mismatch.
10. All auxiliary calculations were isolated under /private/tmp. Prior
    production source changes visible in git status were left untouched.

Cost and practical implementation
=================================
The dominant coordinate term is inexpensive: it is a local operator whose
radial and angular integrals can be cached alongside existing V_ns matrices
and contracted with the already available R/R^N. This is differentiation
of the potential's coordinate argument, NOT the omitted change of the
radial LAPW solution. It does not require an impurity solve or another SCF
loop. A general strain tensor needs the angular terms in r_beta partial_alpha
V, not just the isotropic r partial_r V tested here.

A complete analytic Vop additionally needs the strain response of the
self-consistent KS potential. For the current frozen-Sigma protocol it can
be obtained by one-particle charge linear response including the finite
window and normalization, plus Coulomb/XC response and the chosen number
constraint. Schematically:

 delta n = b_eta + chi_fr delta V_Hxc,
 delta V_Hxc = f_Hxc delta n,
 (1-chi_fr f_Hxc) delta n = b_eta.

The geometric potential source and material-coordinate/Jacobian terms must
be included consistently in b_eta/the potential decomposition. This is not
provided by the simple affine-density guess alone. An analytic response
solver is a separate implementation and its timing has not been measured.
A fully self-consistent DMFT response problem is different from this frozen
Sigma tangent test; do not infer that all higher responses are vertex-free.

For scale: central/perturbed WIEN lapw1 runs here took about 15 seconds each;
R2V lapw0 evaluations took about 0.8 seconds; one additional MPI4 dmft2
control took 20.9 seconds. Those are diagnostic timings, not a production
stress overhead forecast. The earlier SrVO3 charge-relaxed half-step runs
cost about 3.3 minutes each and already existed for this test.

Recommended next action: implement the explicit potential-operator
contraction as a DEFAULT-OFF diagnostic (after asking for source-change
permission), first the central geometric MT and interstitial bookkeeping,
then compare the complete term from saved potentials in FeSe as well as
SrVO3. The remaining ~0.244 GPa must not be dismissed as radial/basis error
without further tests. No empirical scaling or fitted subtraction is proposed.
