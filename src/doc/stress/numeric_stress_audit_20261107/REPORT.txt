Numerical stress audit — 7 November 2026
Focus: whether the SrVO3 RKMAX7 -> RKMAX8 change is a numerical-differentiation error.

Conclusion: the RKMAX8 SrVO3 frozen-Sigma numerical mean stress is reproducible at
approximately +2.934 GPa. No bookkeeping, stale output, normalization freshness,
Matsubara quadrature, strain-unit or tested finite-step error explains the +2.615 GPa
analytic-minus-numerical discrepancy. This is not proof of the entire analytic
functional derivative, and does not establish basis convergence of the analytic result.

Units: all stress numbers below are mean normal stress trace(sigma)/3 in GPa.
Positive sign follows the previous +dK/dstrain convention; compressive pressure is opposite.

1. Functional and independent reconstruction
For these ordinary LDA/GGA mixer runs (no additional hybrid/vdW corrections):
 ENE = E_density + sum_core occupation*epsilon_core + SUM + EORB.
 VSUM = F_lattice_trace_log + mu*N_target.
 K_frozen = ENE - SUM + VSUM - EORB
          = E_density + sum_core occupation*epsilon_core + VSUM.
The frozen tangent impurity term cancels the explicit -Tr[(Sigma-Vdc)Gloc] term;
the remaining impurity reference constant cancels in the strain derivative.
The isolated traces and core/density terms are not stationary by themselves.
No additional mu*N or mu*dN correction should be appended to this K by hand.
Source: dmft2/l2main.f90 lines2106,2122,2126; WIEN SRC_mixer/scfana.f
lines248-264 and613-629. VSUM uses target elecn, not the measured xwt.
For recomputeEF=0 the SUM contains mu*(N_target-N_measured); this is also in ENE
and cancels in ENE-SUM. This cancellation alone does not cure nonneutral or
nonstationary fixed-mu reference calculations such as the present MnO triplet.

The independent SrVO3 reconstruction reads every core level, weights it with
the occupations from case.inc and atomic multiplicities, and adds the same-cycle
lapw0 density energy and VSUM. It accounts for 44 core electrons. Its difference
from the mixer-based K is <=5e-9 Ry, at the scale of output rounding.
 Independent mean stress: 2.933140092 GPa.

6. Remaining limits and next work
The SrVO3 numerical estimate is stable under the explicit tests performed.
The agreement of the frozen-tangent functional derivative with the analytic code
still needs to be established. In particular, this audit is not an independent
proof of the treatment of density-dependent normalized band-window projectors.
Do not infer analytic convergence solely from its modest RKMAX7->8 change;
the previously documented stored-complement-band test shifted analytic stress
by about0.52 GPa at fixed RKMAX8. No new analytic source change was made here.
A useful next comparison is the eDMFT-minus-DFT stress increment, using matched
geometry, basis, integration, projector conventions and stationary functional.
