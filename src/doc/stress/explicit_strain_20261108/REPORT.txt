1. THE SOURCE AND THE DENSITY RESPONSE ARE DIFFERENT OBJECTS

Let eta be an infinitesimal lattice-scale change, A_eta=(1+eta)A_0. Fractional
ionic positions, RMT, radial grids, local axes, selected reciprocal labels and
the prepared electronic energy policy are fixed. The present source is for
primitive orthogonal, nonmagnetic LDA/PBE cells under hydrostatic strain.

Let T_eta push a density in central material coordinates into the deformed
cell, using the SAME transport as the native screening reference. Write

  n_eta = T_eta [ n_0 + eta q + O(eta^2) ],
  tau_n = (d T_eta/deta)_0 n_0,
  P_eta[n] = native potential-construction map in native FILE coordinates,
  K = (partial P_0/partial n)_n0.

The potential response is then

  v' = s_V + K q,
  s_V = (partial_eta P_eta[n_0])_0 + K tau_n.                 (1)

The partial derivative in (1) holds the native density COEFFICIENTS fixed.
It includes ionic and geometric Coulomb, XC and interstitial-mask changes.
q is the density tangent found from (I-J)q=s_charge. It does not include tau_n;
adding K tau_n again to Kq would double count the density transport.

The CHARGE source s_charge also needs explicit H/O/raw-projector geometry,
moving-basis density reconstruction, core/coordinate transport and output
normalization. Those ingredients are not replaced by s_V alone. Existing
screening tests used a native numerical hydrostatic charge source and remain
the independent reference until all analytic charge-source pieces are assembled.

2. EXACT COORDINATES AND RADIAL CONVENTIONS

Input density coefficients are c_00=4*pi*r^2*n_spherical, c_lm=r^2*n_lm for
the other harmonics, and Fourier density STAR SUMS. For lambda=1+eta,

  (T_eta c)_lm(r) = c_lm(r/lambda)/lambda,
  (tau_n c)_lm(r) = -c_lm(r) - d c_lm/dlog(r),
  (tau_n rho)_G = -3 rho_G.                                (2)

The derivative follows the same cubic interpolation as Density.transport,
including its small fixed-RMT edge extrapolation. No value is set to zero at
the sphere boundary. It is an explicit reference coordinate convention, not
an assertion that the physical self-consistent density merely dilates.

Potential coordinates have DIFFERENT radial units:
  spherical VSP: F_00(r)=r*V_spherical(r),
  nonspherical VNS: V_lm(r),
  interstitial: masked per-member Fourier coefficients in Ry.

The prepared field uses INTERNAL REAN4 Fourier coefficients. A complex VNS
file contains their conjugates. NativePotentialFileLayout uses the existing
selector/transposed selector, so conjugation occurs exactly once. It matches
atom/harmonic/G LABELS, not positions in a file. Missing requested modes,
nonzero additional active cutoff modes, duplicate/truncated data and an
observable with support in unexported channels are rejected.

The response operator B maps changes in these potentials at FIXED radial r
into H/O/raw-projector derivatives. One must not insert r.grad(V) without
also transforming the band-geometry derivative to that convention.

For comparison, converting a fixed-r derivative to material radial coordinates
would add

  spherical:    dF_00/dlog(r) - F_00,
  nonspherical: dV_lm/dlog(r).                              (3)

These are derived from F_eta(lambda*r)/lambda and V_lm,eta(lambda*r).
They are supplied as a separate diagnostic, NOT added to (1). For a point
nucleus F=-2Z, the fixed-r derivative is zero, while (3) gives +2Z. This simple
control detects an incorrect density-like scaling of potential coordinates.

3. COULOMB AND IONIC GEOMETRY DERIVATIVE

This differentiates the native finite pseudocharge/radial construction, not
a continuum scaling estimate or two separately converged strained calculations.
All symbols in the following are the prepared Hartree operator's factors:

  m: radial electron multipole moments in complex-harmonic normalization;
  S: conversion from native signed real harmonics to complex multipoles;
  rho: density Fourier star sums;
  D: multipoles of the Fourier continuation inside the fixed MT spheres;
  Q_ion = S z, z_(a,00)=Z_a/sqrt(4*pi), other z entries zero;
  Q = -S m + D rho + Q_ion;
  L_G = 8*pi/|G|^2 for G!=0, L_0=0;
  A: pseudocharge multipole-to-potential map;
  p = L rho + A Q: unmasked Coulomb potential star sums;
  B: star-to-radial-boundary map; b=Re(B p);
  M: native Fourier truncation/reanalysis/interstitial-mask map.

For hydrostatic strain at fixed native density coefficients,
  G'=-G, Omega'=3 Omega; radial m, S, Q_ion and RMT do not change.
  L'=2L, Q'=D' rho,
  p'=2L rho + A' Q + A D' rho,
  b'=Re(B' p + B p').                                     (4)

The maps contain spherical Bessel functions j_l(x), x=|G|R. Fractional Bloch
phases and Y_lm(local G) are fixed for hydrostatic strain. With geometry-
independent factors suppressed,

  D ~ j_(l+1)(x)/|G|,
  D' ~ [j_(l+1)(x)-x j'_(l+1)(x)]/|G|,
  B ~ j_l(x),             B' ~ -x j'_l(x),
  A ~ [L/Omega] j_(n+1)(x)/x^p, p=n-l+1,
  A' ~ [L/Omega] { (p-1)j_(n+1)(x)/x^p
                                  - j'_(n+1)(x)/x^(p-1) }. (5)

n is the native pseudocharge order (9 in these fixtures). The implementation
never divides by j_l, so Bessel-function zeros do not create singular ratios.
The existing G=0 Coulomb gauge is retained, with its separate regular limits.

At fixed radial grids the Coulomb MT Dirichlet operation has no geometric
change in its interior kernel. Its explicit response is its boundary factor
times b'. The point-nucleus interior term is -2Z(1-r/R) in rV units and is
constant under this fixed-RMT geometry variation. Its PERIODIC/boundary
response is already in Q_ion through A', B' and the changing cell.

The interstitial Coulomb response is
  v_C,IR' = M p' + M' p.                                  (6)

The source returned by hydrostatic_coulomb_geometry includes both terms and
the full ionic contribution. The central Coulomb potential is reconstructed
separately and compared with native total potential after adding central XC;
agreement is 7.02e-14 relative (SrVO3) and 8.21e-14 (FeSe). This is a useful
check of nuclear sign, harmonic normalization and the native Coulomb gauge.

4. THE FIXED-RMT INTERSTITIAL SURFACE TERM

For a general symmetric direction e, A_eta=(I+eta e)A, the mask on the FULL
native FFT reciprocal box is

  M_G = delta_G0 - sum_a [4*pi*R_a^3/Omega] f(x_a) exp(iG.R_a),
  f(x)=j_1(x)/x, x_a=|G|R_a.

Fractional sites stay fixed, so the phases do not vary. Define
  d_G = G^T e G/|G|^2, with d_0=0.
Then
  M_G' = sum_a [4*pi*R_a^3/Omega] exp(iG.R_a)
                      [tr(e) f(x_a) + d_G x_a f'(x_a)].    (7)

For hydrostatic e=I, 3f+x f'=j_0(x). The G=0 limit is
  M_0' = sum_a 4*pi*R_a^3/Omega.

Thus the surface term survives for a CONSTANT potential. Its inverse FFT,
multiplication with the central potential on the native grid, and native
star collection give M'p in (6). This preserves finite FFT aliasing and the
native real-linear convention. Applying only the density scaling would omit
this source. The general tensor mask component is tested, but that does not
mean the complete six-component potential/charge source is implemented.

5. XC GEOMETRY AND ASSEMBLY

MT radial/angular grids are fixed, so their explicit XC geometry derivative
at fixed native density coefficients is zero. Their density-transport response
is in K tau_n. In the interstitial quadratic fit, at fixed grid density values,

  n'=0, (partial_i n)'=-partial_i n,
        (partial_i partial_j n)'=-2 partial_i partial_j n. (8)

The existing analytic LDA/PBE formulas evaluate the directional derivative
of V_xc with these field changes using complex-step differentiation on the
central branch. Native positivity/low-density averaging, representative-star
selection, Fourier truncation and masking follow in their original order.

  v_xc,IR,geometry' = M (V_xc,unmasked,geometry') + M' V_xc,unmasked.

There is no differential XC geometry term in LDA before masking; M' still
contributes. PBE adds the spatial-derivative changes in (8).

Finally,
  s_V = K tau_n + v_C,geometry' + v_xc,geometry'.           (9)

hydrostatic_explicit_potential returns (9) and separately named components.
The user of the function adds Kq exactly once. Fixed Sigma, double counting,
radial energy and projector policies remain those of the prepared state.
