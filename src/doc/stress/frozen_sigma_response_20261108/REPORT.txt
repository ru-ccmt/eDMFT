FROZEN-SELF-ENERGY LINEAR RESPONSE -- 2026-11-08
================================================

Implemented and validated an opt-in central-cell response solver. No
impurity solves and no two strained SCF calculations are needed. The source
uses two single, unrelaxed affine-strain charge evaluations. Screening uses
central-cell directional derivatives and Anderson acceleration.

Equations (q is total density in material coordinates):
  q_eta(x) = det(F_eta) n_eta(F_eta x)
  v_eta(x) = V_KS,eta(F_eta x)
  v1 = v_aff + K q1
  q1 = s + A q1,  s = b + chi_frozen v_aff,  A = chi_frozen K
  A x = [F_charge(n0+t*x)-F_charge(n0)]/t

The charge map F_charge runs LAPW0, LAPW1, dmft2, lcore once, refreshes
projector normalization, adjusts mu to fixed N, and applies the ordinary
interstitial charge normalization. Sigma and double counting supplied to
dmft2 stay fixed. This is not the full DMFT susceptibility (no impurity
vertex). The physical response includes radial augmentation and all LAPW
complement states implicitly through the full eigenvector solve.

Pressure is the negative of these mean tensile stresses. The displayed
numbers already contain the hydrostatic division by three. Numerical totals
are the saved frozen-Sigma Klein references from the preceding audit; no
new impurity solve or full total-energy convergence was performed here.

Both predicted corrections match the independent relaxed-potential
references within 0.000040 GPa. Halving source and density-probe steps
changes the correction by 0.0000049 GPa (SrVO3) and 0.0000155 GPa (FeSe).
The remaining 0.244 and 0.070 GPa discrepancies are TOTAL-stress residuals;
they are not solved by claiming that this new response is exact. Further
checks include the complement convergence of the stress adjoint itself,
remaining basis approximations, and functional-reference consistency.

Why the first analytic approximation failed:
The stored-band G*dH*G action including window and X derivatives reproduces
its own nonlinear finite differences. However, it freezes radial functions
and truncates the complement. In SrVO3, the residual against the measured
relaxed charge derivative decreases from 170.26 (43 bands), to 109.94
(100), to 89.01 (200). The complete LAPW response with frozen radials still
leaves 75.73. Including radial response reduces this to 0.0174. FeSe has
426.41 with 66 stored bands and 0.0562 with the complete response. These are
norms of the same density-file coefficient vector, not pressure units.

The final screening solves took 9 actions in SrVO3 and 11 in FeSe, about
169 and 138 seconds of action wall time on this laptop. Including central
and source program evaluations gives about 218 and 172 seconds, before the
separate potential-operator stress contraction. Four MPI ranks were used
for dmft2, serial LAPW1. No impurity calls occurred.

Validation:
- Five independent algebra/unit checks pass: analytic resolvent/normalization
  derivative, uniform-potential gauge and fixed-N response, non-Hermitian
  input rejection, unstable linear screening rescued by Anderson, and
  honest nonconvergence reporting.
- Exported Python central band density agrees with native dmft2 to 3.3e-15
  in SrVO3 and 9e-16 in FeSe.
- Native metadata/k export reads back 75 SrVO3 k points and 43 bands.
- Signed density reconstruction keeps negative modes and rejects a wrong
  eigenvector gauge with nonzero status.
- Default native charge output is identical for SrVO3 and differs by at
  most 1.1e-18 for FeSe. The optional hooks are off by default.
- Fixed integer G sets, band counts, k order and window boundary gaps are
  checked. SrVO3: 75 k, 43 stored bands, W=[12,31]; FeSe: 140 k, 66 stored,
  W=[21,44]. Both original cases use RKmax=8.

Implementation bugs caught in the NEW diagnostic drivers (not attributed
as new bugs in the original production code):
- CLMVAL's spherical channel needs sqrt(4*pi) before adding core/CLMSUM.
- A fixed band count alone does not freeze the G set under strain. The final
  driver uses existing serial WIEN fixed-G marker support.
- Empty in1c files can exist in a real-vector calculation. Select the LAPW1
  executable from unit 5 in its definition file, not file existence.
- A WIEN x wrapper can return zero even after a Fortran error; logs and error
  files must be checked.

The reusable complete charge driver writes response.density plus predicted
physical endpoint potentials. The hydrostatic contraction in this audit
uses the existing frozen-central-basis potential probe and archived window
plus normalization adjoints; it is still a diagnostic projection step, not
an automatically enabled general stress-tensor implementation. The saved
projector_correction_matrices.npz allows its final contraction to be repeated
with frozen_sigma_projector_stress.py without the temporary vector files.

Current tested scope: nonmagnetic, no SOC, primitive orthogonal cell,
projector 5, fixed N, fixed RMT, hydrostatic strain. Do not substitute this
fixed-N driver for the fixed-mu MnO calculation. Magnetic MnTe and general
strain components remain untested.
