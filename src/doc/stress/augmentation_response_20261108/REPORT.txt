ordinary local-orbital angular factors, overlap and RAW projector assembly,

unchanged and does not publish these components as an applied total stress.

PRECISE SCOPE

These routines differentiate at FIXED geometry, RMT, radial grid, local frame,
central KS band gauge and stored plane-wave ordering. They are building blocks
for response to a potential, including the induced change in radial functions.
They do not themselves generate physical strain sources. Geometrical strain
derivatives, the screened charge response, and energy-search policy must still
be connected explicitly. Scalar NREL and scalar RELA are covered; a complete
spin-orbit or DIRACOUT p1/2 implementation is not supplied by this patch.

The all-radial material probe perturbs the potential used to GENERATE the
basis while holding the physical Hamiltonian potential fixed. The earlier
spherical module supplies the necessary physical-minus-basis restoration.
The nonspherical-potential probe separately varies the physical MT potential
at fixed radial basis. Neither probe is a change of lattice constants.

FORMULAS AND IMPLEMENTATION

1. Primitive radial integrals

For atom a, angular momentum l and primitive radial index i, phi_li contains
the native large/small radial components of u_l, its energy derivative, and
ordinary local-orbital solutions. Let W_rc be the exact discrete RINT13 metric,
including its origin term and scalar-relativistic small-component weight.
For a real lattice harmonic L of the potential,

  I_ij,L^(l,l') = sum_(r,c) W_rc phi_li(r,c) V_L(r) phi_l'j(r,c).

V_L here is V in Ry, NOT r*V. The native radial solver takes r*V in Ry; callers
must distinguish these two interfaces. On a fixed grid,

  delta I = <delta phi_l|V_L|phi_l'>
          + <phi_l|delta V_L|phi_l'>
          + <phi_l|V_L|delta phi_l'>.

All three terms are retained. The new Fortran module lapw_radial_potential
provides value, action and pullback. Different l and different numbers of
local orbitals on the two legs are allowed. Its reverse action returns
covectors for BOTH radial legs and V_L. Radial covectors from H, O, projector
and, later, density should be accumulated before one radial reverse solve
per atom/l. These operations are in the small primitive radial space.

2. Cache angular and plane-wave sums once

Use one central set of band coefficients C_Gn. In a consistent site/local-frame
gauge define

  gamma_mG = (4*pi/sqrt(Omega)) i^l Y_lm(q_local)^* phase_G,
  F_mn = sum_G gamma_mG j_l(|q|R) C_Gn,
  D_mn = sum_G gamma_mG |q| j_l'(|q|R) C_Gn,
  L_s,mn = sum_(G in ordinary LO slot s) gamma_mG C_Gn.

q=k+G in Cartesian units, Omega is cell volume and R=RMT. Local directions
and global lengths are supplied separately to preserve native treatment of
rounded local rotations. Complete LO contributions have the native R^2
prefactor. Matching values can be supplied from the host's native Bessel
implementation rather than recomputed with SciPy.

For stored boundary values A_u, A_udot and their radial derivatives AP,
the LAPW plane-wave parts of the primitive expansion coefficients Z are

  Z_m,u,n    = R (A_udot D_mn - AP_udot F_mn),
  Z_m,udot,n = R (AP_u F_mn - A_u D_mn).

The APW plane-wave part is Z_m,u,n=R F_mn/A_u. In both cases add

  Z_m,i,n += R^2 sum_s ABC_i,s L_s,mn.

Only boundary and ABC values change in this prepared potential-response map.
Its derivative and reverse require no repeated traversal of plane waves.
The cached object stores F, D and L, not the original large C array.
The existing radial primitive representation supplies changes in the radial
functions themselves separately from changes in these matching coefficients.

3. Small angular kernels and raw projector

Combine c=(l,m,i) into a component index. Define

  G_L(lm,l'm') = integral Y_lm^*(rhat) K_L(rhat) Y_l'm'(rhat) dOmega,
  K_cc' = sum_L G_L(lm,l'm') I_ij,L^(l,l'),
  S_cc' = delta_ll' delta_mm' <phi_li|phi_lj>.

K_L is the chosen real lattice harmonic; K is a potential kernel, not the
complete Hamiltonian. The supplied noncubic convention distinguishes cosine
and sine harmonics. In particular, sine Gaunt matrices are Hermitian but
need not be real: replacing G with G^T is wrong. A native caller may supply
its own Gaunt matrices, including expanded cubic-harmonic combinations.
Automatic construction of arbitrary cubic combinations is not implemented.

For a fixed correlated radial orbital chi_l and local orbital transform T,

  p_i = <phi_li|chi_l>,       P_(lmi),alpha = p_i T_m,alpha.

The band-space contributions are

  H_ns = Z^dagger K Z,
  O_MT = Z^dagger S Z,
  U_raw = Z^dagger P.

Here O_MT denotes the represented augmentation overlap block; full native
overlap also has interstitial and higher-l contributions as appropriate.
The complete spherical kinetic, boundary and native ordered-Wronskian terms
continue to come from lapw_radial_hamiltonian and native triangle assembly.
Do not replace those terms by the nonspherical kernel above.

The derivatives are

  delta H_ns = delta Z^dagger K Z + Z^dagger delta K Z + Z^dagger K delta Z,
  delta O_MT = delta Z^dagger S Z + Z^dagger delta S Z + Z^dagger S delta Z,
  delta U_raw = delta Z^dagger P + Z^dagger delta P.

Every off-diagonal band matrix element is retained. U_raw has band rows and
correlated-orbital columns. It is NOT the normalized DMFT projector. Window
selection, symmetry averaging and inverse-square-root normalization remain
in the shared electronic response map and must be differentiated there.
The material raw-projector probe used actual on-disk projector-5 radial
functions without forcing their finite MT boundary values to zero. Its
orbital transform was the identity in the m frame. Comparison with the full
native normalized dmft2 projector and equivalent-site orbital transforms
is still needed for the production adapter.

4. Reverse actions for one-adjoint screening

Use the real pairing <A,B>=Re Tr(A^dagger B). For Hermitian band covectors
Hbar and Obar and an arbitrary raw-projector covector Ubar, the reverse of
the small contractions is

  Zbar = 2 K Z Hbar + 2 S Z Obar + P Ubar^dagger,
  Kbar = Z Hbar Z^dagger,
  Sbar = Z Obar Z^dagger,
  Pbar = Z Ubar.

RadialAngularLayout then maps these covectors back through the Gaunt,
overlap and local-projector maps. All ordered radial pairs are represented;
there is no guessed factor of two for independently stored pairs. Prepared
matching and primitive-potential pullbacks return radial/boundary covectors
for the shared native radial reverse solve. Unit tests also compose this
assembly with the existing moving-metric, normalized-window frozen-Sigma
electronic response, using independent nonlinear Dyson endpoints.

NEXT IMPLEMENTATION STEP

Implement native spatial valence-density reconstruction and its transpose,
INCLUDING the explicit basis-density response. If Psi_k(r) is a row of
central band trial functions and rho_k their density matrix,

  delta n(r) = sum_k w_k [Psi_k delta rho_k Psi_k^dagger
                  + delta Psi_k rho_k Psi_k^dagger
                  + Psi_k rho_k delta Psi_k^dagger].

Weights are fixed and must be applied exactly once (the existing electronic
response already includes its production spin/k weighting). The first term
is a change of occupations/coherences. The other two are changes of the basis
and cannot be dropped or counted a second time as electronic response.
The radial/matching derivatives validated here supply their MT ingredients.

Implementation order:
  1. Add MT and interstitial spatial density actions and exact pullbacks;
     preserve native harmonic, spin, symmetry and charge conventions.
  2. Compare their joint action with one-step native charge-map endpoints,
     checking integrated charge under the chosen fixed-N or fixed-mu policy.
  3. Add core and Hartree/XC actions and pullbacks, with an explicit policy
     for linearization-energy search. Provide in-memory native bindings so
     iterations reuse radial and angular contexts without file round trips.
  4. Validate the complete screened charge operator J and J^T on FeSe/SrVO3.
     In the previous notation J=N [R E B + D + C_core] K:
       K maps charge to potential; B maps potential to joint H/O/raw P;
       E is the frozen-Sigma electronic response; R reconstructs charge;
       D is the direct basis-density map; C_core is core response;
       N enforces the output charge convention.
  5. Supply six geometrical strain sources b_nu and a common stress gradient
     g. Solve (I-J^T) lambda=g once and compare each d_nu+lambda^T b_nu
     against direct response, before converting energy derivatives to stress.
  6. Enable the validated fast backend in the existing dmft2_step executor,
     preserving final-iteration-only run_dmft scheduling, separate response
     scratch files, and validated/idempotent stress publication before merger.

The immediate next step is 1, not another adjustment fitted to pressures.
Full material screening and six-component physical stress remain unverified.
