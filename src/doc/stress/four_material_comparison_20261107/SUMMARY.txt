Provenance and interpretation
-----------------------------
SrVO3/FeSe: unchanged rows from the second-pass G1/G2 audit. Their central
analytic runs were refreshed in that pass, with existing converged FD references.

MnO/MnTe DFT: one full current lapw0 -> lapw1 -> lapw2 -> lcore -> merge ->
mixer evaluation on all three saved densities, in isolated copies. No new
full charge convergence. Stale impurity energy and SCF records were excluded
from pure-DFT assembly. Both use RELA and central RKMAX=7.

DFT MnO: 72 identical k points; 23 bands each; matching matrix dimensions
and plane-wave G sets across all three geometries. Final charge distances
are 1.0e-6, 1.2e-6, 1.2e-6 (minus, center, plus). Tetrahedron integration.

DFT MnTe: PROVISIONAL finite-difference comparison. The k points and weights
match, but the matrix sizes, band counts, and plane-wave G sets do not.
The central band counts range from 58 to 66. Charge distances are 3.916e-4,
5.31e-5, 8.454e-4. These endpoints need fixed-basis preparation and tighter
convergence before this difference is interpreted as a pure stress-code error.

DFT MnTe entropy convention: original TEMP=0.005 Ry adds -(T*S)/2 to SUM.
Analytic finite-temperature variational stress is compared here with the
full Mermin energy, using TEMPS. A second lapw2/mixer check on the identical
potential and vectors confirms the extra half-entropy energy shift and
byte-identical valence densities. Raw TEMP FD = 17.544477599 GPa; full TEMPS
FD = 17.199374314 GPa; analytic = 15.884272168 GPa. This entropy correction
does not fix the basis/convergence issues. Repeating the same density made
the copied endpoint MSR1 histories singular; only those scratch histories
were archived/reset before successful mixer verification. Originals untouched.

DMFT MnO: current dmft2 on the prior controlled fixed-mu dense-mesh inputs,
mu=6.609509262416999 eV, 601 Matsubara rows, with the current captured core.
This differs from the user -15.96903 GPa run, which recalculated mu and used
a 131-row mesh. Both are documented in the prior controls; do not mix them.
The existing endpoint valence charges are 20.992718 and 21.008041 at fixed mu.
The projector window [6,16] cuts a degenerate multiplet. The +55.94 GPa
number is therefore an APPARENT mismatch against a questionable reference,
not an established 55.94 GPa analytic-stress error. No mu*dN correction was
subtracted by hand. frozen_sigma_klein.py was rerun read-only on the original
endpoints, with identical frozen sig.inp and Eorb_imp.dat verified.

DMFT MnTe: current dmft2 rerun for BOTH full-cell magnetic mappings, with
the common captured central chemical potential and 501-row meshes. The
valence tensors are AVERAGED, then core and density contributions included
once. This is not a single up-spin result and is not a sum of two full-cell
valence tensors. The frozen-Sigma numerical reference averages SUM and VSUM
in the same convention. Its endpoints recalculate mu and keep charges close
to target; the [21,51] projector window still cuts some degeneracies and
endpoint convergence remains a limitation. No impurity solve or full DMFT
charge reconvergence was performed. Both magnetic runs terminate normally.
