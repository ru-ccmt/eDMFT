The four *_response workers are separate native export executables.
They are installed only in ~/dbin, not over production WIEN executables.
The source archive records the precise Fortran sources and Makefiles used.
Build on the target platform with its WIEN compiler/BLAS configuration; the
included binaries are local macOS/arm64 workers with Homebrew GCC/FFTW and
Accelerate dependencies. Do not copy these binaries to rupc Linux.
The existing dmft2_response and liblapw_response.so are reused (hashes in JSON).
Native export instrumentation is documented by the earlier augmentation and
XC/core checkpoints. It writes extra files in the isolated response workspace.
