CHARGE CONSISTENCY AND FIXED-N NATIVE VALENCE RESPONSE
8 November 2026

The existing FeSe RELA and SrVO3 NREL RKMAX=8 one-k fixtures were used. No user
material directory was changed. These are density-response controls, not new
pressures and not a full Brillouin-zone screened calculation.

1. BAND NUMBER VERSUS SPATIAL INTEGRATION

In the central generalized basis, the exact matrix particle-number variation is
  delta N=Tr(delta rho_RR+rho_R delta O_RR).
The complement cross blocks have zero central trace. At fixed N this vanishes
to roundoff for all three tested native potential directions.

For the combined spherical+nonspherical+interstitial direction:
                                      FeSe                  SrVO3
  matrix delta N                      -1.78e-15             -8.88e-16
  spatial delta N, native l<=8          1.606e-6              1.408e-6
  spatial delta N, all basis l<=10      1.036e-7              3.302e-8

The radial integration in those spatial rows uses the actual native RINT13
metric, including its origin convention. The older charge-assembly cubic-spline
integral gives closely similar results, recorded in the JSON files.

The central integrated charge minus band trace is:
  FeSe:  -1.962e-5 electrons with l<=8; -4.050e-8 with l<=10.
  SrVO3: -2.211e-5 electrons with l<=8;  2.879e-8 with l<=10.

Integrating the complete alias-free Fourier product instead of the stored
native density-star list does not change these numbers to displayed precision.
The dominant discrepancy is therefore the l<=8 spatial-density truncation
relative to the l<=10 LAPW basis. The full-angular reconstruction is a diagnostic
and was not substituted silently for the native production density. A smaller
remaining discrete/assembly residual is not fully attributed here.

This does not justify adding a new projector or stress term. The fast map must
continue to follow the explicitly chosen native spatial representation, and
charge normalization remains a separate operation after core assembly.

2. FIXED-N ENDPOINTS WITH NATIVE DMFT2

The same joint potential perturbation used in the general-input checkpoint was
applied at h=0.001 and 0.0005. Native vectors/potentials were reused. dmft2 was
run with recomputeEF=1, fresh projector normalization and a common target N equal
to the central one-k band count (59.6304906272382 / 41.13459318723834). These
noninteger targets belong only to the isolated one-k controls.

Relative spatial-density derivative error:
                          FeSe                SrVO3
  native EF, h=0.001       4.026e-7            1.762e-6
  native EF, h=0.0005      8.504e-7            2.557e-4

The small-step SrVO3 anomaly was traced to endpoint chemical-potential accuracy.
cmp_EF in l2mainSubs.f90 sets fatol=1e-10 electrons, xatol=xrtol=1e-9 Ry. A
mu error permitted by the latter tolerances can dominate a small finite
difference, even when ordinary SCF convergence is satisfactory.

An independent nonlinear particle-count root was therefore solved at each
endpoint using its exported KS energies, normalized projectors, Sigma, Matsubara
quadrature and static restoration. This uses the nonlinear density evaluator,
not the analytic tangent being tested. High states are thermally empty; the
retained space contains all occupied states and the complete DMFT window.
Root residuals are <=7.11e-15 electrons. Native dmft2 was then rerun with
recomputeEF=0 at those independently polished chemical potentials.

                          FeSe                SrVO3
  polished mu, h=0.001     4.054e-7            1.762e-6
  polished mu, h=0.0005    4.300e-7            1.061e-6

The problematic SrVO3 plus endpoint needed a correction of only 5.95e-9 eV.
The resulting dmu/dh is about 0.5308852 eV in FeSe and 0.5292491 eV in SrVO3.
The production chemical-potential solver and its tolerances were NOT changed.
For future small finite-difference controls, endpoint mu accuracy must be
checked together with density convergence rather than assumed sufficient.

3. SCOPE AND FILES

This extends the general potential-response validation from fixed mu to fixed N.
It does not validate a complete Hartree/XC/core charge map or pressure. Full
material gauge shifts still require an explicit policy for shifting radial and
LO energies with the potential; existing native radial shift-cancellation tests
pass, but the entire material gauge test is not yet complete.

JSON files retain the raw and full-angular charge checks, ordinary native EF
endpoints, and polished endpoints. Drivers use scratch copies and explicit case
names. Native endpoints are under
  /private/tmp/edmft_valence_charge_checks_20261108/{FeSe,SrVO3}/.
Earlier potential endpoints and central fixtures remain in their dated scratch
directories. No ordinary eDMFT input or executable was modified by these tests.
