DECOMPOSITION: KEEP GEOMETRY AND POTENTIAL RESPONSE SEPARATE

Let F(eta)=I+eta e, with symmetric dimensionless strain direction e. The scalar
eta is the deformation amplitude. Lattice vectors transform by F, reciprocal
vectors by F^{-T}. Fractional atomic positions are fixed. For hydrostatic
strain e=I, dOmega/deta=3 Omega. RMT is fixed in physical Bohr, not scaled.

All derivatives below use the CENTRAL coefficient gauge. C contains central
retained KS columns C_R and an O-orthonormal complement C_Q, arranged [R,Q].
R denotes the exported/retained bands, NOT the smaller DMFT window W. Q is
the rest of the same finite LAPW space. This construction does not claim
completeness of the infinite one-electron Hilbert space.

Define
  b = (delta H_RR, delta O_RR, delta Phi_R,
       delta H_QR, delta O_QR).
Phi is the raw orbital overlap before DMFT window selection, symmetry and
inverse-square-root normalization. H and O are covariant coefficient matrices,
projected with central C; no derivative of C is included in their definition.
The common electronic operation then calculates the eigenvector/window,
overlap-metric and normalized-projector changes exactly once.

Let B be the already implemented derivative of b with respect to native
potential coefficients. Let K map a charge change to those potential changes.
The installed hydrostatic potential helper supplies s_V. For induced material
charge q, the joint perturbation is

  b_total = b_geometry + B (s_V + K q).                       (1)

b_geometry is the new helper's output. It holds potential FILE coefficients
fixed, including the masked interstitial VNS coefficients. Therefore it has
no separate explicit derivative of the interstitial potential matrix. The
moving interstitial potential mask belongs in s_V. Fixed file coefficients
are not identical to a fixed physical real-space potential. This distinction
must also be preserved when subtracting the existing Blaha stress baseline.

Notation in the code: raw=Phi; hrr/orr=RR blocks; hqr/oqr=QR blocks;
nr=number of retained bands; npw=number of plane-wave rows. Rectangular columns
carry Hermitian RR and unrestricted complex QR; their RQ adjoint is implicit.

ANGULAR AND BOUNDARY MATCHING DERIVATIVE

For q_i=k+G_i, q_i'=-e q_i and Omega'=tr(e) Omega. Write Q_i=|q_i|,
Q_i'=q_i.q_i'/Q_i and n_i=q_i/Q_i. The phase exp(i G_i.R_a) is fixed under
affine deformation; likewise the raw projector's exp(i (k+G_i).R_a) phase.
An atom's local frame is held fixed in Cartesian coordinates in this adapter.

For spherical harmonic Y_lm, define
  n'=(q'-n(n.q'))/Q, omega=n cross n'.
Then Y_lm'=i(omega.L Y_l)_m, evaluated using the angular-momentum ladder
matrices. This avoids dividing by sin(theta) at a pole. The zero reciprocal
vector stays zero under homogeneous strain and has zero angular derivative.

The common augmentation factor is
  g_mi = 4 pi i^l Y_lm(q_i)^* exp(i G_i.R_a)/sqrt(Omega),
  g_mi' = 4 pi i^l exp(i G_i.R_a)/sqrt(Omega)
          [Y_lm'^* - tr(e) Y_lm^*/2].                       (2)

Let j_i=j_l(Q_i R), d_i=Q_i j_l'(Q_i R), R=RMT. A prime on j_l in these
expressions denotes its argument derivative; a prime on a coefficient means
the strain derivative. To avoid that ambiguity in implementation:
  delta j_i = R delta Q_i j_l'(Q_i R),
  delta d_i = delta Q_i [j_l'(Q_i R)+Q_i R j_l''(Q_i R)].   (3)
The Bessel ODE gives j_l'', with analytic Q=0 limits and no division by j_l.

Prepared augmentation sums are
  F_mn=sum_PW g_mi j_i C_in,
  D_mn=sum_PW g_mi d_i C_in,
  L_j,mn=sum_(rows of LO j) g_mi C_in.
Differentiate both g and matching in F,D, and g in L. Holding the central
radial primitives and ordinary LO coefficients fixed, this gives Z', where
Z[m,radial primitive,n] is the full augmentation coefficient. Z' includes
the same RMT^2 local-orbital prefactor as the original channel.

Spherical volume kernels S and K are held fixed in this partial derivative:
  O_MT'=Z'^dagger S Z + Z^dagger S Z',
  H_volume'=Z'^dagger K Z + Z^dagger K Z'.                   (4)
Angular indices are summed. K here is a SMALL RADIAL Hamiltonian kernel,
not the charge-to-potential operation K in (1); the code names it op.k.
These formulas retain both off-diagonal Hermitian legs.

Native spherical surface terms use p=boundary VALUE of the PW augmentation,
d=boundary SLOPE of the full PW+LO augmentation. The native LO boundary value
is set to zero, but its slope is retained. Thus
  H_surface'=R^2/2 [p'^dagger d+p^dagger d'
                    +d'^dagger p+d^dagger p'].             (5)

The extra native LAPW Wronskian term is
  H_W=R^2/2 w(w-1) T,
  T=C^dagger F_upper{A_ij(d_i j_j-j_i d_j)} C,
  A_ij=sum_m g_mi^* g_mj.                                  (6)
w is the central radial Wronskian, fixed here. F_upper copies the coefficient
matrix's upper triangle to its conjugate lower triangle. Differentiate A and
both Bessel factors BEFORE this ordered copy and central-band projection.
Symmetric averaging is not equivalent. APW has no Wronskian correction.

For the nonspherical radial/angular kernel V_ns,
  H_ns'=Z'^dagger V_ns Z + Z^dagger V_ns Z'.                 (7)
Its radial and explicit potential changes are already handled by B, not here.
For raw projector radial overlaps p_i and fixed local-frame transform T_ma,
  Phi_na'=sum_mi Z_mi,n'^* p_i T_ma.                        (8)
The projector does not need to vanish at RMT; its matching/boundary dependence
is retained. Its radial response to changing spherical potential belongs in B.

INTERSTITIAL OVERLAP AND KINETIC ENERGY

With D_ij=q_i-q_j, x_a=|D_ij|R_a, f(x)=j_1(x)/x and f(0)=1/3,
  Theta_ij=delta_ij-sum_a [4 pi R_a^3/Omega] f(x_a)
                           exp(i (G_j-G_i).R_a).
This is the exact sphere-exclusion overlap matrix, not LAPW0's finite-FFT
potential mask. Define d_ij=D_ij.e.D_ij/|D_ij|^2 (zero for D_ij=0). Then
  Theta_ij'=sum_a [4 pi R_a^3/Omega] phase_a
       {tr(e) f(x_a)+d_ij [j_0(x_a)-3 f(x_a)]}.             (9)
Native interstitial kinetic energy in Ry is (q_i.q_j)Theta_ij, hence
  T_IR,ij'=-2(q_i.e.q_j)Theta_ij+(q_i.q_j)Theta_ij'.         (10)
Project both derivatives as C^dagger(...)C_R. LO rows have no interstitial
component. No derivative of masked VNS coefficients is added at this stage.

NEXT: FORM THE COMPLETE ANALYTIC CHARGE SOURCE

The installed full J is the derivative of the unmixed physical output-charge
map at fixed geometry. It is not the mixer algorithm and not a DMFT impurity
vertex susceptibility. The response equation is still
  (I-J) q = s_charge.
Numerical native hydrostatic s_charge and q are already available. The
remaining task is to replace s_charge by a completely analytic source.

Let E map joint perturbations to the density matrix, R reconstruct spatial
valence charge, D_V be the explicit radial/basis-density potential response,
C_V the core response, and N the native output-charge normalization map.
At fixed input MATERIAL charge the unnormalized native-coordinate source is
  d_out = R E [b_geometry+B s_V] + (D_V+C_V)s_V + d_val,geometry.
The explicit valence spatial-geometry term d_val,geometry is not supplied by
this band helper. Neither are the explicit geometry change of N nor the
inverse density-transport contribution that converts native output charge
back to the central material coordinates. Those must be included once:
  s_charge = N' d_out + (partial_eta N)_explicit
             - tau_(central normalized output).
This is schematic notation for the existing discrete maps, not an instruction
to replace them by continuum formulas. At a nonzero fixed-point residual, the
last transport acts on the central OUTPUT, which need not equal input n0.
