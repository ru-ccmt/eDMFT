1. WHICH FUNCTIONAL IS BEING DIFFERENTIATED?

For the supported nonmagnetic frozen-Sigma tangent, native bookkeeping is

  F = ENE - SUM + YSUM - EORB + XEORB,
  Tr[(Sigma-Vdc)Gloc] = VSUM-YSUM,
  K_frozen = ENE - SUM + VSUM - EORB.

MIXER's ordinary LDA/PBE branch adds valence SUM, orbital EORB, core
eigenvalues and the LAPW0 density term DEN. Thus the strain-dependent tangent
is the lattice trace-log term with the appropriate mu*N convention, plus
the core eigenvalue sum and DEN. The impurity tangent alignment is a constant.
This does not say that the lattice scalar ALONE is the Klein functional.

Source audit:
  src/dmft2/l2main.f90: :YSUM/:VSUM labels near lines 2173-2177.
  WIEN SRC_mixer/scfana.f: SUM/EORB near 251-264, core occupations and
    multiplicities near 326 onward, DEN and core energy addition near 610-629.
  WIEN SRC_lcore/hfsd.f: eigenvalue 2*DEN+inc_shift near 457-465.
  WIEN SRC_lapw0/energy.F: radial/IR density terms and nuclear constants.
  src/python/frozen_sigma_klein.py: implemented tangent bookkeeping.

At fixed geometry, continuum notation suggests

  C[n] = E_H[n]+E_xc[n]-int n(V_H[n]+V_xc[n]) + E_ion,
  dC = -int n dV_Hxc.

The second equality uses a variational identity. It MUST be checked for
the implemented discrete native functional. The energy and potential use
splines, angular projections, finite Fourier sets, masks and gradient
stencils; differentiating the scalar need not reproduce the separately
discretized continuum potential.

Let n be the INPUT density, K = dV_Hxc/dn, and W the native spatial pairing.
Define the actual discrete residual covector

  r_C = grad_n C + K^T W n.

Then the fixed-geometry derivative is exactly

  dK_frozen = g_b db + g_core dV_Hxc - (W n)^T dV_Hxc + r_C^T dn,

with the lattice covector g_b already installed. If B maps a potential to
the full joint H/O/raw-projector perturbation, the charge readout is

  c = K^T [ B^T g_b + g_core - W n ] + r_C.

This c is the eventual right-hand side of ONE shared adjoint,
(I-J)^T lambda=c. Explicit geometric/source contributions belong in a_nu,
and lambda^T s_nu supplies the screened part. Native charge normalization
already enters q and J: it must not be applied a second time to this scalar.
Explicit density transport also enters the geometric scalar derivative;
adding only r_C^T q to the existing stress would be incomplete.

2. SPATIAL PAIRING

Native density coordinates are 4*pi*r^2*n_sph in the spherical MT channel,
r^2*n_lm in other MT channels, and complex Fourier STAR SUMS in the IR.
The potential field contains r*V_sph (Ry), V_lm (Ry), and already masked
per-member interstitial Fourier coefficients. Therefore W is diagonal:

  W_sph = m_a * CHARGE_weight / r,
  W_lm  = m_a * CHARGE_weight,
  W_PW  = Omega on both real and imaginary coordinates.

m_a is the number of equivalent atoms. No extra Fourier star multiplicity
or mask belongs in W. SpatialPotentialPairing implements this bilinear map,
its two derivatives and coordinate/grid checks. It does not change the
density cutoff, radial quadrature or charge normalization.

Use the input density for differentiating LAPW0 DEN. Replacing it by the
raw output density, normalized output density, or band-matrix P^T*rho is
not justified away from a fixed point. The earlier angular-cutoff test
also demonstrated that matrix and spatial contractions can differ even
with fixed coefficients. That issue remains in the complete audit.

3. CORE EIGENVALUE SUM

For atom a, core state i, occupation f_ai, multiplicity m_a, matching
energy E_ai in Hartree and fixed inc shift t_a in Ry,

  E_core = sum_ai m_a f_ai (2 E_ai + t_a),
  F_ai(E,V)=0  =>  dE_ai = -F_ai,V dV / F_ai,E.

MatchedCoreState already supplies the exact discrete energy covector from
inward/outward matching, including origin and outer boundary terms. T_a
maps native delta(rV_Ry) to the shifted/extrapolated Hartree potential on
the extended core grid. The new readout is

  g_core[a] = 2 m_a T_a^T sum_i f_ai grad_V E_ai.

The factor two converts Hartree to Ry; it is NOT an additional spin factor.
The occupations already contain degeneracy. The inc shift is held fixed.
The exterior fit and its central clamp branch are differentiated. Omitting
the extension loses a derivative even though CLMCOR is written only in MT.
The split into interior/exterior gradients is diagnostic, not two stresses.
At fixed RMT, the explicit hydrostatic core eigenvalue derivative uses the
explicit potential source; this is not permission to replace the existing
rearranged E3 stress with an isolated eigenvalue derivative.

4. DISCRETE XC ENERGY DERIVATIVE: THE NEW TERM

Let e[n] be native XC energy per electron (Ry), in the same spatial field
coordinates as V_xc. The new energy field uses exactly the native radial
spline, angular mean subtraction, Fourier representative selection and IR
mask. Native low-density branches are retained. XCPOT3 averages the vacuum
POTENTIAL, not its energy field; these maps therefore remain distinct.

  E_xc = n^T W e[n],
  g_E = W e + (De)^T W n,
  r_xc = g_E - W V_xc,
  grad_n [E_xc - n^T W V_xc] = r_xc - K_xc^T W n.

PreparedXCEnergy implements these gradients. Its partial derivatives are
prepared once by complex-step differentiation of local analytic formulas;
subsequent contractions are cached dot products. There are no density-
coordinate finite differences, new screened solves or impurity calls.
The native potential remains the default observable in lapw_xc_response;
an energy field cannot be used as an Hxc potential operator.
