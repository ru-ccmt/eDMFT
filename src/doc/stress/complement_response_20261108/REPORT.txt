Implementation checkpoint, 8 November 2026

RESULT AND CURRENT POSITION IN THE PLAN

The missing-state response identified by the previous checkpoint now has a
working projected implementation. It uses native central H/O and the retained
KS eigenvectors, constructs their complete coefficient-space complement, and
solves a static equation with reusable factors. Matsubara work remains in the
retained space. Full off-diagonal DMFT density, moving overlap metric, raw
projector response, overlap symmetry and normalization are preserved.

In independent FeSe RELA and SrVO3 NREL material assemblies it reproduces the
previous complete-basis spatial-density response to about 1e-13, and the native
finite-difference response to 1.19e-7 and 1.78e-7, respectively. These are
valence-density response tests for spherical MT potential perturbations. They
are not new stress/pressure results or a complete screened-response validation.

This completes the complement part of roadmap step 1 for the tested scope.
Step 1 still needs general potential inputs, a reusable production material

1. SPACES, METRIC AND INPUTS

Everything here is at fixed central geometry. Radial potential perturbations
may change the basis; the radial/LO energies, RMT and local axes are fixed in
the material tests. Sigma, double counting and temperature are fixed. Material
tests use fixed mu; algebraic tests exercise fixed mu and fixed particle number.
Energies are Ry. Each k point has an independent construction below.

H0 and O0 are the native coefficient-space Hamiltonian and positive overlap.
C_R contains Nr retained generalized KS eigenvectors, with energies e_i:

  H0 C_R = O0 C_R e_R,       C_R^dagger O0 C_R = I,
  e_R = diag(e_1,...,e_Nr).                                  (1)

R contains the entire chosen DMFT window W and all appreciably occupied KS
states. W and R are DIFFERENT selections: R is a response representation.
The implementation checks that every state in Q, the remaining finite LAPW
coefficient space, is thermally empty within explicit tolerances. If it cannot
establish this, it rejects the setup and requires more retained bands.

Factor O0=L L^dagger. Complete an ordinary QR of Y=L^dagger C_R, and let Z_Q
span its orthonormal complement. Then

  C_Q = L^(-dagger) Z_Q,
  C_Q^dagger O0 C_Q=I,   C_Q^dagger O0 C_R=0,
  H_Q=C_Q^dagger H0 C_Q.                                    (2)

C_Q is an arbitrary orthonormal basis of Q. It is NOT a matrix of high-energy
KS eigenvectors. No energies/eigenvectors above R are needed to construct (2).
The native H/O and retained eigenpair residuals are checked before proceeding.

Define projected derivatives in this central representation:

  h_QR=C_Q^dagger delta H C_R,
  o_QR=C_Q^dagger delta O C_R,
  h_RR=C_R^dagger delta H C_R,
  o_RR=C_R^dagger delta O C_R.                               (3)

Phi_R and Phi_Q are RAW overlaps of the respective trial functions with the
fixed correlated orbital. They are assembled with the same actual local
frames, Bloch phases and radial projector convention as the native code.
They have not yet been selected by W or multiplied by its normalization X.

2. THE STATIC COMPLEMENT EQUATION

Differentiate (1) and project it on C_Q. Terms from the retained energy change
and motion within R drop out of this projection. The coefficient T of motion
into Q satisfies

  H_Q T - T e_R = -B,        B=h_QR-o_QR e_R.                (4)

For each retained column i this is

  (H_Q-e_i I) T_i = -B_i.                                   (5)

This is a projected Sternheimer-type equation with a prepared direct solve.
It is not a self-consistent charge loop, an impurity vertex equation, or a
Matsubara-frequency-dependent complement solve. Only central data are factored.

The raw overlap as viewed by the moving retained states changes by

  delta Phi_R,eff = delta Phi_R + T^dagger Phi_Q.            (6)

The second term is essential. Adding only a missing density cross block and
omitting (6) would miss the feedback on overlap normalization and the embedded
self-energy INSIDE the retained problem. The existing retained electronic
response acts on (h_RR,o_RR,delta Phi_R,eff), including its own W-window motion,
symmetrized k sum and derivative of the inverse-square-root normalization.
No independent normalization or extra Sigma response is added a second time.

3. WHY THERE IS NO NEW FREQUENCY SUM IN THE COMPLEMENT

First use the symmetric orthonormal representation. Define

  h_tilde_QR = h_QR - (H_Q o_QR + o_QR e_R)/2,
  T_tilde = T + o_QR/2.                                    (7)

Then h_tilde_QR=T_tilde e_R-H_Q T_tilde. Let S_R(z) be the
retained embedded self-energy, G_R(z)=[z-e_R-S_R(z)]^(-1),
and g_Q(z)=[z-H_Q]^(-1); z includes the same chemical potential convention.
The central embedded self-energy has no Q leg. Motion of the DMFT window gives

  delta S_QR(z)=T_tilde S_R(z).

Consequently the cross Green-function derivative obeys the exact identity

  delta G_QR
    =g_Q [h_tilde_QR+T_tilde S_R] G_R
    =T_tilde G_R-g_Q T_tilde.                               (8)

This is valid for an off-diagonal S_R and rho_R; no commuting-density
assumption is made. It also respects the existing finite Matsubara quadrature
and static subtraction: the identity holds frequency by frequency, with the
static restoration transforming covariantly. Summing gives

  delta rho_QR,orth = T_tilde rho_R-rho_Q T_tilde.           (9)

With a thermally empty Q, rho_Q is negligible. Undoing the overlap
orthonormalization subtracts o_QR rho_R/2, hence

  delta rho_QR,coeff = T rho_R,
  delta rho_RQ,coeff = (T rho_R)^dagger,
  delta rho_QQ,coeff = 0.                                  (10)

rho_R includes the electronic model's spin/k weights. None are reapplied.
Equation (6) follows consistently from the same transformation: the
-o_RQ Phi_Q/2 in raw-overlap orthonormalization cancels the +o_RQ Phi_Q/2
inside T_tilde^dagger Phi_Q. Retaining both would double count the metric leg.

The retained coefficient derivative is therefore

  delta rho_RR = E_R(h_RR,o_RR,delta Phi_R+T^dagger Phi_Q).  (11)

Together (4), (10), (11) replace a full spectral electronic response. The
direct change of the spatial basis is still added separately in R_density E B+D.
This module is NOT by itself a correction to the Klein stress functional.

At fixed N, the retained response determines delta mu. Cross blocks have no
central trace and rho_Q has negligible occupation/slope, so

  delta N=Tr(delta rho_RR+rho_R o_RR).                       (12)

The existing fixed-N projector and its transpose act within E_R. The covariance
and transpose tests exercise this explicitly, as well as fixed-mu response.

4. REUSABLE DIRECT SOLVE

Reduce H_Q once to Hermitian tridiagonal form

  H_Q=U tridiag(b*,d,b) U^dagger.                           (13)

This computes a unitary reduction, not high-state KS eigenvectors. The smallest
tridiagonal eigenvalue is computed ONLY for gap/thermal validation. Each shift
e_i has its own cheap tridiagonal LDL^dagger factors:

  D_0i=d_0-e_i,
  L_ji=b_j/D_ji,
  D_(j+1),i=d_(j+1)-e_i-|b_j|^2/D_ji.                      (14)

All columns are factored together. Each later action uses U^dagger B, the
cached forward/back substitutions, and U. No new eigensolve, inversion,
iterative convergence loop, native program launch or frequency sum is needed.

Cost is O(Nq^3) preparation, O(Nq^2 Nr) per action, and O(Nq^2+Nq Nr) storage
for the complement solve. The construction of C_Q also needs dense native H/O
and O(Nbasis^2) storage. This is a tested direct baseline; it is not claimed
optimal for very large bases or MPI. The complete material adapter currently
still assembles full square basis matrices and discards unused QQ input blocks.
Rectangular assembly and density contractions offer further optimizations.

The implementation requires min eig(H_Q)-max e_R > 1e-8 Ry by default. It
rejects a degenerate cut instead of replacing denominators with a regulator.
Both the omitted charge bound 2 Nq f(Emin-mu) and its conservative Fermi-slope
bound divided by temperature must be <=1e-12 in their stated units. These are
occupation/slope bounds, not a universal response-error bound for arbitrary
unbounded input matrices. Underflow prints zero for these deeply empty cases.

SrVO3's retained/complement boundary gap in this one-k probe is only
2.92384e-6 Ry; FeSe's is 0.0553774 Ry. The former passes the explicit threshold
and numerical residual checks, but larger general perturbation tests should
keep boundary conditioning under review. A cut through an actual degeneracy
must include the complete degenerate cluster.

5. TRANSPOSE AND COMPOSITION

Hermitian RR/QR block pairing is

  <Y,delta rho> = Re Tr(Y_RR^dagger delta rho_RR)
                   +2 Re Tr(Y_QR^dagger delta rho_QR).      (15)

The factor two is represented by sqrt(2) coordinates for both real and
imaginary QR parts. Raw overlaps use the ordinary complex Frobenius pairing.

Apply the retained generalized adjoint to Y_RR, giving bars on h_RR,o_RR,
Phi_R,eff. Reverse (6) and (10):

  T_bar=Phi_Q Phi_bar_R,eff^dagger +2 Y_QR rho_R.            (16)

The operator B -> T in (4) is self-adjoint in the ordinary rectangular
Frobenius pairing, since each shifted H_Q is Hermitian. Reuse the same solve
on T_bar to get B_bar. In the FULL Hermitian block pairing,

  h_bar_QR=B_bar/2,        o_bar_QR=-B_bar e_R/2.            (17)

The retained raw-overlap bar is unchanged. QQ bars vanish. The block-coordinate
adapter exposes a PairedOperator so these forward/reverse operations compose
with spatial density, potential and the later screening/adjoint operators.
