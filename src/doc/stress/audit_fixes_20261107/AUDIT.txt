1. Equations that remain the correct starting point
--------------------------------------------------
Use U[n,l]=<psi_n|phi_l>, with n a KS band and l a correlated orbital.
U has dimensions Nband x Norbital. Sigma below includes the double-counting
subtraction. k weights and Matsubara weights are included consistently in rho,
rhoE, and C; they are not differentiated.

  G(z)^(-1) = z I - epsilon - U Sigma(z) U^dagger,
  rho       = T sum_z G(z),
  rhoE      = T sum_z [z G(z)-I],
  C         = T sum_z Sigma(z) U^dagger G(z),
  B_L       = rhoE - epsilon rho = U C,
  M_Sigma   = rhoE - {epsilon,rho}/2.

The sums mean the convergent, consistently regularized moments used in the
functional, not unregularized divergent sums. epsilon is diagonal only at the
central KS reference. The one-particle and projector parts of the derivative are

  D_1p   = Re Tr[rho H' - rhoE O'],
  D_proj = 2 Re Tr[C U'].

For a stationary interacting functional, implicit G and self-energy responses
cancel against the interaction-functional terms. Explicit projector and basis
responses remain. These identities do not by themselves make a frozen-Sigma
endpoint free energy a stationary functional; that numerical comparison still
requires the agreed frozen-Sigma reference correction and relaxed DFT charge.

The generalized G2 local definitions are most safely written as integrals.
For f_i=u_i(r)Y_i(n), define differentiated bra functions

  g_i^0=f_i,
  g_i^r=r u_i'(r) n_alpha n_beta Y_i(n),
  g_i^a=u_i(r) A_alpha_beta Y_i(n),

where A_alpha_beta is the symmetric angular strain generator. Let

  S^x_ij = <g_i^x|f_j>,
  H^x_ij = <g_i^x|H_sph f_j>,
  V^x_ij = <g_i^x|V_ns f_j>.

A contains the central augmentation coefficients; D contains their
volume-compensated matching derivatives. In this notation the six one-sided
contractions, each completed by 2 Re, are

  d1 = 2 Re Tr[rho D^dagger H^0 A - rhoE D^dagger S^0 A],
  d2 = 2 Re Tr[rho A^dagger H^r A - rhoE A^dagger S^r A],
  d3 = 2 Re Tr[rho A^dagger H^a A - rhoE A^dagger S^a A],
  d4 = 2 Re Tr[rho D^dagger V^0 A],
  d5 = 2 Re Tr[rho A^dagger V^r A],
  d6 = 2 Re Tr[rho A^dagger V^a A].

Here primitive radial indices include u, udot, and every LO radial channel.
For the NREL u/udot channels, H^x_ij=E_j S^x_ij + delta(j=udot) S^x_i,j(u).
LO primitives use their own linearization energies. A scalar-relativistic
implementation needs its actual radial/operator conventions; the NREL identity
is not an independent derivation of every RELA correction.

For Y=D^dagger S^0 A + A^dagger(S^r+S^a)A and
Z=D^dagger(H^0+V^0)A + A^dagger(H^r+V^r+H^a+V^a)A,
the Hermitian basis matrices are O_eta'=Y+Y^dagger and B'=Z+Z^dagger.
The explicit operator/metric part T' and explicit KS-field response must be
included in the SAME frame to obtain full H'.

A Hermitian density matrix can be diagonalized into signed pseudo-band weights
and used to evaluate these quadratic forms. Thus pseudo-bands are not themselves
a diagonal-density approximation. However, the scalar kernel must be the full
bilinear form for arbitrary complex vectors. Agreement only on individual KS
eigenvectors does not establish that property.

Likewise K(E=0)-K(E=1) extracts the coefficient of E of the implemented kernel.
It is the physical overlap derivative only if that kernel contains the complete
H-E O bilinear form. Missing LO terms or a different derivative frame invalidate
that inference without making the subtraction numerically inaccurate.

2. Confirmed error: complex conjugation in the outside-window response
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: src/dmft2/l2mainSubs.f90, BuildWindowResponseMatrices, especially
lines 2492-2522; also BuildNormalizationWindowResponseMatrices, lines 3500-3530.
The projector convention is fixed by Build_DMFT_Projector, lines 1101-1109,
which stores conjg(tmp), and by the U Sigma U^dagger upfolding convention.

For an included band n and excluded stored band a,

  R_an = (H'_an - epsilon_n O'_an)/(epsilon_n-epsilon_a),
  |psi_n>' = sum_a |psi_a> R_an,
  U'_nl = sum_a R_an^* U_al.

Therefore, with K_an=(U_out C)_an,

  D_window = 2 Re sum_an K_an R_an^*.

The conjugation is essential: the projector differentiates the BRA <psi_n|.
For a Hermitian trace representation, the correct response blocks are

  rhoResp(a,n) = K_an/(epsilon_n-epsilon_a),
  rhoResp(n,a) = rhoResp(a,n)^*,
  eResp(a,n)   = epsilon_n rhoResp(a,n),
  eResp(n,a)   = eResp(a,n)^*.

The current routines put K/denominator in the (n,a) block instead. This is a
transpose/conjugation error, not a missing many-body vertex.

Independent test: six complex bands, three included bands, two orbitals,
a static Hermitian Sigma, nondegenerate eigenvalues, and an exactly evaluated
finite-dimensional grand potential. The actual production Fortran routine was
compiled unchanged and called from the test.

  Functional central difference             0.008643713866885
  Direct correct projector derivative      0.008643713871336
  Production matrix contraction            0.009201619001834
  Same production contraction after
    harmless rephasing of KS eigenvectors -0.040138877916062
  Corrected response orientation           0.008643713871336

Maximum eigenvector/projector derivative FD error: 9.20e-11.
The arbitrary KS phase must not change an observable. This failure alone is
decisive and does not rely on any stress convention or LAPW approximation.

A second independent test also varies the generalized overlap O. It gives
functional FD 0.012257018167094, direct derivative 0.012257018152324,
production Tr(rhoResp H'-eResp O') -0.000559129226874, and corrected
Tr(rhoResp H'-eResp O') 0.012257018152324. Thus both response blocks are tested.

The normalization-window routine has the same defect:

  Direct derivative                       -0.063660720766706
  Independent finite difference           -0.063660720762102
  Production matrix contraction            0.789495696605235
  Corrected response orientation          -0.063660720766706

The old checker, src/python/check_projector_sternheimer_fd.py, repeats the error
in its docstring and rotation contractions around lines 535-590. Its previous
agreement with production was circular. Those validations must be repeated with
an independent endpoint eigenproblem and a band-phase covariance test.

The shared response construction is also called for forces. The scope therefore
includes the finite-window force correction; it does not imply that the ordinary
charge self-consistency or original DFT force kernel has this same bug.

3. Passed: matrix normalization derivative and its adjoint
--------------------------------------------------------
Use raw P, global normalization S=sum_k w_k P_k^dagger P_k, X=S^(-1/2), U=P X.
The exact positive-definite matrix derivative is

  X^(-1) X' + X' X^(-1) = -X S' X.

ComputeNormalizationDerivative returns the relative derivative X^(-1) X',
not X' itself. Its noncommuting complex-matrix test against independent endpoint
diagonalizations gives max error 4.64e-12 for X' and Hermiticity error 2.11e-17.
BuildNormalizationAdjoint agrees with the independent derivative to 1.60e-11.
AddProjectorStressNormalization uses the corresponding relative derivative
correctly in U'=P'X+U(X^(-1)X').

This validates the square-root calculus, NOT completeness of the supplied S'.

In a fixed-coefficient nonorthogonal window frame the physical normalization is

  S=sum_k w_k P_k^dagger O_W,k^(-1) P_k,
  S'=sum_k w_k[P'^dagger P + P^dagger P' - P^dagger O_W' P]

at the central orthonormal reference. In a moving orthonormal frame this same
metric connection is already part of P'. It must appear exactly once.

The explicit cmp_overlap strain path (l2mainSubs.f90:1702-1720) forms only the
first two terms. The missing metric contribution is available through the
default-off EDMFT_STRESS_NORM_WINDOW_METRIC diagnostic, and is also enabled by
MOVING_BAND. Default-off status is a real completeness issue for the stated
fixed-coefficient interpretation. It is not a license to add the metric term
again in an already orthonormal moving-frame derivative.

A separate general basis-change identity gives a naive S' norm 0.0716904 and
metric-complete norm 5.37e-17, as it must for a pure relabeling of the same span.
This is why the derivative frame must be specified before total assembly.

4. C: full-rank reconstruction passes; rank-deficient reconstruction is limited
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
For full column rank, C=(U^dagger U)^(-1) U^dagger B_L is correct. The compiled
routine reconstructs the direct C in the complex test to 7.42e-17.

For rank-deficient U, the Moore-Penrose result gives only the minimum-norm
solution of U C=B_L. If N has U N=0, B_L does not distinguish C from C+N.
The physical contraction Tr(C U') can distinguish them. Sigma need not preserve
the row space of U, so its exact C can contain such invisible components.

An additional test with rank(U_W)=1 and two correlated orbitals obtains

  B_L reconstruction error                   1.32e-16
  C error                                    0.0923441
  Functional finite difference               0.008082161684353
  Direct Matsubara/static-C contraction      0.008082161704585
  Pseudoinverse C, with correct rotation     0.009478056832620

Thus a small U C-B_L residual alone is not a complete C validation. This is a
conditional limitation, not evidence that it caused the previously tested FeSe
mismatch. The existing direct-C diagnostic is the right comparison. A general
production implementation should use direct C, or prove and monitor the null
component rather than silently discarding it.

5. Confirmed nonspherical geometry errors
----------------------------------------
The tests extract unchanged build_nsp_geometry, c_alpha_m and C_factor and link
the actual GAUNT/T3J routines. Independent references use numerical quadrature
on the unit sphere and direct finite differences of spherical harmonics.

5a. Wrong conjugation in the radial angular factor

stress_kernels.f90:784-785 uses conjg(c_alpha,t)*conjg(c_beta,u) with a Gaunt
ordering that requires c_alpha,t*c_beta,u. For bra Y_11, ket Y_1,-1, V=Y_20,

  integral Y_11^* n_x n_y Y_20 Y_1,-1
      reference  = -0.036044750314574 i,
      production = +0.036044750314570 i.

Diagonal components agree near 1e-14. The tested angular-derivative channels
away from the cutoff agree near 1e-11. This sign error affects components with
one Cartesian y index. It does not directly change the hydrostatic trace.
Do not replace conjugations indiscriminately: the spherical routine uses a
different Gaunt ordering, where these conjugations can be correct.

5b. Incorrect upper cutoff for an intermediate harmonic

stress_kernels.f90:803 rejects l+v>lmax2n in the angular derivative. Differentiating
Y_l needs the l+1 intermediate harmonic even when l is the largest radial basis
channel. This is an angular expansion, not a request for a new radial orbital.

With the actual production lmax2n=8, bra=ket=Y_80 and V=Y_20, the code gives

  sum_alpha angular_tensor(alpha,alpha) = 0.674918420750859,
  direct quadrature/derivative reference ~1.17e-10 (zero).

The trace must vanish identically: n dot grad_Omega=0. For m=1 the erroneous
trace is 0.646796819886240. This can contaminate hydrostatic TRACE mode, although
its size in a material depends on high-l amplitudes and is not established here.

5c. Non-short-circuit bounds assumption

The preceding-record duplicate check uses ih>1 .AND. label(ih-1). Fortran does
not guarantee short-circuit evaluation. With runtime bounds checking at -O0,
the first record accesses index zero of an array allocated starting at one.
The mathematical harness pads only that stub array at zero to continue the
independent geometry tests; no production subroutine body was changed. This is
a portability defect, not a claim that optimized production runs necessarily
crash. Use nested IF statements in a later patch.

6. G2 completeness: LO/APW and nonspherical channel coverage
----------------------------------------------------------
These are source-level mismatches with the full local matrices, not a demonstrated
numerical correction to a material's total stress. Contributions moved by a
basis-frame choice must be tracked together with their projector partners.

G2 line 1, spherical matching:
  val_corr_stress builds CAPD0/1 from plane-wave columns only
  (stress_kernels.f90:262-343). It does not use lomain_strain. Its matching
  expressions at 300-321 are LAPW formulas without an APW branch.
  The newer cmp_alm_blm_clm_strain does distinguish LAPW/APW, calls lomain_strain,
  and includes derivatives of all coefficient types. It converts D to the
  physical derivative s=D-gamma A/2 at alm_blm_clm.f90:517 onward.
  A common implementation must choose the required D or s deliberately.

G2 line 2, spherical radial:
  Many APW/LO cross terms are present. However, jr3upu_22(jlo,jlop,l,lp) is built
  as integral r^3 u_i u_j' (stress_integrals.f90:145) and consumed with
  conj(C_i) C_j (E_j-epsilon) (stress_kernels.f90:461-470).
  The note's S^r requires integral r^3 u_i' u_j. These are not interchangeable
  when the ket carries E_j. The symmetric overlap part alone can conceal this.

  Independent two-channel NREL free-radial example (both l=0; k=1.1 and 2.3;
  R=1.7) gives a bra-derivative contraction -0.625962286310399 and the current
  ket-derivative indexed contraction -2.193331336393645. Their overlap-only
  contractions agree. This tests the indexed radial algebra, not a complete
  LO-containing LAPW material. For a single identical LO channel the distinction
  vanishes; mixed linearization energies expose it.

G2 line 3, spherical angular:
  stress_kernels.f90:486-537 loops radial labels 0 and 1 only. It has no CLM
  primitive contributions. It is not the full all-channel S^a/H^a matrix.
  The exact angular trace vanishes, so these missing angular terms primarily
  concern deviatoric/shear channels rather than direct hydrostatic strain.

G2 line 4, nonspherical matching:
  The ordinary coefficients include LO bra channels, but the differentiated
  side uses CAPD0/1 only (stress_kernels.f90:613-643). It has the matching coverage
  limitation above. A bra-versus-ket presentation is harmless only after the
  complete Hermitian real contraction has been established.

G2 lines 5 and 6, nonspherical radial/angular:
  The runtime contractions include available LO matrices. Geometry errors are
  listed in section 5. A further input-channel issue is described below.

Nonspherical radial channels inherited from case.nsh:
  dftKS/atpar.f90:235-247 selects radial triples using the ordinary Hamiltonian
  triangle condition for <Y_l|Y_L|Y_l'>. build_nsp_geometry then generates more
  m combinations only for those retained radial triples.
  Strain inserts n_alpha n_beta or an angular derivative, changing allowed
  angular couplings. For example l=l'=0, V=Y_20 is forbidden in the ordinary
  Hamiltonian but

    integral |Y_00|^2 n_z^2 Y_20 dOmega = sqrt(5)/(15 sqrt(pi)) != 0.

  Its xx and yy partners are each minus one half, so its trace is zero.
  The radial strain term needs that triple even though the ordinary Hamiltonian
  does not. The test supplies this channel directly and verifies it; the normal
  .nsh radial list cannot supply it. Complete FULL mode therefore needs a
  stress-specific radial-channel list or equivalent integrals computed directly
  from the stored nonspherical potential. File-format compatibility must be
  preserved when implementing that change.

What passed in this area:
  dftKS/atpar.f90:333-346 implements W=integral r^3 u_bra' V_L u_ket with a
  single radial derivative, consistent with the note. It does not follow the
  suspected two-prime typo in the paper. The failure is not that whole W formula.

7. TRACE mode is an explicit approximation, not the full tensor
-------------------------------------------------------------
cmp_dmft_stress.f90:215-245 keeps the sum of nonspherical matching, radial and
angular terms, then replaces it by one-third of its trace times the identity
when stress_g2_mode='TRACE', the production default.

It preserves the calculated hydrostatic trace but omits nonspherical deviatoric
and shear contributions. Consequently a correct hydrostatic result would not
validate a general tensor. Conversely this deliberate trace projection by itself
does not explain a hydrostatic mismatch. FULL exposes the terms, but enabling
FULL before fixing their geometry and radial-channel coverage is not a solution.

8. Final sign: the direct projector branch is inconsistent by default
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The sign must be audited through the full output path, not inferred from a
variable name or a comment saying that stress has a plus derivative convention.

  l2main.f90:1089: stress_projector += raw_sign * [2 Re Tr(C U')]
  stress_symmetry.f90:633: STR_VAL contains +stress_projector/Omega
  WIEN SRC_mixer/scfana.f:513: Tstress=STR_VAL+core-electrostatic
  WIEN SRC_mixer/mixer.F:2034: final_stress=-Tstress

Thus a raw +D_proj becomes a physical -D_proj/Omega. The current default
raw_sign=+1 is inconsistent with the literal derivative in the note. raw_sign=-1
is required for this direct branch in that convention. The paired-assembly
opt-in already selects the corresponding WIEN sign; ordinary defaults do not.

This sign does NOT automatically carry over to response matrices. cmp_dmft_stress
already assembles their H and O contractions with the WIEN intermediate sign.
An additional reversal of those matrices can apply the sign twice. The
EDMFT_STRESS_NORM_RESPONSE_SIGN=PHYSICAL label is not a proof of correctness.
Sign tests should separately cover direct projector, fixed-window response,
normalization response, and final mixer output. Do not flip all terms together.

The old default is a known code path, not an explanation of the residual in a
run whose environment already enabled paired assembly or the WIEN sign. Exact
switch settings must be recorded with every material comparison.

9. No justified isolated volume/M_Sigma patch
------------------------------------------
The -gamma psi/2 part of Eq. (13), applied on both sides of a matrix integral,
cancels its whole-cell Jacobian. It must not be reintroduced as a standalone
Tr(M_Sigma) pressure correction.

For a general coefficient-frame change T at the central point,

  H'_frame = T^dagger epsilon + epsilon T,
  O'_frame = T^dagger + T,
  U'_frame = T^dagger U.

Using B_L=UC gives

  Re Tr[rho H'_frame-rhoE O'_frame] + 2 Re Tr[C U'_frame] = 0.

The independent complex-matrix test gives -0.0270178359972444 and
+0.0270178359972444, with sum 3.47e-17. This confirms the cancellation assumed
in the note. A proposed missing term must survive this identity; an unmatched
coefficient-response or overlap-moment term does not.

10. Finite projector at R_MT; frozen radial functions; RELA
---------------------------------------------------------
A nonzero projector at the boundary does not by itself prove a missing standalone
surface force/stress. The derivative of an integral over a moving sphere contains
both the integrand derivative and the Reynolds surface term. A pullback to local
coordinates can incorporate the same term implicitly. Radius, center, coordinate
map, and projector transport must be specified before comparing these forms.

With fixed R_MT and co-moving atom-centered coordinates, no additional radius
response is introduced just because the stored radial projector is finite there.
Changing R_MT requires a radius-response term. This audit did not establish a
missing standalone boundary term that can safely be added to the present total.

The ordinary projector radial-response array drix_mat remains zero by default
(p_project.f90:221); diagnostic paths can populate it. This preserves a frozen
radial/projector assumption. It does not establish its numerical adequacy for
self-consistently strained endpoints.

The scalar-relativistic kernel exists and uses additional radial/flux terms.
This turn did not independently validate every RELA matrix element or its
cancellation with core, electrostatic, XC, and field-response terms. Likewise
local-axis constraints and diagonal self-energy truncation have not been
revalidated. These remain explicitly open; NREL algebraic errors should be fixed
before assigning the remaining discrepancy to relativistic or radial physics.

11. What existing material dumps did, and did not, establish
----------------------------------------------------------
Read-only postprocessing used saved central projector gradients and +/- fixed-
potential operator dumps. No new self-consistency or impurity calculation ran.
The script changes only the conjugation in the contraction, not the saved states.
Its values are components, never complete stresses.

FeSe_RELA: 140 k points, minimum retained outside-window gap 0.004883 Ry;
no skipped pair. Old and corrected contractions agree on these saved data:
fixed-X contribution -1.164986 GPa and normalization-window +1.544891 GPa,
expressed as one-third of the hydrostatic derivative/volume. Hence the confirmed
complex-conjugation defect does not explain this saved FeSe hydrostatic mismatch.
The midpoint overlap/eigenpair errors are 3.18e-5 and 3.63e-5 Ry, respectively;
a smaller-strain test is still needed for precise finite-difference certification.

SrVO3 and MnO: attempted combinations of old dump directories give midpoint
overlap errors about 0.029 and 0.032. These are too large to treat the combinations
as clean quantitative references without checking their provenance/alignment.
MnO also has three excluded exact/tolerance-level boundary pairs and a remaining
minimum gap about 1.15e-8 Ry. Their raw contractions are kept in the machine-readable
record but are NOT accepted as corrected material stresses. No conclusion that
SrVO3/MnO improved or failed follows from these particular contractions.

This is another reason to establish gauge and local-matrix tests independently
of historical material differences.
