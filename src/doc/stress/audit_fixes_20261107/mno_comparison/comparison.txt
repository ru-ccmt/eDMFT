Calculation provenance
----------------------
User calculations were read, never overwritten. The central test is in
/private/tmp/edmft_mno_comparison_20261107. Its vector was regenerated with the
installed x_dmft.py lapw1 using the central saved potential and FIXED_NBANDS=23.
The plain central MnO.vector was an older 17-20-band file; the latest completed
MPI energy shards show 23 bands. Both endpoints already have 23-band vectors.

The test copies the endpoints' MnO.indmf2 and sig.inp1 so that the central
analytic calculation has their fixed chemical potential 6.609509262416999 eV
and their 601-row Matsubara mesh. The central diagnostic input had meanwhile
been changed to recomputeEF=1 and a 131-row mesh; it is not used here. Both
executables were run with exactly the same copied inputs. Charge, chemical
potential, SUM, and VSUM agree at printed precision between executables.
No impurity calculation or new charge self-consistency was performed.

The numerical value is obtained read-only from the existing +/- relaxed
calculations using frozen_sigma_klein.py with strain=0.001. Its reported
+(1/Omega)dK/depsilon=-217.042298004 GPa is the TRACE for a uniform strain of
all three lengths; dividing by three gives the mean above. Combined sig.inp
and Eorb_imp.dat are identical across the triplet.

Fresh analytic STR_VAL is combined with the copied central STR_CORE and
STR_DENS as sigma=-(STR_VAL+STR_CORE-STR_DENS). Cubic symmetry gives the same
value in all three diagonal components.

Consistency checks and remaining issues
--------------------------------------
All three current vectors have 72 identical k points, 23 bands at every k,
matching matrix dimensions and weights, and identical plane-wave integer G
sets. The final 18 reciprocal labels seed local orbitals; those can differ
and were excluded from the plane-wave set comparison. An initial full-record
hash comparison included them and is not a plane-wave-cutoff test.

The projector window is [6,16]. At Gamma bands 16,17,18 are degenerate at
approximately 1.79062694 Ry. The upper window boundary therefore cuts a
multiplet; similar boundary degeneracies occur at other k points. Current
response routines report skipped degenerate denominators. This limits the
interpretation of a derivative of that band-selected projector; it does not
prove that the whole observed discrepancy is a window effect.

The changed contributions show a large incomplete cancellation. In the RAW
WIEN valence convention (mixer subsequently changes the sign), their means are:
                              pre-audit          current
main valence correction         89.654471       -106.907628
normalization-window total       2.113132        110.610746
explicit projector              -5.341880          5.341880
The net change is about -77.380679 GPa raw, or +77.380679 GPa in the final
stress. These are diagnostic pieces of the total, not independent pressures.
The consistent assembly of the generalized G2 basis response, normalization
metric connection, and degenerate-window response needs further work.

The stored endpoint valence charges are 20.992718 and 21.008041; the new center
is 21.000532. This is another check for the fixed-mu reference. It is NOT a
justification to subtract mu*dN by hand: VSUM in l2main uses mu*ELECN, the fixed
target electron number, rather than mu times the integrated density. The
actual functional implementation must be followed when analyzing this issue.

The new ordinary nonspherical matrix agrees with .nsh at 3.30e-7 relative or
better. Both runs terminate normally. Historical IEEE flags still occur.
No source-code changes were made for this comparison.
