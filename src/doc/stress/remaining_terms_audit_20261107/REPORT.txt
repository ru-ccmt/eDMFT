REMAINING STRESS TERMS AUDIT - 7 November 2026

Main outcome
============
Three demonstrated defects were corrected and installed in local WIEN2k lapw0
and lcore. The RKMAX=8 SrVO3 eDMFT discrepancy remains unresolved. No dmft2 source
was changed in this audit. No converged material reference was changed.

Read remaining_terms_audit.pdf (and its LaTeX source) for derivations and exact
conventions. All GPa comparisons here are mean normal stress, trace/3, already
including the division by three. Pressure in the conventional sign is minus
this mean stress.

Installed fixes
===============
1. Physical angular momentum is abs(signed WIEN lattice-harmonic label) after
   the sine/cosine phase has been encoded in complex coefficients. Signed l
   incorrectly suppressed valid terms in lcore E3 and lapw0 electrostatics.
2. Full Cartesian, spin-resolved GGA gradient tensor is integrated on the
   existing XC angular grid. Cross-harmonic, tangential-gradient, and mixed-spin
   terms are retained. Default is full; WIEN_STRESS_GGA_MODE=LEGACY is diagnostic.
3. Removed REAL(real8) conversion in lapw0 final electrostatic assembly, which
   had rounded a large contribution to default single precision.

Tests

PASS electrostatic Cartesian angular fixture, density and potential separately
  and jointly nonspherical; max corrected error 1.68e-8 (old 0.0915291).

  channels; max error 2.56e-10. Rotation/spin equivalence errors ~7e-15.

  max error 1.15e-11.

  SrVO3 DFT/DMFT residual -0.001478/-0.001478 GPa;
  FeSe DFT/DMFT residual -0.000452/-0.000452 GPa.
  FeSe pre-audit residual was -0.065402/-0.057307 GPa.
PASS independent actual core radial differentiation: only ~0.00290 GPa shift
  for SrVO3 and ~0.00334 GPa for FeSe, essentially equal in DFT and eDMFT.
PASS serial builds, two-thread stress, MPI 4 ranks SrVO3 and 2 ranks FeSe.
PASS installed executables reproduce staged results in all four snapshots.
PASS ordinary potential and core-density outputs unchanged byte-for-byte at
  one thread. Two-thread Vns differences <=1e-13 Ry; stress unchanged in output.
PASS portable patch check/application/idempotence and site-compiler preservation.
PASS full LAPW0 spin integration: equal-spin representation reproduces the
  unpolarized tensor, and swapping 65:35 to 35:65 spin densities leaves the
  tensor invariant, both exactly at the printed 12-decimal precision.
