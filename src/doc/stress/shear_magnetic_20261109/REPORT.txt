IMPLEMENTATION
1. Tensor directions
   invariant_strains projects all six Cartesian symmetric tensors onto the
   point-group-invariant space, retaining a complete independent basis with
   hydrostatic strain first. Triclinic P1/P-1 retains six components; the tested
   monoclinic group retains four, including xz shear. Hexagonal MnTe has two.
   Cartesian rotations and physical volumes use NativeLattice/wlatgen, including
   primitive/conventional reciprocal-basis distinctions for centered cells.
   The same single screened adjoint serves every independent strain direction.
   Density/star layouts retain their native symmetry. This is a complete first
   derivative tensor within that imposed symmetry, not a general symmetry-
   breaking DFPT or elastic-constant implementation. A non-invariant self-energy
   is explicitly reported as a symmetry projection. Local frames and RMT stay
   fixed. Spin-polarized KS/core and spin-orbit inputs remain unsupported.

2. Detecting spin and physical ensemble
   lapw_response_policy.py detects a nonempty case.indmfldn. The two local
   self-energies/projector mappings remain independent; their densities need
   not be equal. Both channels share the unpolarized KS potential and core.
   Ferro=True in params.dat selects a two-spectrum fermif search when physical
   recomputeEF=1. The temporary recomputeEF=0 used for subsequent charge calls
   must NOT turn the response into a fixed-mu calculation. Preparation metadata
   records the physical policy separately. Ordinary compensated magnets retain
   the existing first-spin EF search followed by the second spin at that EF.
   Fixed-mu calculations reuse EF.dat in both channels without a number search.

   The response imposes only delta(N_up+N_down)=0 for fixed-N runs. The common-mu
   rank-one operation includes the overlap contribution Tr(rho O'). There is
   one combined Hartree/XC/core/normalization response and one adjoint.
   Native branch outputs each carry a factor two: (up+down)/2 is the physical
   spin sum, not an assumption that the spin populations match.

3. Execution and publication
   x_dmft.py dmft2 with total response enabled prepares the companion definition
   and split sigma, executes both channels, averages charge once via combineud,
   then prepares and solves one shared response. --execute-only preserves input
   generation behavior: companion definitions/sigma must already be prepared.
   run_dmft.py continues deferring response during ordinary iterations and
   performs the two-channel response measurement only at the final pass.
   Live inputs are preserved by disposable DEF/control files. Both channels
   are freshly exported in the isolated response workspace. All consumed spin
   files participate in stale-input fingerprint checks.
   SCF assembly publishes the complete tensor once, after lcore, using the
   existing total replacement logic. No mixer source change is needed.
   The exact-cutoff diagnostic cannot publish stress even if its solve converges.

WINDOW-CUT DEGENERACY
No bands or k points are added or discarded by the diagnostic. In the
cross-window derivative, only factors 1/(epsilon_in-epsilon_out) whose absolute
gap is at most the specified cutoff are replaced by zero. Regular pairs at
the same k point remain; the normalized-projector derivative, including X',
uses this same selected derivative. Central projectors/occupations are unchanged.
Strict production behavior still rejects an unresolved window cut.

At an exact split degeneracy the rank-fixed spectral projector is not generally
a unique differentiable function. Pair omission is a controlled diagnostic, not
an exact cure. The mathematically clean fixed-window remedy is an isolated
subspace containing the whole degenerate cluster throughout the nearby strain
path; a smooth/disentangled window is a different projector scheme. We have
not changed the user's fixed-band scheme or expanded only one isolated k point.

Previous hydrostatic cutoff tests (1e-10 to 1e-4 Ry) changed MnO by no resolved
amount and MnTe by 2.82e-7 GPa. Dropping whole affected-k window responses is
not equivalent: the isolated MnO window component changes about 8.03 GPa.
Those checks do not prove general-shear differentiability at the cut.

WHY CAN THIS MATCH FINITE DIFFERENCES BETTER THAN THE OLD DFT STRESS?
The distinction is finite-basis derivative consistency versus basis convergence.
The new engine differentiates the complete discrete scalar and its charge map,
including H/O, matching/radial functions, normalization and successful SELECT
energy searches. The older DFT path used frozen-augmentation approximations.
A finite LAPW Ritz solution is stationary in its coefficient space, but its
radial basis also depends on V_KS. Keeping every occupied state does not remove
changes of that basis outside the represented subspace. Such changes produce
  D_basis = 2 Re sum_kn w_k f_kn <d chi_kn | (H_KS-epsilon_kn) | psi_kn>,
where d chi_kn is the basis change at fixed coefficients. The physical-potential
terms cancel in variational DFT; the finite-basis remainder need not vanish.
Native quadrature/charge conventions must also be differentiated consistently.

Earlier independent DFT radial-only diagnostics, using the actual IN1-reference
paths, reduced the FeSe residual from .09584 to .00752 GPa and SrVO3 from .22366
to -.06011 GPa. See radial_basis_tests_20261108/REPORT.txt. They establish the
importance of basis response, not a complete decomposition of today's result.
Those older DFT controls used tetrahedron occupations. Finite-T Klein physics
is not an intrinsic advantage: DFT Mermin free energy E-TS is also stationary
in occupations, with the remaining occupation variation mu*dN=0 at fixed N.
A finite-T internal-energy derivative is a different observable.

A derivative can match finite differences accurately at RKMAX=7 while both
remain far from the complete-basis stress. Increasing RKMAX remains necessary
for physical convergence. A definitive comparison uses the same new engine
on converged Sigma=0 DFT and the same finite-difference free energy, basis,
occupation integration, and IN1-reference policy. Current searched-energy
completion holds the IN1 reference EF fixed; the older DFT triplets had moving
reference EF and cannot be used blindly for that test.
