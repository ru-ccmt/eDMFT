STRESS RESPONSE: WHERE WE ARE AND WHAT COMES NEXT
Current implementation roadmap, 8 November 2026

MAIN POINT

We have an existing analytic stress calculation, a slower reference response
calculation, and several tested components of a faster analytic response engine.
We have NOT yet connected the complete fast engine to a validated corrected
stress tensor. The latest patches advanced the potential-to-H/O/projector part
of that engine. The next task is spatial valence-density reconstruction,
including the change of the basis functions themselves.

"Installed" means the source/helper/library is available. It does not mean
that ordinary dmft2 calls that new component or that it changes reported stress.
This distinction is essential for reading the earlier progress reports.

THE THREE LEVELS THAT CURRENTLY COEXIST

1. Existing analytic stress
   The existing DFT/eDMFT stress calculation continues through ordinary
   dmft2, SCF assembly and mixer. The newly installed native response modules
   have not changed the production dmft2 executable or supplied a new total
   pressure comparison. Earlier analytic-versus-numeric discrepancies are
   not declared resolved by the recent component tests.

2. Slower reference response
   A working reference computes the frozen-self-energy response by evaluating
   perturbed native charge maps. Its steps use lapw0, lapw1, dmft2 and lcore.
   Screening is an iterative linear-response solve, not an impurity solve or
   two separately converged strained eDMFT calculations. Separate diagnostic
   contractions were used in the earlier material studies.

   The wrappers already schedule this reference calculation:
     x_dmft.py dmft2: requested response immediately after dmft2;
     run_dmft.py: requested response in one final measurement pass.

   STRT and STRFT are wrapper aliases for requesting it. The ordinary native
   executable receives STR or STRF through temporary inputs. Response work
   is isolated in case.response_runs/<run_id>/, with density/potential outputs
   recorded by case.stress_response.status.json.

   Crucially, the current wrapper returns stress_applied=false. It saves the
   screened density and potential; it does not add a complete corrected tensor
   to mixer. The supported reference scope is nonmagnetic, no SOC, primitive
   orthogonal cells, normalized projector 5, fixed band indices, fixed N and
   hydrostatic strain. Fixed-mu MnO and magnetic MnTe are not supported by that
   integrated driver yet, even though some lower-level routines support more.

3. Faster analytic response engine under construction
   This replaces repeated native program evaluations with prepared derivative
   operations and their transposes. It reuses central data and avoids repeated
   frequency work and, where the retained-state representation suffices,
   diagonalization. Its component tests are ahead of its production integration.
   The native reference remains our independent comparison.

STATUS OF THE BUILDING PLAN

A. Execution and file isolation: IMPLEMENTED
   Shared dmft2_step executor, STRT/STRFT handling, final-only run_dmft policy,
   separate response workspaces, input fingerprints and failure checks exist.
   Final publication of a general corrected stress tensor remains pending.

B. Matrix-only dftKS worker: IMPLEMENTED, OPTIONAL
   Native H and O can be assembled without diagonalization. Real/complex and
   serial/MPI checks passed. It remains a diagnostic/fallback building block;
   the integrated reference solver still uses its existing native actions.

C. Cached electronic response: IMPLEMENTED AND TESTED
   Given joint band perturbations (delta H, delta O, delta Phi), calculate the
   density-matrix response delta rho at fixed local Sigma and double counting.
   Phi means RAW local-orbital overlaps, before window selection/normalization.
   The normalized projector chain includes window motion, overlap symmetry,
   inverse-square-root normalization and the moving overlap metric.
   Forward and transpose actions are implemented. Fixed-N and fixed-mu
   electronic branches exist; this does not extend the wrapper's supported scope.
   Repeated Matsubara-dependent contractions can be cached in the central context.
   Accuracy remains subject to exported quadrature/tails and retained bands.

D. Radial and basis response: IMPLEMENTED FOR TESTED CHANNELS
   Native scalar NREL/RELA radial derivatives, normalization, the actual finite
   energy-difference construction of udot, ordinary LO coefficients and matching
   derivatives are implemented with forward and reverse actions.
   They are derivatives of the discrete algorithm, not frozen-radial formulas.
   Grid, RMT and local frames are held fixed in the present potential-response
   building blocks. Energy changes are explicit inputs; automatic energy-search
   differentiation/policy and DIRACOUT p1/2 remain unfinished.

E. H/O/raw-projector assembly: SUBSTANTIALLY IMPLEMENTED, MATERIAL WIRING PARTIAL
   Spherical kinetic/boundary H, overlap, native triangle projection,
   nonspherical potential integrals, ordinary-LO angular factors and raw
   projector contractions now have analytic actions and transposes.
   The most recent patch added the nonspherical/angular assembly and cached
   plane-wave sums. Full off-diagonal band matrices were tested in FeSe and SrVO3.
   A production adapter must still combine all pieces in a consistent material
   gauge, supply actual equivalent-site/orbital rotations and harmonic conventions,
   and compare the FULL normalized material projector. Interstitial potential
   assembly and complete physical strain sources must be included consistently.

F. Spatial valence-density response: NEXT MAJOR TASK
   General algebra, signed-density reference tools and charge-normalization
   derivatives exist. A complete fast native spatial-density action and its
   transpose, including direct basis-density terms, are not connected.

G. Hartree/XC and core response: NATIVE FAST OPERATIONS STILL REQUIRED
   The reference obtains these effects through native program evaluations.
   Efficient analytic forward/transpose operations matching those programs'
   discrete conventions must be implemented and connected.

H. Screening and single-adjoint solver: ALGEBRA IMPLEMENTED
   Paired-operator GMRES, transpose checks and one-adjoint/multiple-source
   contractions exist. Tests include material electronic kernels embedded in
   reduced synthetic spatial maps. They are not six physical material strains.
   The full native charge operator and full stress gradient are still missing.

I. Six physical strain sources and final tensor: NOT COMPLETED
   Hydrostatic reference sources and a hydrostatic density-transport derivative
   exist. General strain sources with fixed-RMT surface/transport terms, local
   frame policy, tensor symmetry and all contributions are not assembled.
   The final fast tensor must be validated and published once before SCF merge.
   Independent radial on/off/diagnostic report policy exists, but is not yet a
   complete automatic radial-correction path in the executor.

WHAT THE MOST RECENT TESTS ESTABLISH

79 installed-source Python tests, nine installed-helper tests and 32 new native
mixed-radial tests pass. The 32 new tests supplement earlier radial/Hamiltonian
controls; they are not 32 full stress calculations.

For a fixed-geometry, fixed-physical-H perturbation of the basis-generating
potential, the complete retained-band H' and O' matrices at the smallest tested
step agree with native finite differences as follows (relative matrix norms):

  FeSe RELA:    H' 8.18e-8; O' 5.26e-8.
  SrVO3 NREL:   H' 1.84e-8; O' 1.03e-8.

These checks include off-diagonal entries and ordinary LOs at the first k point.
Separate nonspherical-potential checks use three k points. The raw-projector
radial derivative shows approximately quadratic step convergence. Small native
cross-code residuals remain and are recorded. These are NOT new GPa results.

The new cached augmentation coefficient calculation is about 8--9 times faster
for the tested l=2 channel. Earlier cached electronic-response actions also have
large measured speedups. Neither measurement is an end-to-end stress speedup.

NEXT STEPS, WITH COMPLETION TESTS

1. Complete the physical valence-density map and its reverse.

   Distinguish rho_k (a band density matrix) from n(r) (the spatial density).
   Let Psi_k(r) be a row of band trial functions in the central coefficient
   representation. With fixed k weights and an explicit spin convention,

     n(r) = sum_k w_k Psi_k(r) rho_k Psi_k(r)^dagger,

     delta n(r) = sum_k w_k [Psi_k delta rho_k Psi_k^dagger
                          + delta Psi_k rho_k Psi_k^dagger
                          + Psi_k rho_k delta Psi_k^dagger].

   The first term comes from the electronic response. The other two come from
   the moving basis. The latest radial/matching work supplies ingredients for
   those terms but does not itself assemble the spatial density. In code the
   electronic response already includes spin/k weights: do not multiply again.

   Implement muffin-tin harmonic and interstitial reconstruction with native
   symmetry, multiplicity and integration conventions. Accumulate the reverse
   contributions before the shared radial reverse solve.

   Completion test: for an arbitrary small potential perturbation, compare
   analytic valence-density response to native one-step endpoints; verify
   integrated charge and the forward/transpose pairing. Check retained-band
   convergence explicitly. A missing complement contribution must be handled,
   not hidden by testing only another version of the same truncated formula.

2. Complete potential and core response and connect one full charge map.

   At fixed geometry the Hartree/XC derivative schematically gives
     delta V_KS = K_Hxc delta n.
   For GGA, K_Hxc includes density-gradient dependence and the actual discrete
   interpolation/integration conventions. Add core density response. Keep
   automatic linearization energies and fixed-N versus fixed-mu policy explicit.
   Use native contexts in memory; repeated files/program launches should remain
   a reference mechanism rather than the inner operation of the fast solver.

   Completion test: compare the complete analytic one-step charge-map derivative
   and its transpose with the native reference on FeSe and SrVO3. Include number
   conservation, gauge shifts and band/radial/angular-cutoff convergence.

3. Solve and validate screened response with the complete native map.

   Let J be the derivative of one UNMIXED physical output-charge update with
   respect to input charge, at fixed local self-energy. It is not the mixer
   algorithm's Jacobian and not a full impurity-vertex DMFT susceptibility.
   For strain component nu, let q_nu be induced charge and b_nu the explicit
   strain source. Then

     (I-J) q_nu = b_nu.

   The software for this linear solve exists; the full physical J is what
   steps 1--2 provide. Start with hydrostatic FeSe and SrVO3 against the current
   reference delta n and delta V_KS. Record actual residuals and timings.

4. Complete the stress functional contraction and six strain sources.

   In a consistent decomposition write the additional energy derivative as
     Delta D_nu = d_nu + g^T q_nu.
   Here d_nu is the explicit correction, and g is the common charge-response
   gradient of the chosen correction functional after removing terms already
   included in analytic stress. Derive and validate the complete g, including
   radial, overlap and normalized-projector dependence. The window/normalization
   part has already been tested; the full material gradient has not.

   Then solve once
     (I-J^T) lambda = g,
   and obtain each component from
     Delta D_nu = d_nu + lambda^T b_nu.

   The six-source algebra is implemented. The six PHYSICAL b_nu, complete g,
   direct terms and their shared conventions still need to be assembled.
   For engineering strain (exx,eyy,ezz,2eyz,2exz,2exy), Delta D_nu/Omega gives
   the tensile stress correction. Pressure is minus the mean normal stress.

   Completion tests: actual forward versus adjoint contractions; zero-Sigma DFT
   limit; fixed-RMT boundary terms; normalized-projector symmetry; trace/pressure
   and shear factors; central finite differences of the matching frozen-Sigma
   functional; step, basis and band convergence. No fitting terms to pressures.

5. Finish production integration, then broaden material scope.

   Replace the reference action with the validated fast engine inside the shared
   executor. Preserve STRT/STRFT controls and final-only run_dmft scheduling.
   Assemble the complete validated tensor, prevent duplicate/stale corrections,
   and add it before lcore/SCF assembly/mixer without changing the mixer format.
   Preserve a separately selectable radial correction and component diagnostics.

   First establish the complete path in nonmagnetic SrVO3 and FeSe, NREL/RELA.
   Then add/validate fixed-mu insulating MnO and magnetic two-spin MnTe, followed
   by broader cell, symmetry and SOC coverage. Measure total wall time and MPI/
   memory behavior only on the connected workflow before claiming overall speedup.

THE IMMEDIATE DELIVERABLE

One tested native operation:
  input: a small change of the KS potential;
  output: the complete spatial VALENCE charge derivative, including changes of
          both the density matrix and basis;
  reverse: its discrete transpose for the later adjoint solve.

This is the next code milestone. Screening, the complete stress gradient and
production tensor publication follow after this map and potential/core response
pass their independent checks. This roadmap does not make source changes.
