EQUATIONS AND WHY THE COMPACT REPRESENTATION IS SUFFICIENT

R contains all retained KS states, including the full DMFT window W and all
appreciably occupied states. Q completes the finite LAPW space. Its size
may depend on k. The existing complement solver checks that Q is empty,
separated from R, and consistent with native H/O and the retained vectors.
The density and its derivative have no QQ block under those checked bounds:
  rho0 = [[rho_RR, 0], [0, 0]],
  d rho = [[d rho_RR, d rho_QR^dagger], [d rho_QR, 0]].
The same RR/QR blocks suffice for H/O input. The physical real pairing is
  <Y,dH> = Re Tr(Y_RR^dagger dH_RR) + 2 Re Tr(Y_QR^dagger dH_QR).
The factor two includes the RQ partner; it is not a spin or k weight.
No such factor is applied to the raw orbital overlaps.

The unchanged electronic map uses
  H_Q T - T epsilon_R = -(dH_QR - dO_QR epsilon_R),
  d phi_R,eff = d phi_R + T^dagger phi_Q,
  d rho_QR = T rho_RR.
There is ONE electronic model for all k points. Global overlap symmetry,
X derivatives and the fixed-N chemical-potential response are shared, never
computed independently for each k point. Native spin/k weights are applied
once by E and are not reapplied by the spatial or potential adapters.

For MT matching matrices Z_R,Z_Q, the fixed-basis density coefficient is
  dD = Z_R d rho_RR Z_R^dagger
       + Z_Q d rho_QR Z_R^dagger + Hermitian conjugate of this cross term.
The k/site sum is performed before the radial/angular contraction.
For the interstitial, transform only Nr columns:
  d n(r) = Re sum_j [FFT(C_R d rho_RR + 2 C_Q d rho_QR)]_j
                         [FFT(C_R)]_j^* / Omega.
Native Fourier-star conventions are then applied. Direct moving-basis
density D uses only the retained central density. Radial contexts are
shared per atom/l, not prepared again per k point.

The complete one-step unmixed charge derivative tested here is
  J = N [R E B + D + C] K_Hxc.
N is evaluated at raw TOTAL OUTPUT density, including core. The physical
screened equation (I-J) q_A=b_A is the next point, and was not solved here.

SCOPE AND NEXT WORK

Native snapshot support is currently nonmagnetic, no SOC, primitive
orthogonal cells, normalized projector 5, fixed radial energies/RMT/axes,
ordinary l<=3 LOs and native density channels l<=8. A common retained band
count is required, but total LAPW dimensions are allowed to vary with k.
Outside-MT projector tails, shifted/multisite impurity orbitals and higher-l
LOs are explicitly rejected by this adapter. Native data exports remain an
explicit prerequisite, not an automatic addition to ordinary dmft2.
