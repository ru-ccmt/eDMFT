SCIENTIFIC FINDING THAT CHANGES THE PLAN
The old alternate-basis WIEN hook is not a complete fixed-operator reference.
It changes radial functions through the wavefunction cutoff but restores the
physical spherical potential through the HNS path only for l,l'<=LNSMAX=4.
Higher-l channels change without the corresponding operator restoration.
This is a diagnostic-hook limitation, not evidence of an ordinary WIEN bug.

Direct matrix exports reproduce the earlier reconstructed-matrix results.
Their finite-step discrepancy was therefore not principally caused by matrix
reconstruction. Truly symmetric V0 +/- h*dV inputs improve convergence:

                matrix minus eigenvalue derivative, GPa
                h=1e-5              h=5e-6
  SrVO3         -1.7808977e-5        -4.4854292e-6
  FeSe          +4.2415043e-5        +1.0453337e-5

These are mean tensile stresses, already divided by three. This agreement
checks one eigenproblem, not whether its physical operator was properly fixed.

Restricting the radial change to l<=4, where operator restoration exists,
gives the following fixed-IN1-reference DFT eigenvalue directional derivatives:

                old all-l probe     restricted l<=4       old contraction
  SrVO3         +0.008172793         +0.107569043          +0.107648437
  FeSe          -0.073676043         -0.041112497          -0.007952897

The two new columns use symmetric h=5e-6. The saved old contraction uses h=1e-5
and the earlier response path, so this is not an exact matched-path closure.
The restricted probe agrees with its native H/O derivative within 4.5e-6 and
1.1e-5 GPa, respectively. No new strained total-energy SCFs were performed.

Thus the earlier claim that the SrVO3 radial contraction was wrong was too
strong. The reference probe had a substantial uncovered angular contribution.
FeSe remains unresolved. The historical DFT/eDMFT radial-corrected totals must
remain provisional until all-angular operator restoration and RELA conventions
are checked. The remaining eDMFT stress mismatch is NOT solved.
