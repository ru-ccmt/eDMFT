1. WHAT THIS ADDS

The fast valence map previously accepted a spherical muffin-tin potential
perturbation. It now also accepts nonspherical muffin-tin and interstitial
potential perturbations, separately or together. All three feed the same
normalized-projector electronic response, including the empty complement.
The action and its discrete transpose are implemented and tested.

This completes the general native-input substep of the valence-response work.
It does NOT complete the native Hartree/XC or core response (roadmap step 2),
or the full screened response validation (step 3). The reusable material loader,
independent spatial charge/gauge checks and fixed-N material endpoint checks
also remain. No new pressure or analytic-versus-numeric stress result is claimed.

The implementation also speeds up the existing spherical assembly by choosing
efficient matrix contraction order. Its equations and physical approximations
are unchanged. The new nonspherical/interstitial operations directly assemble
only retained-retained and complement-retained blocks, avoiding unused QQ work.
The spherical operation still builds full matrices before extracting those
blocks; optimizing that is a separate task.

2. COORDINATES AND THE COUPLED MAP

All equations here are at fixed geometry, sphere radii, k weights, local axes,
radial linearization energies and LO energies. Energies are in Ry. Sigma, double
counting and temperature remain fixed. Native material tests keep mu fixed.

Write the input vector as x=(s,v_NS,v_I), where

  s_a(r)       = delta[r V_spherical,a(r)],
  v_NS,a,L(r)  = delta V_a,L(r), in native real-harmonic coordinates,
  v_I,j        = delta V_j from the Fourier section of case.vns.

a labels an inequivalent atom, L=(l,m) a native real harmonic, and j a native
reciprocal-star representative. v_NS has no extra factor r. The interstitial
coefficients have ALREADY undergone native reanalysis of theta_I V, with
theta_I the interstitial indicator. They are not unmasked Fourier coefficients
of V. No additional sphere mask, star-size factor or cell-volume factor belongs
in this band assembly. Conversion from a physical potential to these exact
coordinates belongs to the upstream potential operator.

C_R are retained central generalized KS eigenvectors; C_Q spans their complete
O-orthogonal coefficient-space complement, constructed from central H/O. Q is
not a set of stored high-energy eigenvectors. R contains the entire DMFT window
and all significantly occupied states. The existing gap/thermal checks reject
an invalid complement.

The new prepared potential operation B returns

  B x = (h_RR, o_RR, Phi'_R, h_QR, o_QR),
  h_AB = C_A^dagger delta H C_B,
  o_AB = C_A^dagger delta O C_B.                           (1)

Phi is the RAW correlated-orbital overlap, before window normalization. The
electronic operation E, rather than B, applies window motion, overlap symmetry
and normalization. These operations must not be applied a second time in B.

Only s changes the radial and matching basis in this fixed-geometry policy.
For explicit nonspherical/interstitial potential inputs at fixed s,

  delta O = 0,          Phi'_R,explicit = 0.               (2)

Equation (2) does NOT mean that the normalized projector stays fixed: H changes
the KS eigenvectors and selected subspace, and E includes that change.

Let R_density reconstruct spatial density from band-density blocks; D_s is the
direct basis-density derivative at fixed band density, and S_s selects s from x.
The complete valence operation in this checkpoint is

  delta n = A x,
  A = R_density E B + D_s S_s.                             (3)

Its discrete transpose is

  A^T = B^T E^T R_density^T + S_s^T D_s^T.                 (4)

All adjoints use explicit real coordinate pairings, including both QR/RQ legs
and real/imaginary Fourier components. They are not formed by probing every
density coefficient separately.

3. EXPLICIT NONSPHERICAL MUFFIN-TIN POTENTIAL

For a fixed atom, let phi_l,i,c(r) be radial primitive i in angular channel l
and native radial component c. The supplied native quadrature/metric is w_rc.
Let G^(L)_(lm,l'm') be the real-harmonic Gaunt coefficient, in the same signed-l
convention as the native code. The small angular/radial matrix is

  delta K_(lmi,l'm'j)
    = sum_(L,r,c) G^(L)_(lm,l'm') phi_l,i,c(r)
                    w_rc v_NS,L(r) phi_l',j,c(r).         (5)

For each equivalent site and k point, Z maps coefficient-space trial states to
these augmented radial/angular coefficients. The explicit potential term is

  delta H_NS,RR = Z_R^dagger delta K Z_R,
  delta H_NS,QR = Z_Q^dagger delta K Z_R.                  (6)

The response of the CENTRAL nonspherical potential through changing phi and Z
already belongs to the spherical-input operation. Equation (6) adds only its
explicit delta V leg, so no radial/matching terms are counted twice. The new
joint adapter rejects a nonspherical (0,0) harmonic, already supplied by s.

For Hermitian Y_RR and rectangular Y_QR, the full Hermitian pairing is

  Re Tr(Y_RR^dagger delta H_RR)
      + 2 Re Tr(Y_QR^dagger delta H_QR).                   (7)

The small-kernel covector sums over sites/k before radial contractions:

  K_bar = sum [Z_R Y_RR Z_R^dagger
                + Z_Q Y_QR Z_R^dagger
                + Z_R Y_QR^dagger Z_Q^dagger].            (8)

Reverse (5) using K_bar to obtain v_NS,bar. The factor two in (7) is supplied
by the two cross terms in (8); it must not be applied again.

4. INTERSTITIAL FOURIER CONVENTION: BUG FOUND IN THE NEW STAGED MAP

For representative g_j and distinct star member G=R g_j, native STERN/SETWAR
form a mean over operations mapping g_j to that member:

  p_G = mean_R exp(-2 pi i g_j dot tau_R),
  W(G) = v_I,j p_G.                                      (9)

Repeated operations are averaged, not summed. The real executable uses the
corresponding cosine factor. These conventions were checked against STERN,
SETWAR and the native potential reader, not inferred from the density stars.

An essential convention appears at vector export: complex TAPEWF writes
CONJG(Z), where Z is the solver eigenvector. For the coefficients actually read
from case.vector, the upper Hamiltonian matrix is therefore

  delta H_I,ij = conjugate[ W(G_i-G_j) ],   i <= j,       (10)

with real diagonal and Hermitian copying to the lower triangle. Only PW rows
occur: ordinary LOs vanish in the interstitial. Thus

  delta H_I,RR = C_PW,R^dagger delta H_I C_PW,R,
  delta H_I,QR = C_PW,Q^dagger delta H_I C_PW,R.          (11)

The first staged version omitted the final conjugation in (10). FeSe's real
potential did not expose it; the complex SrVO3 calculation did. Its interstitial
band-H derivative differed by about 9.4%, and the resulting density derivative
by 9.2e-4 relative. Native coefficient-space comparison and source inspection
identified the cause. The corrected PW coefficient matrix matches native finite
differences to 1.53e-9 relative in the separate sign diagnostic.

Reversing G also fixes physical Hermitian fields, but the implemented operation
conjugates W explicitly. That reproduces native upper-triangle behavior even
for redundant coordinate directions used in adjoint checks. The reverse map
also conjugates its upper-matrix covector. A dedicated complex regression test
distinguishes the solver and exported-vector conventions.

Native source evidence:
  wien2k_24/SRC_lapw1/hamilt.F: WARPIN(G_row-G_column), HPANEL packing;
  wien2k_24/SRC_lapw1/tapewf.F: CONJG(Z) on export;
  wien2k_24/SRC_lapw1/calkpt.F: DumpAssembledMatrices conjugates packed H/O;
  eDMFT_new/src/dftKS/tapewf.FPP: same complex-vector convention.

This was an error in the NEW, uninstalled response module. It does not establish
a corresponding bug in the existing production dmft2 stress calculation.

5. THE EMPTY COMPLEMENT STILL FEEDS PROJECTOR NORMALIZATION

The retained-plus-complement electronic operation is unchanged. For retained
KS energies e_R and projected central H_Q, it solves

  H_Q T - T e_R = -(h_QR-o_QR e_R),
  Phi'_R,eff = Phi'_R + T^dagger Phi_Q,
  rho'_QR = T rho_R,
  rho'_RR = E_R(h_RR,o_RR,Phi'_R,eff).                    (12)

The thermal-empty assumption makes rho'_QQ negligible within the explicit
checks. E_R contains the complete normalized-window electronic derivative.
Hence the new nonspherical/interstitial inputs change the projector even when
their explicit raw derivative in (2) is zero. No extra Matsubara sum over Q is
introduced. The cached static direct solve and its transpose are reused.

6. VALIDATION

108 distinct tests pass with staged modules and repository dependencies, with
the native radial library explicitly loaded (no skipped native tests). The
same 108 also pass with staged modules and dbin dependencies. This is two import
environments, not 216 distinct tests. Six new tests cover:
  native star/triangle assembly and full/rectangular transposes;
  the complex exported-vector convention;
  duplicate symmetry-operation averaging/systematic absence;
  nonspherical rectangular action and transpose;
  explicit input coordinate round trip and reality constraints;
  ambiguous-star and duplicate-PW rejection.

Thirty-two independent native endpoints were run in scratch. Each endpoint runs
native LAPW1 and dmft2_response with fresh projector normalization. Geometry,
PW labels, Sigma, mu and linearization energies are held fixed. No user material
directory is modified. FeSe uses RELA, real vectors, 66 retained/509 total basis
states; SrVO3 uses NREL, complex vectors, 43 retained/703 total basis states.
Both fixtures have RKMAX=8 and one k point.

Perturbations, with scalar amplitude h:
  spherical:     delta(r V) = 0.07 r sin(1.3 r);
  nonspherical:  delta V_L = 0.1 V_L, all native nonspherical channels;
  interstitial:  delta V_j = 0.1 V_j, both real/imaginary components;
  joint:         all three directions together.

Native density differences are [n(+h)-n(-h)]/(2h). Relative errors use the
Euclidean norm of the explicitly packed spatial-density coefficients; they are
not real-space weighted norms or pressure errors.

                              FeSe RELA             SrVO3 NREL
  nonspherical, h=0.01        1.33007e-6            1.70595e-7
  nonspherical, h=0.005       1.34353e-6            3.11600e-7
  nonspherical, h=0.001       1.96469e-6            1.54636e-6
  nonspherical, h=0.0005      3.11421e-6            2.83859e-6
  interstitial, h=0.001       3.87526e-7            3.25952e-7
  interstitial, h=0.0005      8.02543e-7            1.24687e-7

  central density            9.50e-13              1.63e-12

The joint SrVO3 error decreases approximately quadratically with h. Very small
isolated nonspherical steps amplify finite endpoint/output precision. Increasing
h reduces this noise but leaves a FeSe nonspherical residual near 1.3e-6. Its
H-matrix residual is about 2.88e-6; SrVO3's is about 5.45e-7 in the complete
RR/QR norm. The earlier nonspherical checkpoint also recorded small native/
assembled differences. These are not fully attributed or claimed to vanish.
They should be revisited if they materially limit the complete screened result.

H/O are differenced in their shared coefficient gauge BEFORE projecting with
the central LAPW coefficients, avoiding cancellation from subtracting two large
separately projected matrices. Even then, large coefficient norms can amplify
native/file precision. Complete raw step results are in the JSON files.

7. PERFORMANCE, SCOPE AND NEXT WORK

The benchmark compares the same complete spherical-potential-to-valence-density
map, same central data, same direction and same retained/complement space. It
runs baseline and optimized versions serially, one thread, and reports median
of three warm actions/reverse actions. The optimization changes contraction
order in PreparedSphericalBand, allowing BLAS to replace scalar multi-index
loops. Both versions agree with the previous full-spectral reference near 1e-13.
Detailed timing numbers are in TIMINGS.txt and *_benchmark_*.json.

These timings exclude native central H/O generation, Hartree/XC/core, screening
iterations and final stress contraction. They do not establish an end-to-end
stress speedup. General nonorthogonal cells, SOC, magnetic spin assembly,
automatic energy-search response, multiple-k MPI production loading and all six
strain sources are not validated by these material probes.

Immediate next work after approval/application:
  1. Finish reusable native material loading and independent spatial charge,
     gauge and fixed-N endpoint checks. Preserve all native potential units,
     Fourier reanalysis and real-harmonic conventions established here.
  2. Implement/connect native Hartree/GGA-XC and core forward/transposes so the
     actual unmixed charge-map derivative J is available. The slow reference
     already obtains these effects through native program evaluations.
  3. Validate (I-J) q=b and the single-adjoint solve against that reference.
     The generic linear solver exists; the physical J is what is incomplete.
  4. Complete the stress-functional gradient, six strain sources and final
     tensor publication. Existing STRT/STRFT scheduling remains unchanged.
