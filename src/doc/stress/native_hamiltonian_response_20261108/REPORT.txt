NATIVE RADIAL HAMILTONIAN AND RESPONSE COMPOSITION
Implementation checkpoint, 8 November 2026

STATUS AND SCOPE

This continues the approved response-engine plan. It adds the native scalar
radial Hamiltonian derivative and transpose, shared radial-component interfaces,
a native-order angular/band projection adapter, and the complete algebraic
charge-response composition. The routines are integrated into libatpar and the
Python response modules. They are NOT a complete production native-response
backend and do not yet change reported material stresses.

The finite-difference WIEN response remains the reference. No material inputs,
SCF files, installed dmft2 binary, or production WIEN source were changed by
this checkpoint. Test executables and the restricted WIEN probe are isolated
under /private/tmp/edmft_native_operator_20261108.

WHAT WAS IMPLEMENTED

1. Shared radial representation
  phi=(u,udot,u_LO1,...) contains large/small components and their derivatives.
  The complete radial basis is f=phi M. M contains matching and ABC factors.
  lapw_radial_response now exposes M, the tangent (dphi,dM), and its transpose.
  Existing basis/overlap/projector APIs reuse those interfaces.

2. Actual native radial H/O algebra
  S_ij=<phi_i|phi_j> uses the implemented RINT13 metric. Native identities
  S_11=1 and S_12=S_21=0 are held exact as in HAMILTON. Let
    T=diag(E,E,E_LO1,...)+e1 e2^T,
    Delta_ij=<phi_i|Vphysical-Vbasis|phi_j>,
    p_i=f_i(R), d_i=f_i'(R).
  Then
    O=M^T S M,
    H_volume=M^T [(S T+T^T S)/2+Delta] M,
    H_surface=R^2 (p d^T+d p^T)/2.
  Native HAMILTON sets complete-LO p_i=0 algebraically while retaining d_i.
  The two PW cross orientations also require
    H_order,ij=(W_R-1)(M_1i M_2j-M_2i M_1j)/2,
    W_R=R^2[u'(R) udot(R)-u(R) udot'(R)].
  W_R need not equal one for the implemented finite energy stencil and RELA
  conventions. Early symmetrization would differentiate a different algorithm.
  This is an observation about the native algorithm, not a claim that it is
  the source of the outstanding physical stress discrepancy.

  All input energies are Ry; radial potential input is r*V in Ry. Grid and
  RMT are fixed. For physical variation Vbasis=Vphysical, Delta stays zero.
  For a basis-only variation at fixed physical H, dDelta comes from -dVbasis.
  Tests vary both independently. Energy search is not differentiated here;
  dE and dE_LO are explicit caller inputs. DIRACOUT p1/2 remains unsupported.

3. Efficient forward/reverse reuse
  Radial integrals are accumulated in primitive space, before expanding into
  PW factors. This changes radial integration work from O(Nrad*Npw^2) to
  O(Nrad*Nprimitive^2), followed by small-to-large matrix contractions.
  The component APIs allow one radial tangent per atom/l to feed H/O,
  projector, and density assembly. Their covectors can be accumulated before
  one radial reverse solve. No complete response-speedup is claimed yet.

4. Native triangle, angular factors and central band basis
  For real radial block R and complex native angular/phase factors A,
    B(R)=C^dagger F(A*R) C,
  where * denotes elementwise multiplication. F copies the upper triangle
  into the Hermitian lower triangle with a real diagonal. C contains the
  corresponding rows of CENTRAL KS eigenvectors in native order. No band
  occupation/spin/k weight belongs in this map. The new adapter supplies its
  exact transpose, including the factor of two from mirrored off-diagonals.
  The caller must supply correct sector factors and ordered coefficient rows.
  This does not yet generate all nonspherical/LO angular sectors automatically.

5. Charge-response composition
  The new ChargeResponseChain assembles
    J=N [R E B + D + C] K.
  K is Hartree/XC charge-to-potential response; B maps potential into joint
  (dH,dO,dphi_raw); E is the cached moving-metric frozen-Sigma electronic
  response; R reconstructs valence charge with fixed central basis; D is
  direct basis-density response at fixed central density matrix; C is core
  density response; N is output-charge normalization. Every physical adapter
  must supply an action AND transpose. Missing maps are not replaced by zero.

  E already includes the production spin and k weights. R must not add them
  again. R E B alone is not a physical density derivative for a moving basis:
    d[Basis rho Basis^dagger]
      = dBasis rho0 Basis^dagger + Basis drho Basis^dagger
        + Basis rho0 dBasis^dagger.
  D supplies the first and last terms. The existing adjoint solver then solves
    (I-J^T) lambda=g,
  so six contractions are d_nu+lambda^T b_nu, once physical sources b_nu and
  the complete stress gradient g are supplied. This checkpoint tests the
  composition, not the missing physical six-strain sources.

VALIDATION

A. 32 prior native radial-basis controls pass after refactoring.
B. 32 new native H/O controls pass, with independent ATPARN/ABC endpoints
   and literal HAMILTON coefficients. They cover NREL/RELA, LAPW/APW+lo,
   ordinary LOs, material potentials, energy variations and potential changes.
   Largest relative errors:
     central H/O agreement        2.12e-11
     H central finite difference  4.65e-8
     O central finite difference  4.94e-7
     adjoint pairing              3.97e-13
C. Existing Python regression: 62 tests pass. Eight new controls pass:
   native triangle/transposes and gauge covariance; generalized coordinates;
   independent nonlinear Galerkin density endpoints with moving complex basis;
   fixed-N/fixed-mu transpose and number conservation; screened solve and
   one-adjoint/six-source contractions; explicit source composition; dimension
   checks. The Galerkin controls are NOT material stress calculations.
D. Isolated full WIEN matrix comparison, atom 1, l=5 only, fixed physical H,
   three k points each, h=0.002, 0.001, 0.0005:
     FeSe RELA: maximum projected H difference about 1.06e-10 Ry,
                projected O about 2.71e-11.
     SrVO3 NREL (complex): projected H about 9.20e-11 Ry,
                          projected O about 1.71e-12.
   Full coefficient-space relative residuals are ~1.15e-5 H / 3.55e-6 O in
   FeSe and ~2.61e-6 H / 4.98e-6 O in SrVO3. They have not converged cleanly
   with step halving. The small cross-code residual must be understood; these
   are close limited-channel comparisons, not proof of all-channel closure.
   The hook changes only atom1,l5 and disables low-l compensation. It uses
   unchanged physical SELECT energies. Nonspherical low-l and LO responses
   are deliberately absent from this material probe.

NEXT STEPS, IN ORDER

1. Finish all-sector native matrix assembly: nonspherical potential, LO angular
   factors, and raw projectors in a common central coefficient gauge. Verify
   coordinated H/O/projector responses, including the residual above.
2. Add native spatial valence density AND direct basis-density forward/reverse
   maps; connect core and Hartree/XC. Keep energy-search policy explicit.
3. Validate J and J^T against the full reference charge map and its conserved
   charge. Test actual material screening and the common stress gradient.
4. Build the six physical strain sources and compare forward and one-adjoint
   tensor results, including radial on/off and stored-band convergence.
5. Enable the fast backend in the shared dmft2_step executor only after these
   checks. Publish and apply a validated tensor idempotently before SCF merge.
   Preserve run_dmft's final-iteration-only scheduling and scratch isolation.

The existing wrapper still does not publish this as an applied total stress;
its current result remains stress_applied=false. The outstanding eDMFT stress
mismatch has not been resolved by this checkpoint. The next task is full
native angular/density assembly, not another fit to the total pressure.
