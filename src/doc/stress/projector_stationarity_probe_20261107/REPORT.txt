FIXED-GEOMETRY POTENTIAL STATIONARITY PROBE, SrVO3
7 November 2026

Question
========
Does convergence of the ordinary charge loop guarantee stationarity of the
functional when the physical correlated orbitals are constructed by a finite
KS band-window projection and subsequent matrix normalization?

For density-independent physical orbitals the answer should be yes, subject
to the other stationarity equations and numerical/basis convergence. For
Phi_W[V]=Pi_W[V] phi X[V], fixed disk radial phi is not sufficient to make the
physical projector independent of V. Window motion and normalization can
produce an extra potential gradient. A change of KS representation alone
does not do this; its connection terms cancel.

At fixed geometry and fixed basis, with V=V_Hxc,

  Gamma[n,V] = F_sp[H0+V,Sigma_tilde,U[V]]
                  +E_Hxc[n]-integral V n + constant

is the frozen-effective-self-energy tangent functional. Its independent
variations give

  delta_n Gamma = integral (V_Hxc[n]-V) delta n,
  delta_V Gamma = integral (n_G-n) delta V
                  + Re sum_k Tr[(R_k+R_k^N) delta V_k].

R and R^N are the already k/spin-weighted Hermitian response matrices for
the moving fixed-index window and its normalization. Ordinary charge
iteration imposes n=n_G, but does not explicitly include this second term
in its density update. Source inspected: l2main.f90 builds Aweight, computes
charge through cmp_MT_density/cmp_interstitial, and uses rhoResponse and
the normalization-window matrices only for force/stress contractions.

The latest retained central SCF files have charge distance 5e-7 for SrVO3
and 8e-7 for FeSe RELA. Those numbers do not measure the additional functional
gradient, and cannot by themselves certify pressure accuracy. The earlier
independent finite-model audit found a nonzero potential gradient even with
exact ordinary charge consistency; it vanished for the complete window.

The test perturbation is local and preserves the reference space group:

  V(t,r)=V(0,r)+t Ry * Theta_I(r),

where Theta_I is one in the interstitial and zero in every muffin-tin.
Geometry, radial LAPW basis, local orbital functions, self-energy, and mu
are held fixed. The step discontinuity is an allowed bounded test potential;
it is not claimed to be the actual induced strain potential.

Its exact matrix in the stored central KS space is

  M_k = A_k^dagger O_I A_k,

where the plane-wave interstitial overlap O_I is the full-cell identity
minus analytic spherical Fourier integrals over all MT spheres. LO functions
have no interstitial support. Exact reciprocal lengths from the cubic struct
were used instead of rounded BR1 printed in output1. The eigenvalues of M_k
range from 0.00348494 to 0.89019047, consistent with 0<=Theta_I<=1.

75 irreducible k points, 43 stored bands, DMFT window [12,31]. Minimum
inside/outside-window energy separation was 0.05641984 Ry; no denominator
regularization or discarded pair was needed.

Result
======
The computed projector contribution to dGamma/dt is:

  Fixed-X window term       0.0208499861615462 Ry
  Normalization term        0.0003559494879926 Ry
  Sum                       0.0212059356495388 Ry

A spatially uniform potential shift gives zero projector contribution,
as required: it changes eigenvalues but not the selected spectral subspace.
Reconstructed fixed-X response matrices match the production binary dumps
to 1.1e-19 maximum elementwise error.

Independent endpoint diagonalizations of diag(epsilon_k)+/-h M_k were used
to form Pi_W(+/-h) acting on the fixed raw/normalized orbital coefficients
in the original central Hilbert space. This avoids phase matching and
arbitrary rotations inside degenerate occupied/window blocks. Their finite
derivatives were contracted with the central functional gradients, including
the exact normalization-symmetry adjoint exported by dmft2:

  h=1e-3       0.0212059381031509 Ry
  h=3e-4       0.0212059358701112 Ry
  h=1e-4       0.0212059356726836 Ry
  h=3e-5       0.0212059356539224 Ry

The final discrepancy is 4.4e-12 Ry in the derivative. This verifies the
spectral-projector derivative for a real material and a local potential
direction, within the stored-band approximation.

Important limits
================
This is NOT a finite difference of the full trace-log/free-energy functional.
The endpoint projector differences are contracted with the central exact
functional gradients. A full tangent-functional potential probe is still
needed to establish all cancellations independently in this material.

The ordinary residual integral (n_G-n)delta V was not evaluated here. Nor
were radial-basis changes included. Thus the number above is the projector
part of a proposed stationarity residual, not a fully measured total slope.
It is not a stress and must not be converted to GPa without the actual
strain-induced potential derivative and a verified separation from explicit
geometric terms. The numerical pressure discrepancy is still unresolved.

The next decisive fixed-geometry test compares two functional paths:
  A. Hold the physical embedded projector operator fixed while varying V.
  B. Rebuild Pi_W[V] and X[V] while varying the same V.
Use the frozen-Sigma tangent impurity functional and a common fixed basis.
At ordinary charge convergence A should be flat to integration/convergence
errors. The slope of B minus A should equal the projector term above.
Repeat perturbation amplitudes; use a uniform-potential gauge control and a
larger-window control. This costs non-self-consistent evaluations, not an
impurity or charge-convergence calculation.

Density-to-potential clarification
==================================
Once a trial density variation is prescribed, its Hxc potential variation
requires a Poisson solve and XC evaluation, not a self-consistent Sternheimer
solve. In a fixed spatial representation delta V_Hxc=K_Hxc delta n; explicit
ionic and metric/geometry changes belong to their separately accounted terms.
The resulting potential then determines radial LAPW functions and KS
eigenvector changes. Self-consistency is needed only to determine the density
variation itself from the electronic output rather than prescribe it.

The affine density ansatz n_eta(r)=(1+eta)^(-3)n0(r/(1+eta)) gives
dn/deta=-3n0-r.grad(n0). This is linear in eta, but omitted electronic
relaxation also enters at first order. It is an inexpensive trial direction,
not the complete first-order response. In a genuinely stationary functional
all implicit variational-density/Green-function terms cancel; a persistent
projector-dependent potential residual would signal stationarity equations
inconsistent with that composite finite-window functional, not necessarily
insufficient iterations of the ordinary charge loop.
