Stress comparison baseline — 7 November 2026

All stresses below are mean normal stress in GPa: trace(sigma)/3.
Numerical mean = [F(+epsilon)-F(-epsilon)]/(6*epsilon*Omega0), epsilon=0.001.
Difference = analytic minus numerical. Do not divide differences by three again.
Conventional compressive pressure has the opposite sign.
For eDMFT, F denotes the frozen-self-energy Klein-functional comparison.
