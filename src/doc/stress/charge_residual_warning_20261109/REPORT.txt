CENTRAL CHARGE RESIDUAL: WARNING, NOT ABORT — 9 November 2026

User-requested policy
A finite central charge-map residual above the default 0.001 threshold now
issues a warning and continues ordinary STRT/STRFT stress evaluation. There
is no new input switch and no need to increase the threshold to 0.01.
The existing --fixed-point-tolerance option now sets the warning threshold.

The residual is the unweighted Euclidean norm of the normalized output charge
minus the input charge in stored radial/PW coordinates. It is not WIEN2k DIS,
not a particle-number error, and not a universal stress-error estimate. The
report retains the residual, threshold, within_threshold boolean and warning.
The readable journal distinguishes response-solver convergence from central
charge convergence. The warning is emitted only on MPI rank zero and appears
both during the calculation and in the final case.outputdmf2_response.

This changes the reporting policy only. No density, self-energy, normalization,
response operator, tensor, or convergence tolerance is numerically modified.
Non-finite or negative residuals, invalid thresholds, inconsistent snapshots,
failed native reconstruction and failed response solves remain errors.
