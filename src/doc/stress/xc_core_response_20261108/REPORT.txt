WHY THESE OPERATIONS ARE NEEDED

Let n be the total input density at fixed geometry. Define:
  K_H  = derivative of the native Hartree potential with respect to n;
  K_xc = derivative of the native exchange-correlation potential;
  K    = K_H + K_xc;
  B    = potential -> joint band perturbation (delta H, delta O, delta Phi);
  E    = joint band perturbation -> DMFT density-matrix response at fixed Sigma;
  R    = density-matrix response -> spatial valence density, fixed basis;
  D    = direct spatial-density response of the changing radial/basis functions;
  C    = potential -> occupied core-density response;
  N    = derivative of the chosen output-charge normalization, still explicit.

Phi here is the RAW orbital overlap. E already includes the window, overlap
symmetrization, inverse-square-root normalization and moving overlap metric.
Do not add a second normalization or second spin/k-point weight to R or C.

The unmixed physical charge-map derivative will be
  J = N [ R E B + D + C ] K.

The new composition tests evaluate [R E B + D + C] K before the N operation,
at the first k point. Fixed-N below means the electronic band particle count
is fixed by mu; it does not silently implement the separate spatial/core
normalization N. These distinctions matter for the final screened solve.

XC: DIFFERENTIATE THE IMPLEMENTED DISCRETE MAP

Native XC is not just a scalar derivative dv_xc/dn. For PBE write its local
potential as a function
  v_xc = f(n, g, L, t),
  g = |grad n|,  L = Laplacian n,  t = grad n . grad|grad n|.
The output is in Ry; n is total spin-summed density in electrons/Bohr^3.
The routine unpolarized_xc implements the built-in nonmagnetic LDA/PBE
formulas and their native low-density branches. It is not a generic LibXC
adapter. Custom functional parameters, spin response, meta-GGA, SOC and other
functional switches are outside its present scope.

For local fields z obtained from the radial/angular or Cartesian stencil,
  delta v_xc(x) = sum_j a_j(x) delta z_j(x),
  a_j(x) = partial f / partial z_j, evaluated at the central fields.

These partial derivatives are prepared once with complex-step differentiation
of analytic formulas on the central active branch. This avoids subtracting
nearby potentials. There are no per-density-coordinate probes, dense global
Jacobian construction, repeated lapw0 launches or finite-difference SCF loops
inside a response action. The active branches are held fixed; a true threshold
crossing is not a differentiable point and should be checked with endpoint
branch metadata when broadening supported material scope.

Muffin tins
-----------
CLMSUM stores c_lm(r)=r^2*n_lm(r), with a special spherical convention:
  n(r,Omega) = c_00(r)/(4*pi*r^2)
              + sum_(lm != 00) c_lm(r) Y_lm(Omega)/r^2.
Y_lm are the signed native real harmonics. Their actual native angular tables,
polar derivatives and quadrature weights are explicit preparation inputs.
The exact signed (l,m) order is checked in the periodic assembly.

DFUNDR/DERGL differentiate c_lm/r^2 with native SPLINE endpoint conditions,
parity extension through r=0, the first-three-point first-derivative
replacement, and the first-five-point second-derivative replacement. These
are cached tridiagonal solves, with their exact reverse operations.

GRANS then forms n, g, L and t in the local spherical frame, including its
polar branch. The ten local fields are
  (n, n_r, n_rr, n_theta, n_thetatheta, n_phi, n_phiphi,
   n_rtheta, n_rphi, n_thetaphi).
For example,
  g^2 = n_r^2 + n_theta^2/r^2 + n_phi^2/(r^2 sin(theta)^2),
  L = n_rr + 2*n_r/r
      + [n_thetatheta + cot(theta)*n_theta
         + n_phiphi/sin(theta)^2]/r^2.

If Y_aj is harmonic j at quadrature point a, with weight w_a, define
  W_aj = w_a Y_aj,
  P_aj = W_aj - W_a0 * (sum_b W_bj)/(sum_b W_b0)
         + delta_j0 W_a0.
This is the native angular-mean subtraction/projection. The new map evaluates
  delta V_j(r) = sum_a P_aj delta v_xc(r,Omega_a).
The spherical output is r*delta V_00/sqrt(4*pi), matching vsp; nonspherical
outputs are delta V_lm, matching vns. No endpoint zero of a radial projector
or density is assumed in these potential-response operations.

Interstitial
------------
Native PWXAD4/5 uses a quadratic least-squares fit over 27 neighbouring FFT
grid points, not a reciprocal-space spectral derivative. With offsets u_a,
use the monomials
  T(u) = (1,x,y,z,x^2,y^2,z^2,yz,zx,xy).
The fitted coefficients are
  b = (T T^T)^(-1) T [n_+(x+u_a)-n_+(x)],
where n_+=max(n,0). The gradient is (b_x,b_y,b_z); the Hessian diagonal is
2*(b_xx,b_yy,b_zz), with the mixed coefficients unchanged. The native tiny-
gradient thresholds for the total density and half-density spin channels are
kept separately. Preparation contracts the local Jacobian with this fit, so
repeated PBE actions use a 27-point variable-coefficient stencil.

Native low-density averaging is also differentiated. If the averaging set S
is nonempty, v_xc at every low-density point is replaced by the mean over S.
The reverse sends the sum of covectors from replaced points uniformly to S;
it is not the same operation as the forward mean. The positivity and averaging
sets are held at their central values. Default thresholds are explicit inputs.

Fourier order is essential:
  density star sums -> SETFFT expansion -> real-space density -> local XC
  -> low-density average -> inverse FFT -> GETFF1 representative selection
  -> truncated star expansion -> native interstitial mask -> GETFFT average.

GETFF1 takes the representative Fourier coefficient times the star size;
GETFFT after masking averages members. These two operations are different.
The new transpose reverses this exact sequence, including all FFT factors,
phases, star sizes, real-field projections and masking. INTERNAL REAN4 output
is conjugated at the native-potential file-coordinate adapter: lapw0 writes
conjg(cvalue) to a complex VNS file. The periodic Hartree
adapter supplies the shared validated geometry and coordinate conventions.

CORE: IMPLICITLY DIFFERENTIATE RADIAL MATCHING

Native lcore uses a central spherical potential, fixed shell occupations and
a fixed case.inc shift. The input vsp is r*V in Ry; core propagation uses
Hartree units:
  V_H(r) = [vsp(r) - shift_Ry*r]/(2*r).
The exterior a/r+b/r^2 continuation and its central monotonic-clamp branch
are differentiated too. The number of native exterior radial points is kept.

For a point nucleus, let kappa be the Dirac angular quantum number, Z nuclear
charge and c the speed of light (137.0359895 in RELA; 1e30 in NREL). The code
propagates (P,c Q), which avoids NREL underflow in the small component.
It differentiates the origin series, five-point predictor/corrector, exterior
asymptotic start and inward/outward match at the stored central matching point.
The matching and outer-end indices are fixed discrete branch metadata.

Define the matching residual
  F(E,V) = (c Q_out/P_out) - (c Q_in/P_in).
At a converged central eigenvalue, F=0. Therefore
  delta E = - F_V[delta V] / F_E.
The origin includes its dependence on V(0)-E; the outer asymptotic start
includes E. The energy response is not frozen. A nonconverged central match
or singular F_E is rejected.

After matching amplitudes, define
  q(r) = P(r)^2 + Q(r)^2,
  I = sum_i w_i q(r_i),
  c_core(r) = occupation*q(r)/I.
The w_i are the native CHARGE quadrature weights on the extended radial domain.
Then
  delta c_core(r) = occupation/I * [delta q(r) - q(r)*delta I/I].
This includes both large and small components and exact normalization.
Only the MT part is returned in the CLMCOR/CLMSUM spherical convention.
An individual state's full-domain integrated derivative vanishes, but its
inside-RMT charge may change through leakage. Exterior core treatment and
output normalization must therefore remain explicit in the full charge map.

All core propagation matrices, local forcings and the eigenvalue covector are
cached. One forward/reverse pass scales linearly with the radial grid. There
is no repeated root search in a core response call. Atoms without core must
be explicitly declared; missing atoms never mean a silently frozen core.

ADJOINT CONVENTION

The operator interface uses real Euclidean coordinate vectors. Complex Fourier
coefficients are packed as explicit real/imaginary coordinates; radial units,
star factors, occupations and integration weights live inside each operation.
Each operation supplies a genuine reverse action satisfying
  y . A(x) = A^T(y) . x.
A continuum symmetry assumption about the XC kernel is not substituted for
this discrete transpose. That distinction matters with native interpolation,
angular mean subtraction, cutoffs and representative selection.

TESTS AND LIMITS

The complete selected response suite passes 145 tests with zero skips: 124
existing tests plus 20 new XC/core tests and one complex-VNS regression. Native radial/Hartree reference
libraries were enabled. New tests include exact Coulomb densities in NREL/RELA,
core normalization, localized-state gauge response, Coulomb scaling, exterior
potential response, spline polynomial reproduction, forward/adjoint equality,
uniform-density PBE->LDA, independent MT/IR finite differences and low-density
averaging reversal.

Independent native material controls use FeSe RELA/PBE and SrVO3 NREL/LDA,
RKMAX=8, primitive orthogonal nonmagnetic no-SOC fixtures. Each native charge
endpoint uses newly computed potentials, KS states and normalized projectors;
self-energy, local frames, RMT, radial energies and band-window indices are
fixed. Only the first k point is used in the valence controls; 66/43 retained
states plus the full finite-LAPW complement are used in the response.

At fixed N, endpoint mu is obtained from an independent nonlinear N(mu) root
and native dmft2 is rerun at that value. The analytic tangent is not used to
choose endpoint mu. Root residuals are at most 2.14e-14 electrons.

These perturbation amplitudes multiply a density direction. They are NOT
physical strains, so none of the numbers below are GPa or new pressures.

NEW BUG FOUND BY THE COMPOSED TEST

The internal Hartree and XC component checks originally compared native
REAN4 arrays. The previous native-potential adapter then incorrectly copied
the imaginary part to NativePotentialBands without changing its sign.
Native LAPW0 explicitly writes CONJG(cvalue) to complex VNS files. This last
conversion was missing in the new adapter, for both Hartree and XC response.
A real FeSe potential cannot expose that error.

In SrVO3, before this correction:
  final interstitial file-potential derivative error: 1.18e-1;
  band H derivative error:                           1.75e-4;
  fixed-N charge derivative error at h=.0005:         7.12e-4.
After conjugating at the file-coordinate boundary exactly once:
  final interstitial file-potential derivative error: 2.81e-8;
  band H derivative error:                           2.76e-8;
  fixed-N charge derivative error at h=.0005:         4.95e-7.

The inverse operation in the transpose includes the same imaginary sign.
A sine-wave test independently verifies that internal Fourier expansion and
native-file reconstruction describe the same real potential. Merely checking
an operation against its own transpose would not catch this physical error.
The issue was in the new prepared response adapter, not a newly discovered
bug in WIEN2k's VNS writer or in the ordinary analytic dmft2 stress executable.

A separate diagnostic reconstructing all l<=10 basis channels instead of
native dmft2's l<=8 spatial density was also tested. It produces a different
spatial map, already identified in the prior charge-consistency checkpoint.
The native comparison retains l<=8, rather than silently changing that map.
