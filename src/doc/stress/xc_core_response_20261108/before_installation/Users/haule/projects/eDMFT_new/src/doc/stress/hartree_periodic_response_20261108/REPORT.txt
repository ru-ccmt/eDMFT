1. FIXED-GEOMETRY LINEAR MAP AND CONVENTIONS

Let c_aL(r) be a density perturbation in an inequivalent atom a. L=(l,m)
uses native real harmonics: a negative stored l denotes a sine harmonic.
The physical angular momentum in powers/Bessel functions is abs(l).

For L=00, c_00=4*pi*r^2*delta n_spherical (CLMSUM convention).
For all other L, c_L=r^2*delta n_L.
rho_j is the density Fourier STAR SUM for star representative G_j, not its
coefficient per member. g_j=|G_j| in Bohr^-1; G_j integer labels acquire the
reciprocal matrix, including 2*pi. R_a is the fixed muffin-tin radius.
Omega is the fixed unit-cell volume in Bohr^3. Potentials are in Ry.

The radial moments before conversion to complex harmonics are

  m_aL = integral dr r^l c_aL(r) / z_L,                    (1)
  z_00=sqrt(4*pi), z_L=1 otherwise.

The integral in (1) is native CHARGE, not CoulInt or SciPy cubic integration.
On n>=8 logarithmic points, its weights are r_i*delta(log r) times

  [17/48, 59/48, 43/48, 49/48, 1, ..., 1,
   49/48, 43/48, 59/48, 17/48].

Native default short-grid rules are also implemented. The default branch adds
no separate origin term. The internal radial Dirichlet solution continues to
use the previously verified native CoulInt/Spline algorithm. Their quadratures
must not be conflated even though both appear within the Hartree calculation.

S converts real harmonic moments to complex harmonic moments. For m>0:

  cosine: S_(+m)=(-1)^m/sqrt(2), S_(-m)=1/sqrt(2),
  sine:   S_(+m)=-i*(-1)^m/sqrt(2), S_(-m)=i/sqrt(2).

For m=0 the coefficient is one. Different real harmonics can contribute to
the same complex harmonic; these contributions are added, not overwritten.

Define the native star-averaged spherical harmonic

  Ybar_a,lm,j = (1/N_j) sum_(G in star j)
      tau_G exp(i G.d_a) Y_lm*(Rloc_a G),                  (2)

where N_j is the number of DISTINCT star members, tau_G is the mean native
translation phase for repeated symmetry operations producing the same G,
and d_a is the atom position. R_a elsewhere is the scalar sphere radius.
Local rotations
follow LAPW0 SymmRot, including its repair of rounded structure-file entries.

The Fourier-continuation sphere moments are (D rho)_a,lm, with

  D_a,lm,j = 4*pi*R_a^(l+2)*i^l Ybar_a,lm,j
             * j_(l+1)(g_j R_a)/g_j,      j != 0,         (3)
  D_a,00,0 = 4*pi*R_a^3/[3*sqrt(4*pi)],
  D_a,lm,0 = 0 for l>0.

Here j_l(x) denotes a spherical Bessel function. The replacement multipoles
in the native electron/ionic sign convention are

  q = -S m + D rho.                                      (4)

The ionic moments have zero derivative for this fixed-geometry density map.
For smoothing order n=NCON, define

  A_j,a,lm = -[8*pi/g_j^2] [4*pi/Omega] N_j Mult_a
       * (-i/R_a)^l [(2*n+3)!!/(2*l+1)!!]
       * j_(n+1)(g_j R_a)/(g_j R_a)^(n-l+1)
       * Ybar_a,lm,j*,                     j != 0.        (5)

Mult_a counts equivalent sites. The unmasked potential STAR SUM is

  p_j = (8*pi/g_j^2) rho_j + sum_(a,lm) A_j,a,lm q_a,lm,
  p_0 = 0.                                               (6)

The latter is LAPW0's Coulomb gauge. This routine does not adjust particle
number or add a separately inferred charged-cell compensation.

2. BOUNDARY POTENTIALS AND INTERSTITIAL REANALYSIS

For a real harmonic L with angular momentum l, define

  t_aL = sum_(j != 0) p_j j_l(g_j R_a) Ybar_a,lm,j.

For m=0 the boundary harmonic is Re[4*pi*i^l*t_aL].
For m>0 multiply the expression inside Re by sqrt(2)/alpha_L, where
alpha_L=(-1)^m for cosine, and -i*(-1)^m for sine. For the spherical channel,
divide by sqrt(4*pi) to obtain the PHYSICAL SCALAR boundary potential.
Collect this map as

  b = Re(B p).                                          (7)

The already tested radial Dirichlet operator gives

  v_aL = T_aL c_aL + h_aL b_aL,                          (8)

where v_00=r*delta V_spherical and h_00=r. For l>0, v_L=delta V_L
and h_L=(r/R_a)^l. T is the native CoulEM radial Green-function operation.

The interstitial output is

  v_I = M p,                                            (9)

where M reproduces REAN0/SETFFT/REAN4/GETFFT. Specifically: expand p_j/N_j
with native star phases, Fourier-transform, take the real field, multiply
by the native truncated Fourier representation of the interstitial step
function, transform back, and average the resulting star members. REAN0
uses the ENTIRE native FFT box, including frequencies absent from the density
star list. The operation preserves native finite-grid aliasing and the complex
phase convention. Output is a PER-MEMBER coefficient of theta_I*delta V,
exactly the convention expected by the interstitial Hamiltonian adapter.
Do not divide this output by star size or apply a second muffin-tin mask.

The output selector explicitly chooses/reorders the LM and G channels used
by NativePotentialBands. Its transpose returns their covectors to the full
Hartree layout. Missing requested channels are errors. A spherical component
is never silently counted again in the nonspherical part.

3. REVERSE OPERATION

All public vector coordinates use ordinary real Euclidean pairings. Complex
intermediate arrays use Re(vdot). For output covectors y_MT and y_I:

  (c_bar_direct, b_bar) = radial_Dirichlet^T y_MT,
  p_bar = B^dagger b_bar + M^T y_I,
  q_bar = A^dagger p_bar,
  m_bar = -Re(S^dagger q_bar),
  rho_bar = (8*pi/g^2) p_bar + D^dagger q_bar,
  c_bar = c_bar_direct + moment_weights^T m_bar.          (10)

The zero-frequency coefficient of the diagonal factor in (10) is zero.
The reverse of M includes the native real-field projections. Symmetry of a
continuum Coulomb kernel is not used as a substitute for this actual discrete
transpose. No density-coordinate finite-difference probes are used to build K.

4. INDEPENDENT NATIVE COMPARISONS

An isolated copy of the user's WIEN2k24 LAPW0 is instrumented to stop after
the Coulomb calculation, exporting full-precision radial potentials, unmasked
POTK, REAN0 coefficients and REAN4(POTK). This excludes XC while preserving
the original Coulomb algorithm. The scratch exporter allocates its own missing
late-stage arrays before calling reanalysis. Installed sources are untouched.

Inputs are actual FeSe and SrVO3 structures/CLMSUM files. The perturbation is
the independent fixed-N induced valence density from the preceding checkpoint.
Each material has central, +/-0.01 and +/-0.005 density-amplitude runs. These
amplitudes are NOT physical strain magnitudes. Hartree is linear at fixed
geometry; step halving probes file/arithmetic cancellation, not truncation of
a nonlinear Hartree derivative. No impurity calculation is performed.

Relative norm errors at amplitude 0.01:

                                    FeSe                  SrVO3
  unmasked potential star sums      1.52e-10              6.41e-10
  MT boundary values                5.77e-11              1.54e-9
  all radial potential entries      1.19e-10              7.14e-10
  masked interstitial coefficients  7.01e-11              9.20e-10
  complete Hartree transpose        1.68e-17              1.07e-17

At amplitude 0.005, the complete radial/PW vector errors are 2.30e-10 and
7.97e-10. Larger small-step errors are consistent with subtracting the large
central ionic/core potentials and finite text precision of the density files.
Native density/POTK small-value skip thresholds can also limit exact agreement;
this adapter evaluates the linear kernels without introducing those cutoffs.

REAN0 matches native to 1.00e-15 and 3.73e-15. Applying our REAN4 to the SAME
native POTK matches the native reanalysis to 6.51e-16 and 1.81e-15.

A first FeSe comparison showed a stable ~2.5e-8 boundary residual. The source
audit traced it to missing SymmRot repair of 0.7071068 in the staged geometry.
Adding the same native repair removed that residual. This was corrected in the
NEW staged helper; no claim is made about the earlier unrelated stress residual.

Portable tests also cover real/complex harmonic conversion including both sine
signs, nonsymmorphic star cancellation, constant-potential masking, linearity,
boundary values, output-coordinate selection, continuum quadrature convergence,
and rejection of unsupported geometry/grids. Native CHARGE is independently
compiled and compared on short and full radial meshes with smooth/random fields.

5. CONNECTION TO THE VALENCE RESPONSE AND COST

The new K_H has been composed with the existing complete first-k valence map

  J_H,val = (R E B + D_basis) K_H.                        (11)

E includes normalized-projector/window response and the finite LAPW empty
complement at fixed N. B includes all native potential sectors. The direct
moving-basis density leg D_basis is retained. Equation (11) is explicitly
Hartree-only and valence-only: XC and core response have NOT been substituted
by zero inside a purported full physical screened calculation.

The composed transpose errors are 3.13e-16 (FeSe) and 2.70e-15 (SrVO3).
These are first-k algebra/coordinate checks, not independent finite differences
of a full all-k self-consistent map. Each physical constituent was compared
to native endpoints in this or the preceding checkpoints.

Representative single-thread timings:

                                    FeSe                  SrVO3
  prepare Hartree context            ~0.16 s              ~0.16 s
  Hartree forward                    ~0.007 s             ~0.008 s
  Hartree reverse                    ~0.008 s             ~0.010 s
  first-k Hartree + valence action    0.929 s              1.967 s

Radial integration is linear in radial-grid size. Small angular/PW matrices
and the FFT step-function field are cached once; repeated actions use matrix
products and FFTs. No lapw0 subprocess is used by the new helper. These are
component timings, NOT end-to-end stress speedups or an MPI scaling result.

6. SCOPE AND NEXT DEVELOPMENT

This initial material adapter supports primitive orthogonal cells with native
uncompressed real harmonics (negative atom indices), fixed geometry/RMT/local
frames, default CoulEM/CHARGE, and explicit native FFT dimensions/NCON. It
supports inversion-real and noncentrosymmetric complex Fourier fields, as tested
by FeSe and displaced SrVO3. Compressed cubic harmonics and non-orthogonal or
centered-cell geometry are explicitly rejected. Alternative LDMTest integration
branches, moving nuclei/RMT/frames, and explicit charged-background variations
are not implemented. These restrictions must be checked by the eventual loader.

We remain in STEP 2. The next work is native XC response (including the GGA
density-gradient terms and native angular/FFT discretization), then core-density
response, then the complete one-step charge Jacobian. Step 3 is validation of
the physical screened forward/adjoint solve with that full Jacobian. Completing
the stress gradient, six strain sources and production tensor publication still
follows. Existing wrapper status remains stress_applied=false.

No new analytic-versus-numeric GPa comparison is claimed in this checkpoint.
