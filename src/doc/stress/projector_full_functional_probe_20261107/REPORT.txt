FULL TANGENT-FUNCTIONAL POTENTIAL PROBE: FIXED VERSUS REBUILT PROJECTOR
SrVO3, 7 November 2026

Result
======
The independent full trace-log endpoint calculation confirms the previously
predicted finite-window projector contribution. At h=3e-5, for a perturbation
of t Ry in the interstitial and zero in every muffin tin:

  Fixed physical embedding, tangent-functional slope   0.00000129010 Ry
  Rebuilt window and normalization, slope              0.02120707012 Ry
  Difference                                          0.02120578002 Ry
  Analytic projector prediction                        0.02120593565 Ry

The difference agrees to 1.56e-7 Ry, approximately 7.3 parts per million.
Unlike the preceding probe, the endpoint values here are full nonlinear
trace-log evaluations, not just projector differences contracted with a
central gradient. The reference density counterterm uses the computed
central lattice density, removing ordinary SCF charge mismatch by construction.

This confirms a potential-dependent finite-window functional gradient in
the specified fixed-basis, frozen-self-energy, stored-band setting. It does
not determine a pressure correction or establish the source of the entire
analytic-versus-numerical stress discrepancy.

What 'window motion' means
==========================
The window indices remain [12,31] at all endpoints. No band crosses a boundary.
The smallest inside/outside energy gap is 0.05642 Ry, safely larger than the
test amplitudes. The spectral projector is

  Pi_W(t) = sum_(n=12)^31 |psi_n(t)><psi_n(t)|.

The span of these eigenvectors can change although their indices do not.
At first order,

  delta Pi_W = sum_(n in W,a outside W)
      |a><a|delta V|n><n|/(epsilon_n-epsilon_a) + Hermitian conjugate.

Rotations within W cancel in Pi_W. Only couplings between W and its
complement enter. This is a change of subspace, not an energy-cutoff crossing
and not an arbitrary phase/gauge choice for KS eigenvectors.

The interstitial-only constant is not a uniform potential shift:

  <a|delta V|n> = t Ry integral_interstitial psi_a^* psi_n.

Orthogonality sets the integral over the entire cell to zero for a!=n;
it does not set the interstitial integral alone to zero. Thus this bounded,
local potential perturbation can mix selected and excluded states. A truly
uniform potential shift has delta V_an=t Ry delta_an and does not change Pi_W.

The physical localized orbital used in the finite-window embedding is

  Phi_W(t) = Pi_W(t) phi X(t),

with phi fixed and X the symmetry-averaged global normalization. Changing
Pi_W changes the real-space truncated orbital and generally X as well.
Holding the numerical projector matrix fixed in a changing KS basis would
NOT hold this physical embedding fixed.

Functional and two paths
========================
All calculations use the original central KS basis as one fixed orthonormal
Hilbert space of 43 stored bands at 75 irreducible k points. Geometry, radial
basis, local orbitals, linearization energies, effective self-energy and mu
are fixed. The potential has no change inside MT spheres, so it does not
require changing the spherical radial input. There is no charge iteration,
impurity solve, source change, or change of original material inputs.

Let M_k be the interstitial characteristic-function matrix. Set

  H_k(t)=diag(epsilon_k)+t M_k.
  B_0k = central normalized projector in W, zero outside W.

Path A: B_k(t)=B_0k in the original central Hilbert space. Therefore the
physical operator B_0 Sigma(iw) B_0^dagger is fixed even though H changes.

Path B: diagonalize H_k(t), form Pi_W(t), project the same full stored raw
orbital coefficients q_k, and rebuild the global normalization:

  Q_k(t)=Pi_W(t) q_k,
  S(t)=Sym sum_k w_k Q_k(t)^dagger Q_k(t),
  B_k(t)=Q_k(t) S(t)^(-1/2).

The active local space is xz,yz,xy. For the P4mm reference, the forward orbital
average replaces the xz/yz block by their mean times the identity, preserves
the xy diagonal, and removes forbidden off-diagonals. The central saved X
differs from the fresh inverse square root by only ~1e-8 in the normalized
metric. To compare exactly at the saved central state, S(t) is anchored with
a constant offset Ssaved-S(0), norm 6.51e-9. A separate completely fresh-X
endpoint check changes the reported slope by less than 1e-9 Ry.

For each path, evaluate the lattice grand trace-log F_sp(t). The frozen-Sigma
impurity tangent cancels the -Tr(Sigma g) term. The density, Hxc energy and
ionic terms are fixed in this potential-direction probe; their only
t-dependent contribution is the density-potential counterterm. Therefore

  K_test(t)-K_test(0) = F_sp(t)-F_sp(0)
                         -t sum_k Tr(rho_0k M_k).

rho_0k is exported from production dmft2 and already includes spin/k weights.
This is the full frozen-Sigma tangent-functional difference along this
fixed-density, fixed-geometry potential direction, within the stored basis.
It is not the full impurity functional away from its tangent reference.

The ordinary density slope is 6.39106216025 Ry per unit t. Path A F_sp itself
is NOT flat: it has this ordinary first-order potential energy. It is the
counterterm-subtracted K_test that should be stationary for a fixed physical
projector. Path B additionally varies the physical correlated embedding.

Independent numerical evaluation
================================
The Python evaluator uses Hermitian eigendecomposition of

  H_inf=H+B Sigma_inf B^dagger

and the determinant lemma to evaluate the dynamical trace-log through a
3-by-3 determinant at every retained Matsubara frequency:

  F_sp = -T sum_j log[1+exp(-(E_inf,j-mu)/T)]
    -2T sum_(w>0) weight_w log|det[1-g_inf,loc(iw) DeltaSigma(iw)]|
    + the production high-frequency completion.

g_inf,loc=B^dagger(iw+mu-H_inf)^(-1)B. k weights and spin degeneracy are
included once. The self-energy is the frozen sig.inp1, with its last sentinel
record defining Sigma_inf. Its three GOOD tail moments are independently
refitted from the final24 points. The 500 retained-frequency quadrature
covers2387 consecutive positive frequencies, with200 dense points and cubic
cardinal weights in inverse frequency afterwards. Generated weights match
the printed production extrema and sum. The rational-pole tail completion
uses the same C0=1 Ry convention as cmp_dmft_weights.f90.

Central scalar validation:
  Independent F_sp+mu*N = -27.9573399088952 Ry
  Production :VSUM     = -27.957339909     Ry
  Difference            = 1.05e-10 Ry, within printed precision.

Centered finite-difference slopes in Ry per unit perturbation t:

  h        K_fixed'        K_rebuilt'      rebuilt-minus-fixed
  1e-3     1.07780e-6      0.02120686609   0.02120578829
  3e-4     1.27116e-6      0.02120705205   0.02120578089
  1e-4     1.28827e-6      0.02120706842   0.02120578016

The small fixed-embedding residual has been retained, not forced to zero.
At the smallest step its value is almost exactly the derivative of the
free-energy tail completion (1.29025e-6 Ry). The ordinary production density
does not include that same derivative. Finite-step and frequency-completion
differences therefore limit exact finite-quadrature stationarity, but are
much smaller than the 0.0212 Ry projector signal. No tail source change was
made in this test. The ~1.6e-7 Ry analytic-versus-FD projector discrepancy
should likewise not be advertised as roundoff agreement.

Controls explaining the effect
==============================
1. Spatially uniform potential shift: rebuilt-minus-fixed slope is below
   6e-12 Ry. Physical projector change is below3.6e-15. Its total fixed-path
   residual includes the same finite-step/tail issues described above.

2. Remove all W-to-complement matrix elements of the interstitial perturbation:
   rebuilt-minus-fixed slope is zero at printed double precision.

3. Retain ONLY those W-to-complement matrix elements:
   rebuilt-minus-fixed slope is0.02120578017 Ry, reproducing the full local
   perturbation's first-order extra slope. Controls2/3 are algebraic band
   matrix separations, not separately asserted to be local potentials.

4. Enlarge the window to the entire retained43-band space, rebuilding the
   corresponding reference normalized orbitals: Pi_W=identity in this
   finite space. Rebuilt-minus-fixed slope is3.6e-11 Ry, numerical noise.
   This is an algebraic control with a different correlated embedding, not
   a converged material calculation and not the complete infinite Hilbert
   space. Merely adding outside bands to a response sum is different from
   enlarging the DMFT embedding window itself.

Conclusion and next unresolved question
======================================
For this probe the extra functional slope is caused by changes in the
selected spectral subspace and its normalization. It survives perfect
ordinary charge consistency in the fixed-reference construction, and its
predicted derivative is independently verified by the full trace-log test.

It is not established that the same term dominates the hydrostatic stress
error. The physical strain-induced Hxc potential derivative still has to be
measured, with explicit geometry/basis terms separated to avoid double
counting. Paired radial responses, omitted complementary bands and other
stress approximations remain separate issues. The result motivates a
consistent projector/functional treatment, not an empirical pressure offset.

Reproduction
============
The accompanying full_functional.py is a standalone NumPy/SciPy diagnostic.
Its bundled inputs, weights, central density and band-matrix evidence permit
re-evaluation without dmft2, LAPW1, an impurity solver or source changes.
Run a copied directory if preserving the original results.json is desired:

  python full_functional.py

The complete endpoint/control evaluation takes approximately4 seconds on this
laptop after the central matrices are available. Those came from the21-second
isolated dmft2 pass documented in ../projector_stationarity_probe_20261107/.
