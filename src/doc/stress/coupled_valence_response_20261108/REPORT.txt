COUPLED VALENCE RESPONSE: SPHERICAL POTENTIAL INPUT
Checkpoint of 8 November 2026

RESULT AND SCOPE

The joint spherical-potential -> H/O/raw-projector -> electronic density ->
spatial valence-density response now agrees with independent native endpoints
in FeSe RELA and SrVO3 NREL when the entire finite LAPW basis is represented.
The native radial response, both matching legs, nonspherical CENTRAL potential,
moving overlap metric, normalized projector/window, and direct basis-density
derivative are included. Forward and transpose operations agree near roundoff.

A controlled test exposes a major truncation error: restricting the response
to the usual stored band set can lose a large part of this density derivative,
even though the ground-state density is already accurate. This is a requirement
for the new response engine. It does not establish that the same omission is
the sole cause of earlier analytic-versus-numeric PRESSURE discrepancies.

These are optional response modules. They do not change ordinary dmft2 stress,
the production dmft2 executable, or the user's converged material directories.
No new corrected pressure or six-component stress tensor is claimed here.

The tested input is a spherical muffin-tin potential perturbation at fixed
geometry, RMT, local frames, local self-energy, double counting, chemical
potential, band-index window and radial linearization/LO energies. The tests
use nonmagnetic, non-SOC, primitive orthogonal cells. General nonspherical and
interstitial potential INPUTS, automatic radial-energy search, core response,
Hartree/XC response and the final stress contraction are not completed here.

1. THE OPERATION THAT WAS CONNECTED

Let v_a(r)=r delta V_a,spherical(r) in Ry, with one radial mesh for each
inequivalent atom a. All derivatives below refer to this input, not to strain.

Let H_k and O_k be the Hamiltonian and overlap matrices in the fixed CENTRAL
band-coefficient representation. Phi_k contains raw band/local-orbital overlaps.
Let rho_k be the contravariant band density matrix, already including spin and
k-point weights. Let Psi_k(r) be the row of spatial trial functions formed with
the central coefficients and the potential-dependent LAPW augmentation.

  n(r) = sum_k Psi_k(r) rho_k Psi_k(r)^dagger.

Consequently,

  delta n = R delta rho + D_s v,
  (delta H,delta O,delta Phi) = B_s v,
  delta rho = E(delta H,delta O,delta Phi),

  delta n = (R E B_s + D_s) v.                              (1)

R is spatial density reconstruction with basis fixed. D_s contains BOTH direct
basis terms delta Psi rho Psi^dagger + Psi rho delta Psi^dagger. E is the existing
cached frozen-self-energy electronic response. B_s is the newly connected
spherical-input operator. Its output contains full off-diagonal band matrices.

All maps use explicitly packed real coordinates; complex matrices use the
real Frobenius pairing Re Tr(A^dagger B). Reverse operations are transposes
in these coordinates, including the actual discrete integration weights.

The coefficient trace alone is not the particle-number derivative:

  delta N = sum_k Tr(delta rho_k + rho_k delta O_k).        (2)

The independent real-space integrated-charge check remains a completion test
for the broader potential/charge adapter. This checkpoint tests pointwise
stored spatial coefficients and the complete forward/transpose pairing.

2. SPHERICAL H AND O WITHOUT REBUILDING A LARGE PW MATRIX

For one atom/site/angular channel l, let phi_i(r) be the native primitive
radials (u, udot, ordinary LO radials). The stored four components are
(A,B,AP,BP). The native metric W_rc is obtained from the prepared radial solver;
it includes its origin treatment and small-component convention.

  S_ij = sum_rc phi_rci W_rc phi_rcj.

The native assembler imposes S_00=1 and S_01=S_10=0, so their derivatives must
also be set to zero. These are discrete implementation identities, not an
invitation to replace the remaining metric with a continuum approximation.

Define T = diag(E,E,E_LO,...) + e_0 e_1^T, with all energies in Ry. If Vphys
is the physical spherical potential and Vbasis generates the radial functions,

  K = (S T + T^T S)/2
      + phi^T W (Vphys-Vbasis) phi.                        (3)

Z_min contains matched primitive coefficients for harmonic m and central band
n, including LOs. Zpw uses only the PW contribution. At radius R=RMT define

  p_mn = sum_i A_i(R) Zpw_min / R,
  d_mn = sum_i AP_i(R) Z_min / R.

Native LO boundary VALUES are zero in this assembler; their boundary SLOPES
are retained. The spherical contributions are

  O = sum_m Z_m^dagger S Z_m,
  H = sum_m Z_m^dagger K Z_m
      + R^2/2 (p^dagger d + d^dagger p) + H_ordered.        (4)

Both matching legs, S, K, p and d are differentiated. For the present physical
spherical perturbation Vphys and Vbasis move together, so the difference in
(3) and its tangent are zero. A separate frozen-physical-H control instead
sets delta[r(Vphys-Vbasis)]=-v. These tests must not be confused.

The native upper-triangle-copy convention needs a small ordered correction.
For LAPW matching define j_i=j_l(q_i R), dj_i=q_i j_l'(q_i R), and

  M1_i = R(A_udot dj_i - AP_udot j_i),
  M2_i = R(AP_u j_i - A_u dj_i),
  Wb   = AP_u A_udot - A_u AP_udot.

The ordered native PW term is (Wb-1)(M1_i M2_j-M2_i M1_j)/2. Since

  M1_i M2_j-M2_i M1_j = R^2 Wb(dj_i j_j-j_i dj_j),

prepare ONCE

  Tord = Cpw^dagger F[angular_ij(dj_i j_j-j_i dj_j)] Cpw,
  angular_ij = 4 pi/volume (2l+1) P_l(qhat_i.qhat_j)
               times conjugate(site_phase_i)*site_phase_j.               (5)

Cpw is the central PW coefficient matrix. F keeps the native upper triangle,
copies its conjugate to the lower triangle and makes the diagonal real.
It is not the symmetric average of the original ordered matrix.

  H_ordered = R^2/2 Wb(Wb-1) Tord,
  delta H_ordered = R^2/2 (2Wb-1) delta Wb Tord.            (6)

For APW, M2=0 and this ordered term vanishes. Formula (6) allows subsequent
actions and transposes in small primitive/band spaces without a new Npw^2
matrix. The global spherical frame is used; rounded local axes are not needed
for an isotropic kernel.

3. NONSPHERICAL CENTRAL POTENTIAL AND PROJECTOR

The nonspherical part retains all ordered l,l' radial pairs and native Gaunt
coefficients. For each harmonic h,

  I_ijh^(l,l') = sum_rc phi_l,rci W_rc V_h(r) phi_l',rcj.  (7)

Here V_h is the actual potential in Ry, not r V_h. Both radial legs and both
matching legs in Z^dagger K_ns Z are differentiated. The generic class also
accepts delta V_h. The current joint spherical-input adapter holds V_h fixed,
while retaining its radial/matching response. Omitting this distinction would
drop a real contribution to the spherical-input Hamiltonian derivative.

For projector 5, let P_a(r) be the fixed radial projector stored on disk,
Wp the NREL/RELA native projector metric, and L_ma the orbital transformation:

  q_i = sum_rc Wp_rc phi_rci P_rc,
  Phi_na = sum_mi conjugate(Z_min) q_i L_ma + Phi_tail,na,

  delta Phi_na = sum_mi [conjugate(delta Z_min) q_i
                         + conjugate(Z_min) delta q_i] L_ma.             (8)

Only the two radial value components enter q. Native NREL projector integration
sets its small-component weight to zero. For one non-SOC block L=CF^T, following
the native final conjugation; using CF^dagger here would give the wrong gauge.

At fixed geometry and fixed central coefficients, a supplied interstitial
projector tail is constant for this potential response. This is NOT a formula
for its geometric strain or moving-boundary derivative. The material fixtures
have no nonzero radial interval outside RMT; their actual tails are zero.

The actual local/equivalent-site frame and Bloch phase are essential. A
symmetry mapping R pos_first+tau to an equivalent site supplies the unwrapped
position R pos_first+tau, including its lattice translation. The raw-projector
phase uses G+k. A scalar k-dependent site phase may cancel from a density or
Hamiltonian matrix but cannot be discarded from a raw projector comparison.
Using these native conventions reproduces the exported raw projectors to
2--5e-15 without fitting, gauge alignment or a pseudoinverse.

E applies the normalized-projector chain ONCE after (8):

  P_k = Pi_W,k Phi_k X,
  X = [Sym(sum_k w_k Phi_k^dagger Pi_W,k Phi_k)]^(-1/2),

  delta P_k = delta Pi_W,k Phi_k X
              + Pi_W,k delta Phi_k X + Pi_W,k Phi_k delta X.             (9)

Pi_W is the fixed-index spectral subspace, not a fixed set of real-space
functions. Metric orthonormalization is included in E before this chain.
Band storage truncation is separate from the chosen DMFT window W.

4. INDEPENDENT NATIVE TEST AND THE MISSING COMPLEMENT

Native endpoints run lapw1 and dmft2_response in scratch, with fresh SOlapm.dat,
fixed Sigma/mu, identical frozen radial-energy inputs and identical PW labels.
The spherical perturbation is delta(rV)=0.07*r*sin(1.3*r), multiplied by h.
Reported errors use ||analytic-FD||_2/||FD||_2 on the packed spatial-density
coefficients. This is a coordinate norm, not a pressure or a maximum physical
density error. No fitting to endpoint derivatives is used.

The strongest convergence control uses exactly the same ONE-k-point central
calculation and native endpoints, truncating only the analytic response space:

                            Usual stored set       200 states      Full basis
  FeSe RELA (509 basis)      0.544603 (66 states)   0.176345        1.5358e-7
  SrVO3 NREL (703 basis)     0.190156 (43 states)   0.030142        7.2177e-7

This table uses h=0.0005. It must not be compared as a k-convergence table with
the separately archived three-k probes. The three-k probes give errors 0.5707
and 0.4314 for the usual FeSe/SrVO3 storage; their 200-state errors are 0.1834
and 0.06873. The comparison within each set is consistent.

Full-basis step convergence:

  h                 FeSe RELA                  SrVO3 NREL
  0.001             4.2643e-7                  2.8691e-6
  0.0005            1.5358e-7                  7.2177e-7
  0.00025           1.1936e-7                  1.7755e-7

SrVO3 follows quadratic central-difference convergence; FeSe approaches a
small numerical floor. The full-basis central density matches native output
to 9.5e-13 (FeSe) and 1.63e-12 (SrVO3). Coupled transpose errors are 1.43e-15
and 4.26e-16, respectively. This checks one smooth physical potential direction,
not every possible perturbation or all k points.

Why the truncation matters: even an empty high-energy state |a> contributes
to motion of a low-energy state |n> through

  <a|delta n> = <a|(delta H-epsilon_n delta O)|n>
                /(epsilon_n-epsilon_a), a != n.            (10)

This off-diagonal mixing changes the density and the moving window. Negligible
occupation of |a> does not imply a negligible sum of all such contributions.
An accurate central density therefore does not certify an accurate response.
For DMFT the full off-diagonal electronic density and normalized-projector
chain still have to be retained; (10) alone is not a replacement for E.

SrVO3 has an additional diagnostic bookkeeping detail: lapw1 writes 703 states,
but dmft2's reader/export ceiling of 100 Ry leaves only 701. The last two actual
vector states lie above 100 Ry. The full-basis fixture adds their actual energies,
coefficients and analytically assembled raw overlaps, all outside the DMFT
window and thermally empty. The central density is checked unchanged. It does
not extrapolate eigenstates or modify the production reader. Initial 703/701
dimension failure was a fixture failure, not a physical discrepancy.

The full-array normalization offset in SrVO3 includes an inactive block. The
active correlated blocks agree to roundoff; the inactive offset is not evidence
of a stale SOlapm in these tests.

Separate small-band H'/O' checks agree with native endpoints at about 1e-8--1e-7.
In the complete 703-state SrVO3 space, H' differs by roughly 1e-5 in full-matrix
norm, concentrated in this harder high-energy comparison; O' is about 1e-6.
Thus we do not claim machine-precision agreement of every high-energy matrix
entry. The coupled density itself passes the step-convergent test above.

5. SOFTWARE, COST, AND VALIDATION

New optional helpers:
  lapw_spherical_response.py          cached spherical H/O and ordered term
  lapw_nonspherical_response.py       radial/matching/nonspherical H map
  lapw_projector_response.py          raw projector-5 map in explicit gauge
  lapw_spherical_potential_response.py shared B_s action and transpose

The in-memory radial C API is version 2. It exports its actual prepared radial
mesh and overlap/projector metrics. The Python wrapper and shared library must
be installed together. Existing radial actions are unchanged. Contexts are
shared per atom/l; B_s accumulates H/O/projector covectors from all k/sites
before one radial reverse call per channel. D_s separately pools its density
covectors. Fusing their remaining repeated radial action/reverse calls is a
small future optimization, not already an implemented speedup. Registry calls
are serialized per process.

The ordered PW tensor is prepared once. Actions use small radial kernels and
cached matching coefficients; no new LAPW diagonalization is performed inside
an action. However, using a full spectral representation has preparation and
memory costs that are not yet a production solution to complement response.

The isolated spherical all-channel action took about 0.11 s in FeSe and 0.07 s
in SrVO3 in the recorded small-band first-k probes. The larger full-basis setup
and first forward density action took about 10 s and 23 s. These are component
measurements, NOT end-to-end screened-stress speedups. Transpose costs and
production MPI scaling still need profiling once the complete operator is wired.

A clean native library build and 96 source Python tests pass, including four
new independent spherical/projector/nonspherical derivative and transpose
controls. The spherical value test compares the compact formula against explicit
PW/LO matrix assembly with a deliberately non-unit Wronskian. Tests cover complex
off-diagonal covectors, LAPW/APW and both radial legs. Native NREL/RELA radial
controls remain enabled. Installed-source/helper results are in INSTALLATION.json.

6. NEXT IMPLEMENTATION STEPS

(a) Keep the full-basis result as an independent reference. Do not silently use
    the truncated response in a converged screened solve. Add a response-space
    convergence criterion; ordinary ground-state or projector-window convergence
    alone is inadequate. Do not enlarge the DMFT window just to add response
    states: these are different controls.

(b) Implement the missing complement economically. At fixed geometry prepare
    the native central H/O once. Compare a complete spectral baseline with a
    projected linear solve in the coefficient-space complement. Reuse central
    factorizations/preconditioners for forward and adjoint actions. Preserve
    generalized-metric, off-diagonal DMFT, window and normalization terms; a
    diagonal DFT occupied/unoccupied formula is insufficient. Select the faster
    accurate route by measurements, not by assuming full diagonalization cheap.

(c) Extend B_s to arbitrary nonspherical and interstitial potential inputs;
    complete spatial charge/gauge tests and fixed-N material endpoint tests.
    Turn the limited material fixture into a validated reusable adapter with
    explicit supported geometry, local frames, energy policy and failure checks.

(d) Add native Hartree/XC and core forward/reverse operations. Connect the full
    unmixed charge-map derivative J and validate it against the native reference.
    Only then use (I-J) delta n=b or its adjoint as the fast screening engine.

(e) Complete the common stress gradient, six physical strain sources, and
    direct corrections. Validate forward versus adjoint contractions and the
    matched frozen-Sigma functional finite differences before publishing a
    corrected tensor through the existing final-only dmft2 wrapper.
