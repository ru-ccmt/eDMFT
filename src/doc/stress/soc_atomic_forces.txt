Spin–orbit contributions to atomic forces in second-variational LAPW
==================================================================

Central analytic spinor forces (November 2026)
--------------------------------------------

FORT on a nonmagnetic second-variational spinor calculation with normalized
projector 5 and fixed DMFT band indices evaluates the frozen-self-energy
Klein derivative from one geometry. Put FORT on the first line of the active
case.in2 or case.in2c and run x_dmft.py dmft2. In run_dmft.py the screened
measurement is deferred to the final charge pass. A case.inso, lapw1.def,
lapwso.def and both physical spinor components must describe the parent run.
The supported scalar solver is dftKS. The spinor projector uses one
unshifted correlated shell per impurity block with fixed local axes and
a radial projector confined to the muffin tin. Magnetic KS potentials, relativistic
p1/2 local orbitals and an LAPWSO orbital potential require extra operators.
An explicit solver.backend of native_soc_forward selects the independently
displaced reference used for validation.

The wrapper prepares one private case.response_runs/<run>/central. The native
programs export scalar H, O, the two spin components, site/spin rotations,
radial SOC integrals, projector and Matsubara metadata, and potential/core
data. The original vectors supply the native k-point ranks and a digest of
their ordered G labels. This matters for an energy cutoff whose band count
varies with k. The driver checks the normalized projector and the spinor
valence density against the native exports. case.outputdmf2_response contains
the component forces and the GMRES residual history. total_force/report.json
contains the fixed-charge force, the induced force, and the complete force
to be published by merge_scf.py or run_dmft.py before mixer.

For an atomic displacement u and fixed input charge coefficients n, define

   a = partial_u F,      s = partial_u M,      c = partial_n F,      J = partial_n M,

where M is one unmixed charge-map iteration at fixed impurity self-energy.
The dimensionless electronic density operator, occupied core levels, native
density integral and normalized projector use the same central charge. With
Ry energy, velocity in Bohr per unit displacement, and m equivalent atoms,

   (I - J^T) lambda = c,    force(u) = -1000 (a + lambda^T s) / m   [mRy/Bohr].

One adjoint serves every requested symmetry-compatible direction. A force at
fixed charge coefficients is already a whole derivative. The report adds
the induced force to that whole derivative and replaces the total on selected
sites at SCF assembly. Values for other sites come from the ordinary force.
The default relative GMRES tolerance is 1.e-8. Because c contains large
canceling contributions, a relative residual of 1.e-3 can give a poor force.

The finite scalar and spinor spaces
----------------------------------

A contains the retained scalar LAPW eigenvectors, O is the LAPW metric, and
e is the diagonal matrix of scalar energies. In the parallel-transport gauge

   Z_ai = [A_a^H (dH0 - e_i dO) A_i] / (e_i - e_a),
   dA   = A_out Z - A (A^H dO A)/2,
   de_mat = A^H dH0 A - {e,A^H dO A}/2 .

Indices i are retained and a are outside the scalar window. The complement
is diagonalized in the native LAPW metric. A denominator at or below 1.e-10 Ry
is omitted; the report counts such pairs. Next differentiate the complete
second-variational matrix diag(de_mat,de_mat) + d(B^H R_SO B). The native
spin angular matrices multiply all four (down,up) blocks. Radial changes
enter both R_SO and the matching matrix B, including APW/LO coefficients,
the LAPWSO fourth-order radial integration and the dftKS energy search.
The native LAPW energy stencil for the radial energy derivative is evaluated
at one atomic position. Its geometry derivative propagates analytically through
each radial solve at its own energy. The central XC response caches local
LDA/PBE partial derivatives computed by complex-step differentiation.
Neither operation requires geometries at displaced atomic positions.
The Bloch phases translate each atom, with radial integration limits fixed
in its own frame. A separate MT surface term for that translation would
duplicate the two moving matching legs.

For one (atom,l), let g label a point on the logarithmic radial mesh and
i,j the packed LAPWSO primitive radial functions. Use the native Rydberg
potential V_g (the spherical potential file contains r_g V_g), energies E_i,
large radial components P_gi, C0=2c, and D the five-point radial derivative
used by VDERIV. Put

    L_gi = C0 + (E_i - V_g)/C0,      Y_gi = P_gi/L_gi,
    R_ij = sum_g w_g (D V)_g Y_gi Y_gj .

The weights w_g include the native CALI prefactor and endpoint rule. At
fixed R_MT an atomic displacement preserves this radial grid. Consequently

    dY_gi = [dP_gi - Y_gi(dE_i-dV_g)/C0]/L_gi ,
    dR_ij = sum_g w_g [(D dV)_g Y_gi Y_gj
                          + (D V)_g(dY_gi Y_gj + Y_gi dY_gj)] .

The regular radial solution and its energy derivative have the same E_i.
A genuine local orbital carries its own energy. The native radial tangent
also gives the boundary data for dB. The explicit potential response is
evaluated at fixed density coefficients for the atomic source. The adjoint
contracts the change induced by the density. The two potential responses
can give large, opposing radial forces in a screened calculation.

If C contains the retained second-variational eigenvectors, the spinor
derivative is dpsi_s = dA C_s + A dC_s. The spinor complement determines
dC with the same cross-window formula, now in an orthonormal scalar-band
basis. Rotations internal to a degenerate retained block are a gauge.
Projecting the perturbed spinors and orbitals gives the raw overlap. Its
window and globally normalized inverse square root are differentiated
before the Matsubara density and energy. A stored nonmagnetic DMFT2 band
density includes 2*w_k, so the physical trace over both spinor components
uses a factor of one half. Each transpose uses the real Frobenius product.

Writing U = U_raw X, the normalization differentiated here is the same
one used in the scalar force. At fixed k weights,

    S = sum_k w_k U_raw(k)^H U_raw(k)       (rows inside the DMFT window),
    dS = sum_k w_k [dU_raw^H U_raw + U_raw^H dU_raw],
    S^(1/2) dX + dX S^(1/2) = -X dS X,      X=S^(-1/2).

The normalization is evaluated in the active native orbital frame. The
window acts in spinor band space; the earlier scalar-band cutoff belongs to
LAPWSO. Differentiating both selections is essential even for a paramagnet.

Explicit operator in ordinary FOR
--------------------------------

In the full scalar coefficient space put Q=A A^H O. The retained SOC
operator has covariant matrix Q^H W_MT Q. Differentiating in this frame
at frozen radial shape gives A^H dW_MT A and two scalar-window legs
proportional to (dQ)A=A_out Z. The ordinary scalar H/O force already
contains the retained metric connection through its energy-weighted density.
Only the external leg belongs in this operator term. The native force
contracts its angular and radial derivatives for each spin component with
the scalar Hamiltonian. A derivative of W_MT couples the two components
and has to be provided separately. Its energy covector includes the density,
the normalized DMFT-window gradient and the rotation of the retained spinors
in the raw projector. No additional radial-potential response is added to FOR.

An ordinary FOR automatically evaluates this contribution for supported
spinor calculations. A TOT run with force_last=True evaluates it on the
final private FOR. The normal DMFT2 run exports its actual density and
projector moments after the chemical-potential search. A private dftKS
pass supplies the scalar LAPW H and O. LAPWSO exports its angular and
radial integrals before spinor diagonalization. The operator contracts
these quantities once; it needs no second DMFT2, LAPW0, LCORE or charge
adjoint. The SCF merger publishes the additional FVA in site-local axes.
Set {"solver":{"soc_operator_force":false}} in case.stress_response.json
to reproduce the old ordinary force. Explicit true requires a supported
configuration. In automatic mode an unsupported spinor input retains the
native force and the wrapper prints the reason. Large per-k FOR intermediates
are temporary. The input identity, preparation record, force components and
publication report remain under case.response_runs/<run>. FORT differentiates the
whole coupled Hamiltonian including the same operator and the radial
and charge response. It replaces the selected forces.

uses 171 irreducible k points, the same scalar and spinor band ranks
at each geometry, and a frozen impurity self-energy. The energy slope
uses independently charge-relaxed endpoints at +0.016 and +0.024 Bohr.

phase      FOR     fixed charge   charge response      FORT   energy slope  FORT - slope
alpha  -1.23567       -1.93057           0.62933   -1.30124      -1.30110      -0.00014

The two middle force contributions sum to FORT. The FOR column is the

On the one-k alpha-Ce pilot, the seven allowed directions on the five
inequivalent sites use one adjoint. Rotating to all eight physical sites
gives a net force of (0, 0, 0.00000494) mRy/Bohr without imposing
the acoustic sum rule.

Checking the native local-orbital phase convention
--------------------------------------------------

For scalar phases D_ii=exp(i theta_i), matching obeys B[A D]=B[A] D.
In spinor space let D2=diag(D,D). The second-variational Hamiltonian then
transforms as D2^H H_so D2. Its eigenvalues and the free energy are invariant
under these arbitrary phases. The historical cutoff applied separately to
the real and imaginary parts of small LO coefficients violates B[A D]=B[A] D.
Consequently even a smoothly transported analytic derivative on historical
vectors can differ from a native energy slope that follows other phases.
To check the corrected force, regenerate the spinors at every geometry using
the same matching rule.

The native regression repeats LAPWSO after
randomizing the phases of the complex scalar vectors at one k point. It copies
the input into a temporary directory. From src/python run

    PYTHONPATH=.:$EDMFT_ROOT EDMFT_TEST_SOC_CASE=/path/to/complex/SOC/case \
        EDMFT_TEST_LAPWSO=$EDMFT_ROOT/lapwso EDMFT_TEST_SOC_K=86 \
        python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_native_lapwso_phase.py

The input is an unsplit, nonmagnetic dftKS/LAPWSO case with the scalar vector,
energy, potentials and lapwso.def. Choose a k index in that mesh. New native
spinors carry the :SOC_LO_MATCHING: LINEAR marker in case.scfso.

Earlier isolated measurements and the signed reference
-----------------------------------------------------

Native geometry and the two explicit matrix derivatives

Let A_i solve H_0 A_i = O A_i e_i in the scalar LAPW basis, with A_i^dagger O
A_j = delta_ij. S selects the scalar bands passed to LAPWSO. W is the sum of
the spherical spin–orbit operators over MT sites. The second-variational
Hamiltonian is diag(e_i) + W_SS, including both spin components. The eDMFT
window is a separate selection on the resulting spinors.

In coordinates attached to an atom write its projected W as B^dagger R B.
B contains the LAPW/LO matching coefficients. R contains native radial SOC
and angular integrals. At fixed scalar coefficients and radial profiles, rigid
translation gives

    dW_rigid = (dB)^dagger R B + B^dagger R dB.

In the lab frame differentiating the sphere indicator produces a surface
distribution. In the moving coordinates the integration limits are fixed and
the same translation enters through the matching legs. Adding a separate
surface term to this expression would count it twice. Changes of R due to the
radial potential and wave functions are distinct.

Even with W held fixed, the finite scalar space can rotate. Its external
component at an isolated cutoff is

    Z_ai = A_a^dagger (dH_0 - e_i dO) A_i / (e_i - e_a),
    dA_i(out) = sum_{a outside S} A_a Z_ai,       i inside S.

In a linear matching map this gives Z_s^dagger W_out,in + W_in,out Z_s, where
Z_s = diag(Z,Z) in spin. The in–out scalar H_0 block vanishes by the scalar
eigenproblem. Internal rotations are a basis choice; handle them consistently
in the density and all other matrices. The external contribution vanishes for
a complete scalar LAPW space. W may still have an explicit translation derivative.
Current LAPWSO keeps each complex local-orbital coefficient and records
:SOC_LO_MATCHING: LINEAR in case.scfso. Previous versions clipped its real
and imaginary parts independently at 1e-6. This changed spinor eigenvalues
under a pure scalar-band phase rotation and introduced discontinuities near
the threshold. Private replays use the parent's marker; an unmarked old
case.scfso selects the historical branches. Keep case.scfso with vectorso.
A legacy replay can also be requested at the LAPWSO level with
EDMFT_SOC_LEGACY_LO_FILTER=1.

Each force is minus the trace of its derivative with the central spinor
density. DFT uses normalized k weights and thermal occupations. DMFT2 stores
2*w_k times its band density and the physical nonmagnetic spinor trace uses
a factor of 1/2. Both spinor gauges are verified for off-diagonal traces.
These isolated contractions use the spinor density. The production FOR
expression above also carries the normalized DMFT projector gradient and
uses the geometry derivative of the scalar subspace in the same frame.

Measuring the two terms

For rigid motion, create an absolute control path containing 'matrix' (eDMFT)
or 'diagonal' (DFT) on line one and one Cartesian velocity vector per physical
site in STRUCT order. Set EDMFT_SOC_DISPLACEMENT to this path for a normal
LAPWSO run. For the scalar-window contribution generate the control instead:

    soc_scalar_window.py --structure Ce.struct --vector Ce.vector \
        --central central/native --plus plus/native --minus minus/native \
        --step 0.0005 --output-dir scalar_tangent

Each prefix is dftKS DFTKS_OVERLAP_DUMP output. H is in Ry and the step in
Bohr. The matrix frame, k mesh, G/LO ordering and radial policy must match.
For a clamped-charge displacement keep the input density coefficients fixed
and run LAPW0 and dftKS on each side. Alternatively pass --derivative prefix
for precomputed dH and dO in the native formats. The helper streams over k
and diagonalizes the complement in the O metric. It accepts a fresh output
directory and detects a degenerate scalar cutoff. For this run set
EDMFT_SOC_DISPLACEMENT to the absolute path of scalar_tangent/subspace.
The native diagnostic supports nonmagnetic scalar input, WFFIL and no
relativistic p1/2 LOs. Use --layout diagonal for a DFT-only derivative.

For eDMFT set EDMFT_ONEPARTICLE_DUMP to an absolute prefix (e.g. /tmp/rho) on
a native DMFT2 FOR pass using precisely the exported LAPWSO spinors. Then run

    soc_translation_force.py /path/to/control --klist Ce.klist --moments /tmp/rho

For DFT give --mu and --temperature (both Ry) instead of --moments. The result
is mRy/Bohr per displacement pattern. A force per equivalent atom requires
division by the multiplicity when the pattern moves the full orbit.

Optional finite-displacement reference for a screened force

Set FORT in the active case.in2[c] of a converged charge calculation. Prepare
unsplit lapw1.def, lapwso.def and dmft2.def. For a nonmagnetic SOC reference
include this solver in case.stress_response.json:

    {"solver":{"backend":"native_soc_forward","force_atoms":[1],
               "force_steps_Bohr":[0.006,0.003],"tolerance":0.001,
               "max_iterations":50}}

Run x_dmft.py dmft2 --execute-only, lcore, merge_scf.py and mixer as usual.
force_atoms contains inequivalent site indices. Omitting it selects all sites.
The code builds symmetry-compatible unit Cartesian displacement patterns and
divides their force by orbit multiplicity. FORT replaces the complete forces
on selected sites at SCF assembly. Other sites retain ordinary FOR. The sum
over a mixture of the two forces cannot test the screened acoustic sum rule.

For one pattern u, n_* is the fixed point of the native unmixed charge map M.
The directional charge q is computed to O(h^2) from

    q = [ M(R+h*u,n_*+h*q) - M(R-h*u,n_*-h*q) ] / (2h).

Each map pass executes LAPW0, dftKS, LAPWSO, DMFT2 and LCORE in isolation;
self-energy and double counting are fixed. The normalized projector, radial
orbitals, scalar space, both spin components, and native chemical-potential
and charge normalization policy enter each calculation. The varying energy
readout for this tangent is the native density integral (:DEN), the occupied
core eigenvalue sum, and :VSUM. Displacement-independent terms cancel.
Anderson mixing solves for q. The larger-step solution seeds the smaller
step and its residual is reevaluated. The code extrapolates in h^2. Examine
the residual histories and step dependence, especially for a small force.
The charge tolerance is not itself a bound on force error.

case.outputdmf2_response reports progress and selected forces. The run
directory contains soc_forward/native_passes.jsonl with energy components
and charge normalizations, and total_force/report.json with residuals,
explicit and induced forces, and step/extrapolation comparisons. This is
a directional numerical reference. Select backend native_soc_forward explicitly
to reproduce it. The central analytic SOC solver described above uses one shared adjoint.
The reference costs two native passes per nonzero response iteration and
direction at each step size.

Earlier Ce checks with the historical matching rule

Eight-site alpha-Ce pilot, one k. A finite rotation in the exact scalar
eigenbasis reproduces the in–out matrix contraction. Differentiating the
literal native SOC matrix (including LO clipping) gives a maximum absolute
matrix error of 1.6e-8 Ry/Bohr at step 1e-7 Bohr. Its scalar-window force is
+0.002566 mRy/Bohr with thermal DFT occupations on the pilot potential and
+0.003823 with the eDMFT density. The native rigid-force routine on a separate
three-k test matches matrix differences to 4.1e-9 Ry/Bohr.

For the selected atom the pilot's screened FORT through x_dmft.py and the
WIEN mixer gives -4.73561 mRy/Bohr. Independent nonlinear endpoint charge
convergences at +/-0.004 Bohr give -4.74038. Starting each response solve
from zero gives -4.74439. The two response solutions differ by 0.009
mRy/Bohr at the tested 0.001 relative charge tolerance. This one-k test
checks screening and publication; a phonon calculation needs its physical
mesh. The isolated explicit terms use different held-fixed quantities and
must not be added on top of FORT.

171-k physical meshes at the positive 0.020-Bohr Ce displacement (mRy/Bohr):

                         alpha eDMFT      gamma eDMFT
    rigid SOC matrix     -0.03526983      -0.04913601
    scalar-window term   +0.01314997      -0.00235459
    earlier discrepancy  -0.024986        +0.392085

The first two rows use the central spinor density at the positive displacement.
The scalar H and O tangents use +/-0.0005 Bohr about that geometry at clamped
input charge. The separate gamma k-point terms sum to 0.05255 in absolute
value before cancellation; the corresponding alpha sum is 0.05346. Their
minimum scalar in–out gaps are 0.000663 and 0.000645 Ry, respectively. On this
short interval gamma retains the same scalar band count at every k; alpha gains
or loses one at k=165 on the positive side. The derivative follows the smoothly
continued central rank and uses the full scalar H and O on both sides.

The last row comes from a different comparison, F_energy(h) - F_odd(h), where
F_odd(h) = (F(+h)-F(-h))/2 and F_energy(h) = -1000*(E(+h)+E(-h)-2E(0))/h.
Here h=0.020 Bohr and E is in Ry. The older three-geometry comparison changes
the scalar rank at three k points in alpha and five in gamma. It measures a
curvature-based force residual, not the derivative at +h at fixed rank.
The two isolated SOC terms sum to -0.02212 in alpha and -0.05149 in gamma.
The agreement of the first sum with the old alpha discrepancy does not establish
that these terms should be added to ordinary FOR. In gamma even the sign differs.
The matched-rank energy-slope tests above supersede that comparison.

A separate pilot check isolates changes in the spherical KS potential. Holding
the central scalar vector, positions, nonspherical potential and radial energy
parameters fixed, substitute the two clamped-density LAPW0 spherical potentials
from +/-0.0005 Bohr in LAPWSO and difference the native spin–orbit matrices.
The one-k eDMFT contraction gives +0.02253 mRy/Bohr. This measures the change
of the local SOC radial matrix and matching data when the radial electric field
changes. It is distinct from rotating the scalar space, and is already sampled
by the total forward reference. The quoted isolated value is for the pilot mesh.

Repeating the full-mesh H/O differences at +/-0.00025 Bohr gives a
scalar-window force of +0.01307237 mRy/Bohr in alpha and -0.00214217 in
gamma. At alpha k=165 the retained counts are (422,422,423) on the larger
step and (422,422,422) on the smaller step. A nondegenerate band can cross
a fixed energy cutoff and change the rank even when the scalar in–out gap
is finite. Such a finite jump is separate from the smooth Pulay force.
